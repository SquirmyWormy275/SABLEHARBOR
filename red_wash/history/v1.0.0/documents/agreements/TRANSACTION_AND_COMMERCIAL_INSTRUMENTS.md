## 7. Equity Purchase Agreement — signed July 2, 2025; closed July 18, 2025

### Purchased interests

Northstar transfers all equity interests of the dedicated Red Wash operating company. Red Wash remains the holder/operator of the scheduled mine, mill, tailings, equipment, inventory, contracts, permits, records, water interests, easements, and other assets to the extent legally transferable.

### Consideration and liabilities

Buyer pays $28.0 million cash consideration, with $3.0 million administered through the environmental/title escrow and $0.5 million retained as a holdback under the modeled risk-allocation mechanics. Those amounts are disclosed separately and are not added to consideration a second time. Buyer assumes only scheduled liabilities, including the $16.0 million ARO and $2.5 million other liabilities. Transaction debt and goodwill are zero.

### Representations

### Covenants

Pre-close operation follows ordinary course subject to safety and permit requirements. Seller preserves people, source records, permits, insurance, customers, inventory, and physical condition. No new related-party agreement, unusual inventory sale, permit amendment, or material capital deferral may occur outside the agreed covenant.

### Closing deliveries

Closing deliveries include equity instruments, officer resignations, disclosure schedules, customer consents, permit-transfer/acceptance evidence, title cures, bill of sale and assignments, escrow, transition-services agreement, employee census, retention agreements, inventory certificate, source-record index, bank/authority changes, and closing certificate.
