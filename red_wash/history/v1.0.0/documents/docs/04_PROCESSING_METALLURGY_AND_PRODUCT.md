# Processing, Metallurgy and Uranium Product

> **Synthetic diegetic record.** Red Wash, its parties and operating facts are fictional.

## Process description

Red Wash is a conventional uranium mill. It does not enrich uranium or fabricate fuel.

The process flow is:

1. ROM receiving, sampling and domain-controlled blending;
2. primary jaw and secondary cone crushing;
3. closed-circuit grinding;
4. sulfuric-acid leach with controlled oxidant addition;
5. solid–liquid separation and counter-current washing;
6. ion-exchange / solvent-extraction concentration;
7. uranium precipitation;
8. thickening, filtration and controlled drying;
9. product assay, drum filling, weighing, sealing and release;
10. neutralization and tailings deposition.

The 2026 nameplate is 600 tons per day. Annual throughput of 175,000 tons reflects planned downtime and rehabilitation rather than an implausible full-year nameplate run.

## Metallurgical accounting

For 2026:

- contained uranium: 595,000 lb U3O8;
- product: 547,400 lb U3O8;
- modeled recovery: 92.0%;
- unrecovered uranium to tailings and process loss: 47,600 lb.

Recovery is not independent of mineralogy. Carbonate and clay affect acid consumption, settling and washing. The blend plan limits carbonate-rich East 12 feed and uses campaign-specific metallurgical factors.

## Product and quality

Final product is uranium concentrate reported on a U3O8-equivalent basis. Each lot carries production period, drum population, net weight, moisture, independent assay, release authorization, seal identifiers, storage location and shipment allocation.

Title does not pass when a truck leaves Red Wash. Under the modeled contracts, title and final quantity settle after receipt and final assay at a licensed conversion-facility delivery point.

## Tailings and water

Tailings are neutralized and placed in engineered impoundment cells under the source-material license and environmental program. Process water is recycled where practical. Underdrain, groundwater and radon controls are operating systems rather than end-of-life paperwork.

The MW-17 trend and Cell 1 underdrain record are addressed together in the post-close
corrective program. The public record contains company-facing measurements and actions;
nonpublic evaluation material remains outside this repository.
