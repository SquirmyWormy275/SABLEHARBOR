Mineralization is hosted by permeable lower Eocene fluvial sandstone. Uranium precipitated along reduction–oxidation interfaces where oxidized, uranium-bearing groundwater encountered reducing carbonaceous material and sulfides. The deposit is expressed as stacked, sinuous lenses rather than one continuous sheet. That geometry explains both the mine's selectivity and the historic tendency for broad models to overstate continuity.

## Domains

- **North Roll:** comparatively continuous, moderate thickness and reliable recovery.
- **Central Lens:** historically best understood; principal 2026 production source.
- **East 12:** locally higher grade but discontinuous and subject to royalty step-up.
- **Lower Channel:** deeper, structurally quieter and more expensive to develop.
- **South Limb:** lower confidence and excluded from the base schedule except definition work.

## Mining method

Red Wash uses selective drift-and-fill mining with cemented paste backfill. A decline provides access to five principal level horizons extending to approximately 2,500 feet. Ore is extracted by LHD and underground haulage truck, transported to the surface ROM pad, sampled, crushed and processed in the adjacent conventional mill.

The method accepts lower bulk throughput in exchange for reduced dilution, inspectable stope boundaries and controlled ground exposure.
