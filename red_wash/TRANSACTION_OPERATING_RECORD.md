# 1. Executive case

Red Wash is a compact conventional underground uranium mine and mill in Carbon County, Wyoming. It is operated through a dedicated Sable Harbor-controlled company under the Pale Sun business line. Pale Sun owns resource stewardship, mine planning, commercial contracting, closure, capital allocation, and the parent interface. Red Wash is the physical site and operating boundary.

Sable Harbor acquired the business from Northstar Resources (Wyoming) LLC on July 18, 2025 after a seller re-trade, a Sable Harbor walk-away, and a return on materially different terms. The transaction recognized $42.0 million of operating assets, $4.5 million of current assets, a $16.0 million ARO, $2.5 million of other liabilities, and $28.0 million of cash consideration. Goodwill and transaction debt were zero.

The acquisition was not justified by heroic commodity pricing. The thesis was narrower: weak records, deferred maintenance, fragmented institutional knowledge, and real operating uncertainty had been treated as if they proved an absence of operable value. Sable Harbor accepted only the smaller case that survived technical, environmental, legal, commercial, financial, and operating challenge.

The 2026 plan carries 140 FTE, 175,000 tons at 0.170% U3O8, 92.0% recovery, 547,400 pounds produced, 500,000 pounds sold, $36.475 million revenue, $27.950 million cash production cost, $3.500 million DD&A, $4.000 million sustaining capital, and $5.000 million rehabilitation capital. The operation is profitable before inherited rehabilitation spending and negative after the full capital program. That is a derived result, not a chosen target.

# 2. Ownership and asset history, 2004–2026

## 2.1 Frontier Basin Minerals, 2004–2007

Frontier Basin assembled federal claims, private mineral leases, surface-access easements, and water options across approximately 7,480 acres. It reprocessed public radiometric work, mapped oxidation fronts, and drilled the first modern core holes. The early thesis was conventional roll-front uranium in lower Eocene fluvial sandstone. Frontier Basin never built a mine.

## 2.2 Carbon Basin Uranium, 2007–2010

Carbon Basin acquired the position and established that mineralization occurred as stacked, sinuous, discontinuous redox-front lenses rather than one continuous tabular body. Its 2008 internal resource was directionally useful but relied too heavily on broad sectional polygons. Capital-market deterioration and uranium prices prevented construction.

## 2.3 Northstar Resources, 2010–2025

Northstar acquired the project in December 2010, consolidated permitting, constructed a decline-access underground mine, compact 600-ton-per-day mill, tailings system, roads, power, water, laboratory, warehouse, administration, dry, and maintenance facilities. First concentrate shipped in 2013.

The mine operated through 2017. Production improved, but development lagged; experienced employees carried unrecorded continuity; maintenance was deferred; resource and grade-control models accumulated multiple versions; and the commercial book, inventory ledger, and environmental records did not share one authoritative timeline.

Red Wash entered care and maintenance in 2018. Northstar conducted restart studies in 2019. COVID disrupted contractor availability, equipment delivery, travel, and regulatory/technical work in 2020. Rehabilitation resumed in 2021–2022. Commercial production restarted in 2023.

Northstar's 2024 result appeared stronger than the normalized operation because legacy inventory was sold, repairs were capitalized, environmental work was deferred, and an owner-affiliate provided services below market. These were not all fraud; together they made the business look more durable than the underlying operating system.

## 2.4 Sable Harbor / Pale Sun, 2025 onward

Northstar opened a controlled sale in October 2024. Sable Harbor initially valued the business at a higher operating value than the final deal because the first data-room view did not integrate geological continuity, maintenance, tailings/groundwater, source-record integrity, contract consents, title, inventory, and key-person dependency.

Sable Harbor walked on May 5, 2025 when Northstar raised its price while the risk picture weakened. Northstar later returned with a structure that separated operating asset value from assumed liabilities, created escrow and holdback, obtained utility consents, cured title matters, delivered source records, and retained personnel whose knowledge constituted part of the acquired asset.

Pale Sun now governs the business. Red Wash is an operation first and a proving ground second.

# 3. Geology, drilling, resource, and reserve basis

## 3.1 Geologic model

Mineralization is hosted by permeable lower Eocene fluvial sandstone. Uranium-bearing oxidized groundwater moved through channel sands and precipitated where it encountered reducing carbonaceous material and sulfides. The resulting ore occurs along oxidation-reduction interfaces as stacked, irregular lenses.

That geometry is economically important. Broad interpolation can manufacture apparent continuity across barren or low-confidence gaps. Selective underground mining requires local control of grade, thickness, geometry, ground conditions, access, and dilution.

The principal internal domains are:

- **North Roll:** moderate thickness and comparatively reliable continuity;
- **Central Lens:** best understood and a principal 2026 source;
- **East 12:** locally higher grade, discontinuous, and subject to a royalty step-up;
- **Lower Channel:** deeper and development-intensive;
- **South Limb:** lower confidence and excluded from the base schedule except definition work.

## 3.2 Technical database

## 3.3 Acquisition basis

The accepted internal acquisition and operating basis is 2.5 million short tons at 0.170% U3O8, containing 8.5 million pounds and supporting 7.82 million modeled recoverable pounds at 92% recovery. A further 0.9 million tons at 0.145% is classified as inferred exploration inventory and contributes no base-case acquisition value.

This is not a public S-K 1300 technical report. Geological inventory, mineable shape, metallurgical recovery, production schedule, and economic value are separate claims.

## 3.4 Diligence adjustment

Sable Harbor's independent re-blocking removed East 12 stope shapes that bridged low-confidence gaps. The base case accepted lower early production and retained excluded continuity only as unvalued upside. A second material risk was feed-dependent acid demand and recovery, especially where carbonate and clay affected leaching, washing, and settling. The mill plan therefore constrains blend rather than applying one universal recovery factor.

# 4. Mine plan, operating system, and workforce

Red Wash uses selective drift-and-fill mining with cemented paste backfill. A surface decline serves five principal level horizons extending to approximately 2,500 feet. Development headings provide access, ventilation, services, exploration platforms, escapeway connections, and stope preparation. Ore is mucked by LHD, hauled to surface, sampled, blended, and processed in the adjacent mill.

The 2026 plan schedules 175,000 short tons through a nominal 600-ton-per-day mill. Nameplate capacity is not treated as uninterrupted annual throughput. Availability reflects development sequencing, rehabilitation, planned maintenance, environmental work, and ordinary operational variance.

# 5. Processing, metallurgy, product, and custody

Red Wash produces natural uranium concentrate reported as U3O8. It does not enrich uranium and does not fabricate fuel.

The process is:

1. ROM receiving, sampling, and feed blending;
2. primary and secondary crushing;
3. closed-circuit grinding;
4. sulfuric-acid leach with controlled oxidant;
5. solid-liquid separation and counter-current washing;
6. ion exchange and/or solvent extraction;
7. precipitation;
8. thickening, filtration, and controlled drying;
9. assay, drum filling, weighing, sealing, and release;
10. tailings neutralization and placement.

For 2026, 595,000 contained pounds produce 547,400 pounds at 92.0% recovery. Carbonate and clay affect acid use, wash efficiency, and settling. East 12 feed is campaign-constrained.

Each product lot carries production period, drum population, net dry weight, moisture, independent assay, QA release, seal identifiers, storage location, and shipment allocation. A truck departure does not by itself transfer title. The modeled contracts settle quantity and title after receipt and final assay at the named licensed conversion facility.

The product, tailings, process-water, underdrain, groundwater, and radon systems are part of the operating model, not end-of-life paperwork.

# 9. Valuation and investment-committee reasoning

Red Wash was evaluated through replacement/permitting value, resource cash flow, liability-adjusted net assets, and downside/abandonment cases. A headline EBITDA multiple was rejected.

The base case values only the accepted 2.5 million-ton operating basis, excludes inferred material, uses contract-specific price mechanics, includes rehabilitation and closure, and does not treat Sable Harbor's confidence in itself as an asset.

Mari Varela's kill criteria were:

- kill it if the economics require heroic commodity assumptions;
- kill it if closure or remediation must be ignored;
- kill it if “Sable Harbor is smart” substitutes for an operating edge;
- kill it if the case cannot survive community and water history;
- kill it if information quality is confused with operability.

The Board authorized the acquisition subject to final agreement, utility consents, permit transfer, title cure, escrow, retention, and no material adverse change.

The surviving thesis remained narrow:

> The market may not be wrong about uranium. It may be wrong about this mine.

# 13. Decision case

By April 2025 Sable Harbor had spent months on diligence. Uranium conditions were stronger, permits and the mill would be costly to reproduce, and Northstar knew Sable Harbor was serious. The seller raised price.

At the same time, the buyer found weaker mineable continuity, feed-dependent recovery, a larger maintenance backlog, environmental records that changed meaning when read together, incomplete customer and title conditions, inventory inconsistencies, and a site whose value depended on people no one had asked to stay.

The committee had to distinguish:

1. Sable Harbor understood the records better;
2. Sable Harbor could operate the mine;
3. Sable Harbor could buy the risk at terms that survived failure.

Options included accepting, negotiating without walking, walking while preserving a return path, or terminating. Sable Harbor walked. Northstar returned. The deal closed with a different allocation of risk.

The public record does not declare every decision correct. The private Alexandria companion contains seller knowledge, hidden causal truth, expected detections, counterfactuals, and the teaching note.
