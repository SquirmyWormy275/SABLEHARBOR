# Reference source inventory

These records support the approved corporate/Reno/Boise reference design. They contain original short planning topics, source locators and fingerprints, not licensed full source text. They do not certify complete applicable obligations or approve crosswalks.

- `soc2.json`: 61 TSC identifiers and nine description-criterion identifiers, original topic labels, candidate native IDs and one-based PDF page locations. Identifiers/pages were reconciled to the hash-pinned AICPA originals with `pdftotext -layout`; Privacy group headings ending in `.0` were excluded. The recorded pages refer to the downloaded file variants, not printed pagination. TSC points of focus and DC guidance still need source-context analysis; one short objective does not capture every circumstance.
- `hipaa_inventory.json`: every section and `P` element from Parts 160 and 164 in the pinned GovInfo Title 45 XML: 102 sections, 1,697 paragraph fingerprints. The source's volume 2 amendment date is August 28, 2026, distinct from retrieval and the live eCFR title date. Normalize paragraph text by joining XML text nodes, collapsing whitespace and hashing UTF-8. `P0001` etc. identify document-order paragraphs within each section, not legal paragraph citations. Leading markers are retained only as extraction aids; they do not reconstruct nested statutory hierarchy. `validate_source_inventory` re-reads the original and rejects missing/changed fingerprints.
- `c5_discovery.json`: 594 extracted B/AS/AC identifiers plus two explicit internal source-population gates from the previously verified C5 discovery export. The internal gates are not BSI requirements. Surrounding context, complementary criteria, assessment chapters and complete semantic decomposition remain review work.
- `c5_workpapers.json`: original planning decomposition for eight continuity criteria, tied to candidate native control IDs. BIA completeness/refresh, exercise cadence/participation, findings, customer communication and notification-plan review illustrate additional work around reusable baseline controls. All remain unreviewed.

Source hashes and URLs are carried into the catalog by `reference.py`. Originals must be supplied by `--source-root`; no account credentials or publisher documents belong in this public source directory. Available sources with no accepted review remain unresolved in the engine. Owner approval of a reference planning boundary is not an independent review of the generated scope record, mappings, source population or tests.

HIPAA interpretation must reconcile the HHS [reproductive-health legal-status notice](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/special-topics/reproductive-health/final-rule-fact-sheet/index.html). Presence in the XML is not proof that a provision remains enforceable. Keep direct associate duties, contracted support of covered-entity duties, supplier performance, exceptions and possible nonapplicability distinct; none is automatically excluded by the builder.
