# Baseline design authoring records

These original engineering analyses expand the approved Reno/Boise reference scope. They do not approve legal applicability, source inventories, mappings, appointments or operating effectiveness.

- `control_procedures.json`: one distinct proposed procedure for each of the 64 selected native baseline controls; owner IDs, native triggers and boundary records are bound during build.
- `soc2_analysis.json`: all 38 selected TSC criteria and DC1–DC9, with source-context considerations and explicit mapping limitations. Read the pinned AICPA originals and their introduction/points of focus; these notes are not substitute criteria or a mandatory points-of-focus checklist.
- `hipaa_analysis.json`: all 102 inventoried sections, classified as proposed direct duties, customer support, conditional processes, context or legal-status holds. Exact paragraph semantics and independent legal review remain open.
- `supplements.json`: authentication, cryptography/transfers, physical safeguards, capacity and environmental design additions to existing controls. Proposed technical choices need actual-system validation before deployment.
- `SERVICE_DESCRIPTION.md`: management's factual-completion workpaper, with nine description-criterion routes and eight candidate data flows.

Primary legal references: [45 CFR Part 160](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/subtitle-A/subchapter-C/part-160), [Part 164](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-45/subtitle-A/subchapter-C/part-164), [HHS associate guidance](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/privacy/guidance/business-associates/index.html), [HHS court-status notice](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/special-topics/reproductive-health/final-rule-fact-sheet/index.html). Checked September 11, 2026 Pacific. The current-text reconciliation and court-affected provisions remain review gates; the source XML alone does not establish enforceability.

The five-stage exercise uses invented criteria and reviews in a separate catalog. No exercise helper is used to stamp reviews into the actual reference catalog.
