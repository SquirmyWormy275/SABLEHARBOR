support this factual application; its diverse-business analysis does not require
identical products. Preserve separate member NOLs and separate nexus/minimum-tax
tests. Combined computation does not itself elect one group return.
[FTB1061](https://www.ftb.ca.gov/forms/2021/2021-1061-publication.pdf),
[FTB Legal Ruling1995-8](https://www.ftb.ca.gov/tax-pros/law/legal-rulings/1995-8.html).

[86Ill100.3450, effective November18,2015](https://www.ilga.gov/ftp/JCAR/AdminCode/086/086001000M34500R.html).
