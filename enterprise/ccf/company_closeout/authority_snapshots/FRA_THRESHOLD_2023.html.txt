      <h1 class="page__title node__title"><span class="field field--name-title field--type-string field--label-hidden">Monetary Threshold Notice</span>

  <div class="field field--name-field-keywords field--type-string field--label-inline">
    <div class="field__label">Keywords</div>
              <div class="field__item"> Monetary Threshold; 54; F6180.54; Threshold</div>

              <div class="my-4">
          <p><span style="font-size:12pt"><span style="background:white"><span style="font-family:&quot;Open Sans&quot;"><span style="font-size:11.0pt"><span style="font-family:&quot;Calibri&quot;,sans-serif"></span></span></span></span></span></p>

The threshold remains $11,300 through December 31, 2022.</span></span></span></p>
