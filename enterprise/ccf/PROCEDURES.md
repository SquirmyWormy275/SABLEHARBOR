# CCF preparation procedures

These are engineering proposals and public synthetic exercises. They confer no appointment, production access, risk acceptance, procurement authority or assurance opinion. All example dates and actors belong to explicitly fictional 2027 exercises; the source inputs and required populations are versioned separately from conclusions.

## Finance close

Use existing `LC-REVENUE` / `SH-REV-003` for Advisory. Schedule the scoped close before extracting its population. Collect acceptance/invoice events, journal lines and AR/deferred rollforward. Independently reconcile the declared source counts, period and boundary, then reperform the existing business control assertions. Incomplete evidence cannot pass. An independent reviewer must hold the exercise's scoped review permission; a different display name is insufficient. Preserve the original result, issue a time-bounded synthetic waiver where configured, escalate expiry, and retest the same original-period source after restoring a missing attachment. A later period's success cannot close it.

## Identity lifecycle

Use proposed `SH-IAM-004-CORP-PREP-01` / `SH-IAM-004`. SuccessFactors represents authoritative worker events, Okta represents accounts, and IBM Security Verify represents review/governance evidence. The example uses local JSON contracts, not live vendor APIs. Reconcile all scoped lifecycle events, account identities and entitlements, including contract expiration and mover removal. A terminated or expired worker must have no enabled account or active entitlements. A mover must retain only the approved new role's entitlements; a joiner requires approved role access. Review independently and preserve failed original evidence before documenting remediation. Timing targets in fixtures are exercise assumptions, not newly approved enterprise SLAs. Production role correlation and disclosure authority remain separate acceptance work.

## Runtime recovery

Use existing Reno `SH-BCM-003-RUNTIME-RENO-COLO` / `SH-BCM-003`. Inventory every recovery-required service and reconcile backup and restore evidence to that inventory. Verify original artifact hashes, restore content, dependency recovery, stated exercise RTO/RPO and restoration suppression of deleted/held data. Use the existing runtime restore/tombstone function. A successful backup job alone cannot pass a recovery exercise. Targets are synthetic exercise parameters, not measured or approved commitments. Actual provider reports, carrier independence, load qualification and deployed failback remain external gates.

## Review and exception authority

The fixture actor registry binds immutable subject IDs to scoped prepare/review/approve permissions and effective intervals. These synthetic grants model workflow separation; they do not establish real appointments. A reviewer cannot be the preparer even when that subject has two role labels. Waiver approval requires an authorized independent subject, a reason, a risk statement, compensating action and expiry. Expiry escalates; it never deletes the finding. Closure requires a later authorized independent re-performance of the original period and population, linked to a documented remediation. For corrected source versions, preserve old and new content identities and require the original natural-key population; do not replace an original failure with a new period.
