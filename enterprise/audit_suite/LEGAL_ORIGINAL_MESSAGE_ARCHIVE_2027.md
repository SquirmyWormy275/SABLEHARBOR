# Company legal mailbox originals

This company-owned archive corroborates the two inquiries retained in the
reviewed 2027 intake ledgers. It supplies original fictional message bodies,
their contract-question attachments, four channel configurations and forty-eight
monthly exports per branch. Records exist before any audit and require ordinary
company access to collect. Original intake screens, late classifications and
open exceptions remain unchanged.

The 57 native versions per branch contain four channel configurations, two
attachments, two messages, 48 monthly exports and a separate Internal Audit
archive reconciliation. Each export executes the recorded query against the
actual archive and filters channel and half-open business-event window. Its
timestamp follows the completed month. December exports become available on
January 1, 2028; they cannot support an earlier December 31 snapshot.

The provider inquiry requests retention/return instructions, and the customer
inquiry asks for response status. Their attachments are contract worksheets,
with no agency notice or official case identifier. These additional fictional
originals are authored as company history, not recovered real messages. Actual
insertion time remains separate from the simulated receipt times. No external
message is sent.

Completeness is limited to the four registered reference channels. Other legal
entities, personal inboxes and unregistered channels remain outside this archive.
Original mailbox support improves the auditor's evidence basis but does not
accept nonoccurrence, legal applicability, N/A or any audit conclusion.

Run `python -m enterprise.audit_suite.company_legal_message_archive_2027 create`
with `--private-repository` pointing to the reviewed main-private sources and
`--destination` pointing to a fresh path beneath an existing 0700 parent. Use
`verify` with the same arguments for a separate read-only check. The verifier
re-performs exact original content, attachment hashes, source joins, query
results, completed windows, immutability and private modes. Frozen P1 and all
predecessor sources remain unchanged.
