# Fictional 2027 provider lifecycle source

Status: isolated company-native training exercise. This is a prospective 2027
scenario authored in 2026, not a real deployment, provider representation,
executed agreement, PHI flow, legal determination or audit result. Its inputs
are the independently reviewed runtime transition V3 (receipt SHA-256
`0a0a448f619e490870f69959d196eb6d5ca3747af0d4fb68056195910ce6cc11`,
review `f1acaeaea963055f99b0de120fd1b0347ee984edf085d353e7af1e427234d5a9`)
and payload-free BA flow V1 (receipt
`20e3762aabb45fcfa3107d695da37e7eeb677f6c5e98f1bb767fa463fe857d9d`,
review `39bb9b071179ed672c5cf07c2867285d36b2f3e8cdce2106dc4472d17047911e`).
The owner-selected fictional-world decision, 2026 planned/nonoperating site
canon, and the independently reviewed 85-route provider/BA delta plan are
hash-pinned in the producer. No audit A/B side is paired to these Clean/Messy
source branches.

The source reconciles three distinct relationships: Switch Reno primary site
(`CP-SWITCH`), IDACORE Boise recovery site (`CP-IDACORE`, the exact canon ID),
and fictional recovery-support BA persona (`SIM-RECOVERY-SUPPORT-01`). Each row
pins its scenario role, site or support data-access boundary, review owner,
scenario tier/rationale, and the exact upstream contract/release or BA/flow
native tuples. Switch and IDACORE are facility dependencies; their synthetic
site terms do not authorize ePHI processing or establish a BA role. The
support persona is a training-only BA/subcontractor assumption, not a real
provider or an actual HIPAA status decision.

Clean registers all three relationships and contract-derived obligation
calendars before a 2027-09-16 population reconciliation. Messy initially
registers only the sites. The independent expected population comes from the
site contract tuples and the V1 BA scope/Boise flow, not the vendor list; it
finds the support omission. The reconciliation event is dated September 16 but
becomes available September 18. The support relationship and calendar are
backfilled with a September 20 source event and September 22 availability.
The stable `EXC-SIM-PROVIDER-SUPPORT-OMISSION-01` receives an October 20 open
follow-up version, distinct from the upstream
`EXC-SIM-BA-FLOWDOWN-01`. Backfill never erases the historical omission or
claims that the BA flowdown exception was cured.

A single Q4 internal review checks each of the three relationship/contract
joins and its obligation calendar. It indexes five site obligations per site
and eight synthetic support obligations, with each obligation's external
performance state `NOT_VERIFIED`. Assurance requests are
`NOT_SENT_NOT_RECEIVED`; no vendor report or external response is created.
Offboarding is terms-indexed readiness only: no revocation, deletion,
transition or financial closeout is asserted. The Clean/Messy receipts contain
24 native versions in total (11/13), six Q4 review records, one distinct open
Messy exception across two versions, and zero grants or collections.

Every native version has immutable identity/version/content SHA, source event
and availability clocks, actual import time, exact upstream/source pins and a
prior-event hash. The verifier checks reviewed source bytes, private regular
files and sidecar-free SQLite snapshots, required native tuples and role facts,
all locally replayed row bodies and clocks, exception semantics, and no audit
credit. Focused tests include missing upstream flow despite repinned receipt,
a dangling SQLite sidecar, an unsafe provider-operation assertion, source mode
change during immutable read, and receipt/limit tampering. This source may be a
future **candidate** for ordinary collection and exact procedures only after a
fresh zero-evidence audit pair and separate review. The existing active P1,
workpapers, Key and Atlas are untouched.
