`SH-SEC-001` / `SOC2:CC5.2` authored clause in the independently reviewed V10

and challenge an unsupported component. V10 already lists the selected SEC003

technology lifecycle or outsourced-component population. This source adds

`UNSUPPORTED_EXACT_CLAUSE`, `NOT_STARTED`, and `NOT_RUN`.

The locked architecture handover says the third-party landscape is not yet
designed. The 2027 exercise therefore uses two synthetic, non-deployed

dependency candidate. It names no vendor, contract, production service,
supplier commitment, or actual technology population. AS-P007 (Technology
Services), AS-P008 (CISO), and AS-P013 (Procurement and Supplier Risk) are
canon-listed fictional contacts whose appointment acceptance remains pending.
Their local exercise entries cannot approve deployment or the enterprise
