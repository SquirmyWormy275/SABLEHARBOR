# One selected fictional 2027 internal customer case

This company-owned source models an **internal** commitment, proposed change,
impact/notice gate and concern history for one fictional customer and service:
`SIM-COVERED-CUSTOMER-01` and `SIM-RESTRICTED-HOSTING-01`. The selected
customer/service IDs, narrow synthetic marker hosting/recovery obligation, and
Messy support-relationship omission come from the independently reviewed
PHI/BA V1 and provider lifecycle V1 native records. The service is not a
general Atlas customer population. There is no real external message,
customer concern/response/receipt, real contract, PHI, deployment or legal
status assertion. Future 2027 scenario events are not actual operation as of
September 29, 2026; CompanyStore's `imported_at` is the actual import clock.

The exact controls are `SH-PRD-002`, `SH-PRD-003` and `SH-PRD-004`: twelve
frozen tasks per side. Nine TOD/IMPLEMENTATION/TOE tasks have **inferred**
generic gates and can receive only bounded source-candidate context. The three
authored `ACTION-S-COMMUNICATION` clauses require a changed responsibility and
external concern traced through receipt, response and appropriate audience.
They remain unsupported. Every A/B task remains `NOT_STARTED`/`NOT_RUN`, with
zero task credit.

Source authority is limited. [ATL-209](../../docs/canon/DECISION_REGISTER_ADDENDUM_2026-09-09_ADVISORY_TIER1.md)
assigns product roadmap/platform/reliability/support to Atlas Product and
separates Advisory's professional judgment. The
[catalog](../../docs/controls/COMMON_CONTROL_CATALOG_v0.1.md) assigns
customer commitment, impact and issue-routing responsibilities, while the
[completion procedures](../ccf/assurance/completion_data/control_procedures.json)
require actual agreements, change/customer populations, notice duties and
support channels for full operation. The
[fictional owner decision](../../docs/internal/development/audit-suite/FICTIONAL_2027_SCENARIO_DECISIONS_2026-09-29.md)
authorizes the synthetic customer/BA training world but leaves service terms,
dates and approvers to exact source-pinned, independently reviewed scenario
specifications. This source uses only existing reviewed synthetic terms; it
does not create new accepted customer promises. The 75 synthetic finance-model
customer contracts are outside this selected service.

The pinned [organization chartbook](../../docs/organization/source/chartbook.json)
