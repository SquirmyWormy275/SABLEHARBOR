# Credit and Treasury operating histories

This separately versioned 2027–2031 model adds invoice-specific collection histories to the accepted business-finance source. Source assumptions are in [credit.json](../source/credit.json). The first Foundry Field terms include a partial receipt, a disputed balance and an explicit default with a later recovery. Other invoices retain their declared collection timing. These are public synthetic exercise histories, not customer observations, approved credit policies or operational payment records.

Receipts reduce the specific open invoice. Disputed, overdue and defaulted receivables receive distinct allowance rates, with the estimate reconciled monthly to both the AR register and financial journal. A writeoff removes only the surviving open claim and releases any obsolete allowance at close. A recovery reduces the recognized credit loss and cannot exceed the uncredited written-off claim.

Credit notes apply first to open AR, then to the remaining written-off claim, and finally to a cash refund supported by actual modeled collections. A contract credit after a writeoff therefore cannot manufacture a refund of money that was never received. Credits are capped by the uncredited invoice amount and carry unique identities; unearned credits debit deferred revenue, earned-service remedies debit revenue. Commercial owns contract-level deferred-revenue allocation.

Host liabilities accrue on accepted proceeds, while host cash requests follow customer receipt by one month. Defaults and credit notes release the unearned host share; later recoveries restore it. Refunds reduce pending host payments. If the affected host share has already been paid, a refund is held for a separately authorized host-clawback arrangement; the model does not invent a host receipt or a host receivable. Host cash requests remain subject to enterprise Treasury funding.

`allocate_treasury` reads the legal enterprise cash journal and its existing operating, investing and financing payment deferrals. It identifies individual vendor invoices, aggregate payroll batches, host settlements, capital requests, debt principal and other eligible operating requests. Amounts actually funded and outstanding are attributed FIFO within each cash-flow category, and older arrears receive their modeled repayment in source order. Each obligation retains the originating event and journal, date and reporting unit. This is a declared allocation assumption, not a claim about which real employee or vendor was paid.

Every period must reconcile opening arrears plus new deferrals less arrears paid to closing arrears, separately for all three cash-flow classes. Every individual request must equal cumulative funding plus the amount still unpaid. These amounts independently tie the enterprise funding register. Treasury allocation changes no legal journal, financing limit or prior-year reconstruction. It includes 2026 source obligations solely to carry any opening arrears faithfully into the successor; those source amounts are not restated. The package supplies monthly liquidity and due-date evidence. It makes no daily or weekly cash-sufficiency claim.

Evidence tables: `credit_history`, `credit_notes`, `credit_allowance`, `host_collection_settlements`, `treasury_obligations`, `treasury_obligation_history`, and `treasury_reconciliation`. The original invoice register and monthly subledger rollforward remain available.

Run `python -m pytest enterprise/operations/tests/test_credit.py` for regression evidence covering partial and disputed receipts, writeoff and recovery limits, credit ordering, refund backing, host settlement timing, duplicate/over-credit rejection, and exact individual Treasury allocations.
