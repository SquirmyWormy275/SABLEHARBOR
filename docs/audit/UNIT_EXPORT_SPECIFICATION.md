## Scope and identity

The seven units are Foundry Field, Atlas Meridian, Advisory, Willow, Project Cradle, Pale Sun / Red Wash and ARU / BS&T. Core units report through SHI in this model; names do not make them subsidiaries. Pale Sun and ARU preserve their separate legal companies beneath SHIH.

A release covers one complete enterprise generation identity and the named base/downside/expansion scenarios, retaining scenario and period on every scenario-dependent fact. The package manifest records source revision and source-file hashes, business input hash, canonical source paths, preserved legacy run/profile/seed/schema identity, period coverage and classification. Every unit supplies its own identity, explicit schema/column/row-count inventory, CSVs, allowlisted SQLite database and audit workbook. Multi-scenario packages are intentionally comparative; filters must use scenario and period together.

## Required financial populations

Each unit has annual/monthly financial statements, complete journal lines, monthly trial balances and the applicable operating subledgers. The parent package retains corporate/elimination residuals and the complete replacement bridge. Cash flow, balance sheet and income are part of the statement tables. Reporting clearing and same-entity asset clearing are explicit. Unit and legal books reconcile by scenario, period and account, including 2026 opening/close conventions.

The aggregate Treasury schedule is included as a shared reference, not incorrectly attributed unit financing. Its unpaid operating/capital/debt obligations qualify scheduled cash requests. Core and industrial evidence must never imply that a synthetic payment request is an observed bank transfer.

## Rejection and review rules

Exports are built from explicit allowed tables and classified unit populations, never by copying a raw enterprise database. Unrecognized source schema, unit/scenario scope or unexpected file inventory must fail. Database values are compared with their allowed source rows, and workbook financial values are compared with the database. Workbooks contain no macros, external workbook links or interpreted formula-like source strings. A public-artifact safety scan rejects credentials, private NAILEX implementation and hidden benchmark material.

The root manifest and checksum inventory must match every distributed file exactly, including unit databases and workbooks. Hash mismatches, missing files and stale extras fail verification. The release identity records a clean source revision; development builds are labeled and cannot pass the release workflow as clean builds.

Acceptance requires all seven unit extracts to pass together, reconcile to the legal enterprise and retain explicit evidence limitations. CI review artifacts and accepted main release evidence establish delivery. A polished workbook alone cannot establish completeness, operating effectiveness or real company history.
