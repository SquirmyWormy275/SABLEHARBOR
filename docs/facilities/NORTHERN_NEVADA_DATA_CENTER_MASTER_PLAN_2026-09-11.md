# SABLE HARBOR Northern Nevada Data Center — Master Plan

**Planning pin:** 39.5450, -119.4550
**Planning district:** Tahoe-Reno Industrial Center / USA Parkway industrial corridor, Storey County, Nevada
**Reality state:** fictional Sable Harbor facility in real geography; no real parcel/APN claimed
**Planning parcel:** 7.5 acres
**Planning acquisition date:** 2026-09-04
**Status at 2026-09-11:** LAND ACQUIRED / PRECONSTRUCTION; 0% vertical construction; 0 kW commissioned owned critical capacity.

## Purpose

The campus is the future Sable Harbor-owned primary computing estate for Alexandria, protected enterprise services, Atlas Meridian professional-plane services, and eligible private production workloads. Boise remains independent recovery. Reno colocation remains the transition primary until the owned site passes commissioning and service acceptance.

## Site program

Reserve the 7.5-acre planning envelope for a 10,000–15,000 sq ft initial hardened building, secure perimeter/setback, dual fiber entrances, electrical yard, generator/fuel yard, closed-loop/dry heat rejection, loading/receiving, secure media handling, staff/service parking, stormwater/fire access, and a future second data-hall/module area.

### Initial building program

- Secure lobby/mantrap and security station.
- Receiving/staging and quarantined equipment intake.
- Data Hall A: conventional compute/storage/network rows.
- AI Hall / high-density zone: liquid-ready, CDU space, high-density busway, enhanced floor loading.
- Network meet-me rooms A and B on opposite sides of the building.
- HSM/key room with separate access boundary.
- NOC/operations room and incident workspace.
- Secure spares/media room.
- Electrical rooms, UPS/battery rooms or modular equivalent, generator controls.
- Mechanical/cooling plant spaces.
- Fire/life-safety systems.
- Small technician office, break, sanitation, and maintenance support. This is not a general corporate office.

## Acceptance gates

1. Land/planning geometry and title package complete.
2. Geotechnical/drainage/environmental screens accepted.
3. Utility will-serve and transformer/switchgear delivery path documented.
4. Two physically diverse fiber routes accepted.
5. 90% design and code/permitting package complete.
6. Finance approves funded phase and reconciles capital/cash/depreciation treatment.
7. Security approves perimeter, access, CCTV, receiving, HSM/key, and emergency designs.
8. Technology approves workload benchmark and capacity module.
9. Commissioning agent completes electrical/mechanical integrated systems tests.
10. Recovery test demonstrates Boise independence and failback.
11. Production owner accepts migration/rollback.
