# Reader task checks

Six tasks were attempted from the repository README or Wiki entry pages, following
visible links rather than searching filenames. This is an **agent review**, not a
human user study or a measured estimate of how quickly a newcomer learns the case.
The checks cover the versioned Wiki sources; they do not claim that held changes
are already published in the live Wiki.

## Problems corrected

- The Wiki invoice description promised delivery evidence. It now names the
  contract terms, dated movements and journal entries actually supplied.
- The legal review link was below a 56-source inventory and missing from its
  generator. It now appears under the initial Reading packages section and
  survives regeneration.
- The legal database instructions omitted `build_readers.py`. Running only the
  first build discarded its separately generated dossier tables. Both commands
  are now documented and were executed; the complete reconstructed SQL contents
  matched the original database, whose bytes were retained.

The routes are recorded in [tasks.json](tasks.json). [RESULTS.json](RESULTS.json)
retains each exact path, file hash, observed worksheet/PDF result and finding.
These checks verify access and the stated evidence boundary. They do not establish
that external confirmations exist or certify the substantive case conclusions.
The end-to-end accounting work is separately recorded in the
[case walkthroughs](../../legal/gap-instruments/walkthroughs/README.md).

Replay with `python tools/reader/usability.py`; use `--check` to reject changed
routes or evidence until the results are reviewed again. Browser review images
and their input identities are retained under `qa/`. They approximate GitHub's
Markdown rendering and are not new corporate designs.
