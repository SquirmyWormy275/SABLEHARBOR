# Three exercises using the existing records

Start with a question, open the evidence, and save your own working paper. These routes require a browser and a spreadsheet application; neither SQL nor an AI assistant is needed. They are learning guides, not new company instruments, financial statements or answer keys.

On a narrow screen, scroll the table sideways to see the evidence and deliverables.

| Question | Route | Evidence you will use | What you produce |
|---|---|---|---|
| Does the ARU acquisition funding reconcile to its opening books? | [Acquisition reconciliation](ACQUISITION.md) | Existing closing statement and accounting PDFs; PPA and opening trial-balance CSVs | Funding bridge, opening-book check and explanation of book versus tax goodwill |
| Why does a zero receivable not mean the customer paid? | [Invoice to ledger](INVOICE.md) | Accepted Foundry Field PDF and five-sheet workbook | Movement reconciliation, event-to-journal trace and evidence request list |
| What would demonstrate compliance with the Taylor–Red Wash service terms? | [Contract obligations](CONTRACTS.md) | Complete existing agreement summary and its PDF | Clause-based obligation register and a list of records needed to test performance |

Use the route's exact period, identifiers and source version. The acquisition schedules reproduce a 2026 industrial model; the invoice packet draws from the separate `business-operations-v1.0.0` base-2027 forecast. Do not add their populations together. All records are synthetic and retain their original authority and limitations.

The linked documents and workbook already exist on accepted main. No exercise requires the new designs held in PR #145 or proposed billing details in PR #138. The Foundry Field packet's [acceptance record](../../finance/evidence/SH-FIN-HUMAN-001/ACCEPTANCE.json) controls its current status even though its frozen PDF retains earlier draft wording.

For each exercise, record your evidence paths, record IDs and reporting boundary before calculating. A completed working paper should distinguish a reproducible calculation from a claim requiring a missing agreement, external confirmation or filing. Keep your working papers separate from the supplied evidence. There is no private assessment solution supplied here.

If you want to extend a case, consult the [ranked legal evidence gaps](../transactions/GAP_PRIORITIES.md) first. They distinguish useful existing records from additions that would need new terms or approval.

[Machine-readable route and evidence register](routes.json) records exact evidence hashes and workbook sheets for link and drift checks. [Broader finance exercises](../../finance/READER_EXERCISES.md) and the [legal source directory](../transactions/README.md) provide further material after these three routes.
