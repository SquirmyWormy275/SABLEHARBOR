# Enterprise coordination and review addendum

**Document ID:** SH-ENTERPRISE-COORD-20260913

**Version:** 1.0.0

**Owner:** Corporate Governance

**State:** DELEGATED IMPLEMENTATION PENDING ACCEPTED MERGE

The [dated appointment decision](../canon/ENTERPRISE_APPOINTMENTS_2026-09-13.md) supplies occupants for existing functions. It does not replace the Board and capital governance charter, J2 doctrine, ESS charter or professional authority boundaries, and it does not amend their finance-locked historical bytes.

The control assignment bridge distinguishes primary contact, evidence custodian, operating record reviewer and independent assurance reviewer. A primary contact coordinates an existing owner role; local process ownership, source system, period and business boundary must be separately established. Internal Audit does not execute or approve management operating controls. Its own assurance process is subject to Board Audit & Compliance oversight.

Operating reviewers check completeness, accuracy, chronology and exception disposition in their assigned records. These are fictional quality-review delegations, not a transfer of all underlying executive, legal or technical authority. Corporate Controller records route to CFO; CFO and General Counsel record quality routes to CEO; Secretary records route to General Counsel; risk coordination routes to General Counsel; People, workplace and environment coordination route to ESS leadership; technology and security records have distinct reciprocal quality-review contacts; product and delivery records route within product leadership or CEO. Industrial subsidiary routes retain their own management chain. Scientific reviewer qualifications and professional conclusions are never inferred merely from a name.

A Board or committee control retains collective decision authority. Its named review contact checks the record and cannot replace quorum, votes, recusals or required signatures. Evidence recipes must retain actual member/action records for collective decisions and must not label a contact's signature as collective approval. Independent assurance contacts have no default operating approval authority.

The exported assignment's `operating_approval_authority` preserves the original role label; `collective_approval_required` marks Board/committee roles. `operating_reviewer_person_id` is the assigned record-quality reviewer. `reviewer_person_id` is restricted to independent assurance. Historical overlays require their own period-valid operating reviewer; the current appointments cannot be backdated.

No additional staffing, payroll, offices, headcount or physical occupancy is established by these coordination routes. Sources and preserved financial identity assumptions remain in the dated decision and workforce bridge.
