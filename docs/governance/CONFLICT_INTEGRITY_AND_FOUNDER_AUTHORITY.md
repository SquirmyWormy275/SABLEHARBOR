# CONFLICT INTEGRITY, CHALLENGE, AND FOUNDER AUTHORITY

**Document ID:** `SH-GOV-CONFLICT-001` | **Version:** 1.0.0 | **Effective:** September 2, 2026 | **State:** LOCKED

Directors are stewards of Sable Harbor, not representatives of appointing capital. Actual or apparent conflicts trigger disclosure, independent consideration, and recusal where appropriate. Harrison Vale and Wolf Ridge receive full board voice but no privileged commercial channel, private operational veto, special access outside proper process, or informal pressure through future financing.

Outside advisers, investors, scientists, specialists, customers, and employees may challenge aggressively. Outsider status does not confer authority, and insider knowledge is not infallibility. Authority stays with those accountable for the enterprise and carries an affirmative duty to seek and seriously consider informed challenge. Advice informs judgment; it does not confer authority.

Institutional memory matters, but power is not hereditary. The Original Eight are not a permanent caste. Authority expands through demonstrated competence and judgment and can contract when exposure outruns control.

Founder conviction earns latitude, listening, and strategic runway. It does not create sovereignty. If Daniel cannot persuade the board on a matter properly belonging to the board, conviction alone does not override it. Before decision, argue hard. After lawful decision, execute fully.

If the board overrules management, the board owns strategic authorization risk and management owns execution quality. Daniel's recorded good-faith dissent remains dissent; execution is not retrospective endorsement. He must not sandbag the decision. New material information can reopen it, but history never rewrites who owned the underlying judgment.
