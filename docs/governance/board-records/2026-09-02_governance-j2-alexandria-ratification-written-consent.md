## Resolutions

The Board ratifies the **Governance Constitution** as Sable Harbor's controlling governance doctrine, including authority following exposure and accountability; freedom inside the envelope; consequence rather than uncertainty as the escalation trigger; the duty of challenge and humility; and conflict integrity.

The Board approves the reserved-matters, subsidiary-autonomy, rapid-adjudication, Assumption of Risk, abstention, decision-quality, AAR, conflict-integrity, and founder-authority doctrines as controlled governance records.

The Board ratifies **J2 — Judgment & Junction** as an internal Sable Harbor institutional capability, not a legal entity or customer-facing business line, consisting of a small Headquarters, Contact, Judgment, Orientation, Junction Advisory Group, and Education.

The Board ratifies the J2 identity package and letterhead system as controlled internal identity collateral subordinate to the Sable Harbor corporate document standard.

The Board approves **Alexandria** as the umbrella institutional intelligence environment and ratifies the naming and purpose of **Pinakes** as the human catalog/portal, **Semaphore** as the cable-like traffic system, **Canon** as the institutional knowledge corpus, and **Daedalus** as the personal non-authoritative AI research companion.

The Board approves the Daedalus AI-authority boundary: intellectual aggression without institutional authority; no authoritative record write; no self-promotion into Canon/Judgment/Semaphore/EIB; no institutional authority except by quoting human-authored records; personal instance isolation; content-free skill sharing; and universal read with bounded disclosure as an architectural direction subject to future runtime controls.

The Board approves the corporate document system requiring canonical Markdown, controlled corporate publication where appropriate, and structured representation where appropriate, with source/publication/catalog reconciliation.

The Board directs Corporate Governance, J2 Headquarters, Orientation, and the relevant owning functions to maintain the controlled-document index, structured registers, validation routines, and supersession boundaries so that polished publications do not create canon beyond the source records they publish.
