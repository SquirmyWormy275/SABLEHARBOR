## Attendance

The meeting was held in person at Sable Harbor headquarters with remote participation available.

**Directors present:** Margaret "Maggie" Kincaid; Daniel; Priya Raman; Jon Bell; Thomas "Tom" Calder; Evan Mercer; Marianne Voss; Dr. Lilia Maratovna Galiullina; Hendrik "Harry" van der Velde.

## Purpose

The Board reviewed the company's governance maturity after the 2021 and 2022 financings, increasing technology/model risk, early physical-operating exposure, capital intensity, and the need to distinguish founder leadership from founder sovereignty.

## Materials reviewed

The Board reviewed the governance maturity memorandum, committee architecture memorandum, management-authority and reserved-matters summary, enterprise-risk update, early J2/intelligence-principle concept note, finance and technology oversight needs, and succession/governance benchmarking materials.

## Discussion

Daniel stated that separating the Chair and CEO roles would strengthen Sable Harbor without diminishing founder accountability or strategic conviction. Directors discussed the risk of both extremes: founder exceptionalism that insulates decisions from challenge, and bureaucratic governance that prevents the company from acting. Kincaid emphasized that the Chair role should protect decision quality, agenda discipline, and board challenge without becoming management.

The Board discussed five standing committees as the appropriate mature structure for the company's 2024-2026 trajectory: Audit & Compliance, Compensation & Human Capital, Governance & Nominating, Finance & Investment, and Technology & Operations. The Board agreed that the mature structure should not be projected backward into earlier periods when the company did not have the same exposures.

## Resolutions

The Board accepted Daniel's voluntary separation of the Chair and CEO roles.

The Board elected **Margaret "Maggie" Kincaid** as independent Chair, effective immediately.

The Board confirmed the five standing committees as the intended mature oversight architecture and directed Governance & Nominating to maintain committee membership, charters, and annual calendar materials consistent with the company's evolving risk profile.

The Board affirmed that founder conviction earns latitude, listening, and strategic runway, but does not create sovereignty where a matter properly belongs to the Board.

The Board directed management and Corporate Governance to preserve historical sequencing so that future records accurately show which governance practices existed at the time of each decision.
