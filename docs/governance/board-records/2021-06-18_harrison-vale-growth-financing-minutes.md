# SABLE HARBOR BOARD OF DIRECTORS

## Special Meeting Minutes — Harrison Vale Growth Financing

**Record ID:** `SH-BRD-MIN-2021-06-18`  
**Meeting date:** June 18, 2021  
**Record state:** LOCKED IN-UNIVERSE BOARD RECORD  
**Prepared for:** Corporate Governance / Board records  
**Related doctrine:** `BOARD_AND_CAPITAL_GOVERNANCE.md`; `BOARD_EVOLUTION_2016_2026.md`

## Attendance

The meeting was held by videoconference and recorded in the corporate minute book.

**Directors present:** Daniel; Priya Raman; Jon Bell; Hendrik "Harry" van der Velde; Dr. Lilia Maratovna Galiullina.

**Invited participants:** finance, legal, and commercial leadership as requested by the Board. Representatives of Harrison Vale Partners were excused for deliberation and voting except where management requested factual clarification.

## Purpose

Management presented a proposed growth financing led by Harrison Vale Partners. The Board considered whether the financing supported Sable Harbor's post-Crossing productization, repeatability, reliability, and commercial scale without converting the company into an investor-controlled operating model.

## Materials reviewed

The Board reviewed management's financing memorandum, proposed use of proceeds, capitalization and dilution analysis, investor-rights summary, operating plan, productization roadmap, implementation-capacity constraints, reliability/security investment needs, and commercial pipeline analysis.

## Discussion

The Board discussed the risk that services-era success could be mistaken for product repeatability. Directors emphasized that institutional capital should follow the Crossing-era product breakthrough rather than rewrite it as investor rescue. Harry van der Velde challenged the downside financing case and the company's ability to preserve operating independence if customer growth outpaced deployment discipline. Dr. Galiullina questioned whether technical and reliability investment was sufficient before broader commercial acceleration.

The Board determined that Harrison Vale's vertical enterprise-software and industrial-data experience was strategically useful if accompanied by balanced governance, no ordinary-course veto, no privileged commercial channel, and clear distinction between investor advice and company authority.

## Resolutions

After discussion, the Board approved management's entry into definitive documents for an approximately **$48 million** growth financing led by **Harrison Vale Partners**, subject to final legal review and no material adverse change in negotiated terms.

The Board approved the principal use of proceeds for repeatable deployment, productization, reliability and security maturity, commercial growth, customer operations, and finance/people systems needed to support scale.

The Board approved one Harrison Vale board seat while the negotiated ownership threshold remained satisfied. The Board appointed **Evan Mercer** as Harrison Vale's initial board representative effective upon closing.

The Board reaffirmed that investor directors serve as directors of Sable Harbor and not as constituency representatives with ordinary-course veto rights.

## Canon boundaries

This record does not create a special investor veto, does not convert Harrison Vale into an operating authority, and does not imply that the financing caused the Crossing. Exact definitive legal provisions, side letters, and option-pool mechanics remain implementation records unless separately canonized.
