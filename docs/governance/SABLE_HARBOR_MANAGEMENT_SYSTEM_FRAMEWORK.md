## Status

This file records the development framework used to derive SHMS from the accepted Sable Harbor corpus. The archaeology phase is complete and the resulting management doctrine is locked in `SABLE_HARBOR_MANAGEMENT_SYSTEM.md`.

It must not be used to add machinery, workflows, scorecards, certifications, process bureaucracy, or other management-system architecture that is absent from the locked SHMS doctrine.

## Archaeology method used

The development pass:

1. inventoried accepted principles across corporate canon, governance, J2, ESS, People & Culture, Technology Services, portfolio/authority/capital doctrine, and business-line lore;
2. traced candidate principles to their sources and authority state;
3. identified contradictions and productive tensions without silently reconciling them;
4. distinguished enterprise management principles from function- and business-specific mechanisms;
5. tested proposed universal principles against bureaucracy cost and local-autonomy impact.

The controlling result is intentionally smaller than this development map: ten management principles, CEO ownership, Orientation Officer stewardship in collaboration with the CEO, and no additional universal SHMS workflow.

## Representation rule

The locked SHMS is maintained in three synchronized forms:

1. canonical Markdown;
2. controlled corporate-letterhead publication;
3. structured/database representation.

This historical framework does not itself constitute a fourth SHMS representation or an additional layer of doctrine.
