# AUDIT & COMPLIANCE COMMITTEE CHARTER

**Document ID:** `SH-GOV-COM-AUDIT-001` | **Version:** 1.0.0 | **Effective:** September 2, 2026 | **State:** LOCKED
**Members:** Tom Calder (Chair), Lilia Galiullina, Maggie Kincaid
**Owner:** Board / Audit & Compliance Committee | **Related:** control dissent, assurance, compliance | **Cross-reference:** `../CONTROL_DISSENT_AND_RAPID_ADJUDICATION.md`; `../ASSUMPTION_OF_RISK_DOCTRINE.md`

The Committee assists the Board with financial reporting and control, compliance, whistleblowing, material adverse findings, and independent-assurance interfaces. It receives serious control dissent, monitors rapid adjudication and Assumption of Risk records, and preserves dissent and accountability. It does not command operations, replace the Board, or settle the still-OPEN 2026 Internal Audit operating model.

The Chair controls agendas and reports material conclusions, unresolved dissent, conflicts, and recusals to the Board. Members act for Sable Harbor rather than any constituency.
