# GOVERNANCE & NOMINATING COMMITTEE CHARTER

**Document ID:** `SH-GOV-COM-GOVNOM-001` | **Version:** 1.0.0 | **Effective:** September 2, 2026 | **State:** LOCKED
**Members:** Maggie Kincaid (Chair), Tom Calder, Jon Bell

The Committee assists the Board with composition, independence, nominations, conflicts, committee architecture, and governance effectiveness. It protects the separation of Chair and CEO, prevents institutional continuity from becoming hereditary power, and reviews whether board abstentions reveal deficient intelligence/process or individual fitness concerns.

The Committee applies cultural consensus norms without manufacturing legal veto rights.
