# COMPENSATION & HUMAN CAPITAL COMMITTEE CHARTER

**Document ID:** `SH-GOV-COM-COMP-001` | **Version:** 1.0.0 | **Effective:** September 2, 2026 | **State:** LOCKED
**Members:** Maggie Kincaid (Chair), Lilia Galiullina, Harry van der Velde
**Owner:** Board / Compensation & Human Capital Committee | **Related:** leadership, decision quality, learning | **Cross-reference:** `../DECISION_QUALITY_AND_AFTER_ACTION_LEARNING.md`; `../../j2/EDUCATION.md`

The Committee assists the Board with executive compensation, succession, leadership fitness, human-capital philosophy, and cultural health. It protects decision-quality evaluation from outcome-only judgment and expects candor that is hard on ideas and evidence but never demeaning to people.

Dedicated recruiting, promotion, and talent architecture remains future work; this charter does not invent it.
