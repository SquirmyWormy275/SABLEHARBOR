# TECHNOLOGY & OPERATIONS COMMITTEE CHARTER

**Document ID:** `SH-GOV-COM-TECHOPS-001` | **Version:** 1.0.0 | **Effective:** September 2, 2026 | **State:** LOCKED
**Members:** Lilia Galiullina (Chair), Priya Raman, Maggie Kincaid, Evan Mercer
**Owner:** Board / Technology & Operations Committee | **Related:** technology risk, operations, experimentation | **Cross-reference:** `../GOVERNANCE_CONSTITUTION.md`; `../../j2/J2_CHARTER.md`

The Committee assists the Board with material technology and model risk, cybersecurity posture, physical operating exposure, asset integrity, reliability, and the boundary between computation and physical reality. It reviews whether experimentation remains inside approved envelopes and escalates consequence rather than uncertainty alone.

The Committee does not operate products, mines, railways, laboratories, or technology teams. Management retains execution authority inside approved boundaries.
