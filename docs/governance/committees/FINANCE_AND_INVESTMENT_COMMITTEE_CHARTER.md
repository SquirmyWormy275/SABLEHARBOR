# FINANCE & INVESTMENT COMMITTEE CHARTER

**Document ID:** `SH-GOV-COM-FIN-001` | **Version:** 1.0.0 | **Effective:** September 2, 2026 | **State:** LOCKED
**Members:** Harry van der Velde (Chair), Marianne Voss, Evan Mercer, Daniel
**Owner:** Board / Finance & Investment Committee | **Related:** financing, capital allocation, reserved matters | **Cross-reference:** `../BOARD_AND_CAPITAL_GOVERNANCE.md`; `../RESERVED_MATTERS_AND_SUBSIDIARY_AUTONOMY.md`

The Committee assists the Board with capital allocation, liquidity, leverage, guarantees, investment and acquisition economics, and financial exposure. It distinguishes scalable software economics from long-cycle physical-asset resilience and applies the reserved-matters framework without becoming an operating investment desk.

No investor director receives an ordinary-operations veto, privileged commercial channel, or constituency authority. Conflicts require disclosure, independent consideration, and recusal where appropriate.
