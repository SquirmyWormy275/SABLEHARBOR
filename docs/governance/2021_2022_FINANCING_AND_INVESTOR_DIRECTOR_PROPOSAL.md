## Design constraints already locked

- institutional capital follows rather than causes the product breakthrough of The Crossing;
- financing governance is genuinely balanced, with no implausible unilateral founder or investor control;
- one investor represents vertical-software/growth discipline;
- one represents long-duration industrial capital comfortable with physical operations and mining infrastructure;
- each financing produces one board seat;
- strategic differences are substantive but neither investor is a villain;
- the 2026 board remains nine seats with founder, investor and independent representation.

## 2021 Series A Growth — proposed

**Lead:** Northline Growth Partners  
**Profile:** U.S. growth-equity investor specializing in mission-critical vertical software, data infrastructure and industrial enterprise applications. Northline is comfortable with implementation/services revenue where it drives durable software adoption, but pushes hard against uncontrolled custom engineering.

**Proposed financing:**
- close: May 2021;
- primary capital: **$32 million**;
- pre-money valuation: **$118 million**;
- post-money valuation: **$150 million**;
- modest employee/founder secondary liquidity is permitted but not central to the round;
- use of proceeds: engineering, remote deployment/productization, security/reliability, customer operations, finance/people systems and expansion of repeatable commercial capacity.

**Governance posture:**
- one Northline board seat while agreed ownership threshold is maintained;
- customary preferred protective provisions for fundamental changes;
- no veto over ordinary-course hiring, product roadmap, customer contracts within delegated authority or engineering operations;
- pro-rata participation and information rights;
- board seat sunsets or converts to observer rights below a negotiated ownership threshold.

### Investor director — Leah Moravec

**Role:** Northline partner; Sable Harbor director from 2021.  
**Background:** enterprise software and vertical SaaS operating/board experience; previously held operating finance/strategy roles at industrial-software companies before moving into growth investing.

**Board contribution:**
- repeatable deployment economics;
- gross-margin and customer-concentration discipline;
- software pricing and packaging;
- scalable implementation/customer-success models;
- organizational metrics and operating cadence.

**Productive tension:** Moravec is skeptical when exceptional bespoke work is described as a future product without evidence of repeatability. She is not opposed to Emberline, Cradle or industrial expansion; she insists the board distinguish strategic experimentation from disguised services sprawl.

## 2022 Series B Industrial Expansion — proposed

**Lead:** Ironcliff Industrial Partners  
**Profile:** patient private investment firm focused on industrial technology, mining services, infrastructure, logistics and complex asset-backed operating businesses. Ironcliff invests on longer horizons than a typical software growth fund and is comfortable with blended software/industrial economics where governance and downside controls are credible.

**Proposed financing:**
- close: October 2022;
- primary capital: **$85 million**;
- pre-money valuation: **$315 million**;
- post-money valuation: **$400 million**;
- use of proceeds: Foundry Field scale, security/reliability maturity, selected R&D including Atlas holding-pattern work, Emberline expansion, strategic hiring and balance-sheet capacity for future industrial opportunities;
- financing does **not** predetermine later ARU or Red Wash transactions.

**Governance posture:**
- one Ironcliff board seat while agreed ownership threshold is maintained;
- participation in Finance & Investment governance is appropriate but does not create a standalone investor veto forum;
- customary fundamental-protection rights;
- no automatic right to approve ordinary capital within the company’s delegated authority framework;
- no contractual mandate requiring Sable Harbor to own physical assets.

### Investor director — Owen Rourke

**Role:** Ironcliff managing director; Sable Harbor director from 2022.  
**Background:** mining-services/infrastructure investing, industrial operating-company boards and complex capital projects; financially literate but not cast as a software operator.

**Board contribution:**
- capital-project governance;
- acquisition diligence/integration;
- industrial operating models;
- physical-asset risk and financing;
- post-investment accountability.

**Productive tension:** Rourke is more willing than Moravec to support ownership/control of strategically important physical operations where the operational learning or economics are compelling. He is also more demanding about field accountability, asset integrity, downside cases and exit/recovery options than a pure software investor might be.
