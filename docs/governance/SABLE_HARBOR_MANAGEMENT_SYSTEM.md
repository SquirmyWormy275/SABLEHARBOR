# SABLE HARBOR MANAGEMENT SYSTEM (SHMS)

**Document ID:** `SH-GOV-SHMS-001`  
**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Decision date:** September 3, 2026  
**State:** LOCKED  
**Owner:** Chief Executive Officer  
**Stewardship:** Orientation Officer practice, collaboratively with the Chief Executive Officer

## Purpose

The Sable Harbor Management System is Sable Harbor's management doctrine. It consists of ten management principles. It does not create a separate process office, management bureaucracy, checklist, certification system, scorecard, or shadow authority structure.

The Chief Executive Officer always owns SHMS. The Orientation Officer practice stewards it collaboratively with the CEO, including as part of the transition whenever a new CEO assumes office.

## The Ten Principles

1. **Own your decision.**
2. **Spend the company's money like it's your own.**
3. **Give people room to be wrong.**
4. **Don't build bureaucracy to cover for weak management.**
5. **Know when to get out of the way.**
6. **Build something that can outlast you.**
7. **Reality gets a vote.**
8. **Build successors, not followers.**
9. **Build the thing you wish existed.**
10. **Competence and continuity are capital.**

## Boundary

These principles are deliberately concise. SHMS does not prescribe a universal management workflow beneath them. Existing Sable Harbor governance, authority, information, capital, learning, people, technology, J2, and business-specific doctrine continues to govern its own domain.

Ambiguity that properly belongs to managerial judgment is not a defect to be eliminated through additional SHMS machinery.

## Document control

This Markdown source is one of the three required synchronized representations of the controlled SHMS doctrine. The corresponding corporate-letterhead publication and structured/database representation must carry the same ten principles, ownership, stewardship, and boundary without adding doctrine.
