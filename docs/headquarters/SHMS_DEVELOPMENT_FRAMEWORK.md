# SABLE HARBOR MANAGEMENT SYSTEM — DEVELOPMENT FRAMEWORK

**Document ID:** `SH-HQ-SHMS-000` | **Version:** 0.1.0 | **Effective:** September 3, 2026 | **State:** NEXT PRIORITY / OPEN
**Owner:** Office of the CEO | **Do not treat as completed doctrine**

## Status

Development of the Sable Harbor Management System (SHMS) is the **next deliberate corporate-design workstream** after this September 3 headquarters closeout.

The session explicitly rejected generating a polished management constitution from generic management-model prose. SHMS must be developed by mining accepted Sable Harbor decisions and principles from the canon and originating conversations, classifying each as LOCKED, LOCKED DIRECTION, PROVISIONAL, OPEN, or superseded, and drafting only from that evidence.

## What SHMS is

SHMS is not an information system and is not a new corporate department. It is:

1. a short management doctrine;
2. a living body of methods and cases;
3. a very small stewardship capability in the CEO's orbit if and when stewardship is needed.

J2 remains the enterprise information/intelligence capability. SHMS is the doctrine for how management uses authority, judgment, information, capital, people, and learning.

If SHMS becomes a checklist bureaucracy, adoption-score program, process office, or large corporate function, it has violated its design intent.

## Required source-mining themes

The next workstream must extract, reconcile, and source at least:

- freedom inside the envelope;
- authority follows exposure and accountability;
- vertical authority / horizontal expertise / shared foundations;
- information symmetry and the right of reality to bypass hierarchy;
- judgment as a trained competency;
- one accountable decision owner;
- proportional documentation;
- dissent preservation and rapid adjudication;
- capital allocation and no use-it-or-lose-it behavior;
- continuity is capital;
- learning as work;
- anti-bureaucracy / burden of proof on restrictions;
- management humility and after-action learning;
- business autonomy and professional standards;
- institutional obligation to share useful learning across businesses;
- the principle that every institution needs explicit anti-mission-creep boundaries.

## Deliberately deferred items

This framework does not resolve:

- intervention mechanics for a failing business;
- exact doctrine for organic birth of a new business;
- Alexandria runtime/access implementation that was deliberately pushed downstream to the CCF/technology work;
- detailed ITGC/Common Controls Framework implementation.

## Acceptance criteria for the next session

- source/decision extraction before prose synthesis;
- explicit contradiction audit;
- comparison to external management-system analogs only after internal principles are extracted;
- no invented tools merely because another company uses them;
- a short constitutional core, a living case/method layer, and an explicit anti-bureaucracy boundary;
- triplicate publication: canonical Markdown, corporate-letterhead controlled publication, and structured/database representation.
