**Owner:** Sable Harbor Headquarters | **Location:** Sacramento, California

## Location and purpose

Sable Harbor's headquarters is in **Sacramento, California**. Headquarters is a working institutional center rather than a palace or a place from which the businesses are remotely run.

The campus houses enterprise support functions, the Office of the CEO, institutional convening, first-class learning and Education facilities, selected technical/project spaces, leadership and board facilities, and the common spaces needed to make headquarters useful. Business leadership should remain close to the actual businesses, sites, products, and operating environments rather than collecting headquarters badges as status objects.

A working physical scale discussed for the campus is approximately 250-350 people routinely based there, rather than placing the entire corporate workforce on one campus. Exact space programming remains an architectural implementation detail.

## Architectural direction

The campus is **beautiful, formidable, distinctive, and quiet in posture**. It should communicate long-duration institutional quality without becoming gaudy, theatrical, or a glass trophy tower.

The locked visual language is represented by the canonical September 3, 2026 exterior render at:

`assets/headquarters/sable-harbor-hq-sacramento-canon-2026-09-03.webp`

The repository WebP is a loss-preserving visual derivative of the user-approved render for practical repository use; the design itself, not a later reinterpretation, is canonical.

The render controls the intended architectural language: low-rise modern institutional massing; concrete, metal, glass, and warm wood; strong horizontal lines; a recognizable Sable Harbor arrival monument; carefully designed Sacramento-appropriate landscape; water and stone used with restraint; warm interior light; generous pedestrian approaches; and an overall impression of advanced research campus meeting durable modern institution.

No Sacramento landmark, government rotunda, or implied adjacency to the State Capitol is canon unless separately established. Earlier collage/brochure experiments are not controlling references.

## Workplace doctrine

The office must earn the commute. It provides genuinely good collaboration rooms, quiet work areas that remain quiet, useful commons, strong Education/classroom capability, project rooms, technical presence, dignified board space, and ordinary conveniences that eliminate employee friction. The design does not rely on candy jars, forced recreation, or novelty perks to create culture.

Headquarters should be somewhere employees can choose to work because being around colleagues is useful and pleasant, not because arbitrary attendance quotas force presence.
