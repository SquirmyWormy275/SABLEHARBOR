# Sable Harbor Advisory

**Status:** controlled business-line operating documentation  
**Controlling decision:** [`SH-ADV-ATL-DR-001`](../canon/ADVISORY_ATLAS_MERIDIAN_CLOSEOUT_2026-09-08.md)

Sable Harbor Advisory is a client-facing professional-services business adjacent to, but institutionally separate from, J2. It uses Atlas Meridian as its AI-native professional substrate and client relationship system. Advisory is organized around consequential matters, one common professional bench, three market-facing practices, outcome economics, capability transfer, and strict protection against using J2 as a commercial feeder.

## Controlling principles

1. The matter is the unit of production; the hour is not the product.
2. The client buys judgment, intervention, measurable results and durable capability—not a pyramid of labor.
3. Atlas Meridian is both a commercial product and the professional workbench through which Advisory operates.
4. Every transferable machine capability should have a client-ownable derivative; Sable Harbor's proprietary professional plane remains protected.
5. Advisory may recruit aggressively from operating lines and from the external market, but may not solicit serving J2 personnel.
6. Carry is reserved to former J2 professionals who satisfy explicit service gates; carry eligibility never creates line authority.
7. Conclusions are not for sale.
8. A transferred capability must be operable by the client without dependency theater.
