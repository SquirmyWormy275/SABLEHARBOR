## 1. Purpose

Advisory should be managed as a professional institution, not by revenue alone. The management scorecard balances growth, economics, professional quality, client outcomes, transfer, talent, product leverage and institutional risk.

## 2. Monthly executive scorecard

### Market and portfolio
- signed committed backlog;
- variable-fee opportunity by certification status/probability;
- qualified pipeline by practice and matter class;
- win/loss/decline rate;
- client/sector/geography concentration;
- repeat-client and cross-product relationship mix.

### Economics
- revenue by stream;
- contribution margin by matter class;
- DSO and collections;
- reserves/claims;
- outcome-fee disputes;
- Atlas-related professional revenue separated from Atlas product revenue;
- carry-pool forecast after hurdle.

### Delivery
- active matters by Q1–Q4;
- milestone slippage;
- transfer acceptance rate;
- failed/conditional transfers;
- value realization versus target;
- Matter Principal load;
- Independent Reviewer capacity.

### Quality and risk
- material review findings;
- client complaints;
- confidentiality/conflict events;
- model/agent incidents;
- material error corrections;
- safety/operating escalations;
- overdue remediation.

### Talent
- headcount by ladder/level and scarce capability;
- hiring pipeline;
- voluntary attrition;
- operating-line transfers;
- former-J2 hires after compliant post-service initiation;
- promotion readiness;
- workload/burnout indicators;
- sponsorship/apprenticeship coverage.

### Atlas/product flywheel
- Advisory matters using Atlas;
- client-plane active use;
- transferred agent/workflow packages;
- generalized product requirements accepted/rejected;
- product incidents affecting matters;
- client self-service expansion.

## 3. KPI interpretation rules

Metrics are signals, not autonomous management instructions.

- High utilization with worsening quality is a failure.
- High revenue with extreme client concentration is not healthy growth.
- High win rate may indicate underpricing or weak pursuit discipline.
- High recurring revenue created by failed transfer is not success.
- High Atlas usage created by forcing irrelevant product adoption is not product-market evidence.

## 4. Annual plan

The President prepares an annual plan covering:

1. market thesis and target client problems;
2. practice priorities;
3. revenue/backlog scenarios;
4. outcome-fee risk exposure;
5. Atlas product relationship and roadmap dependencies;
6. talent and succession;
7. operating-line recruiting needs;
8. J2 firewall assurance;
9. quality/risk priorities;
10. investment in methods, evaluation and knowledge;
11. capital/working-capital needs;
12. carry-pool forecast and unit capacity;
13. client concentration and diversification;
14. scenario stress tests.

## 5. Required planning scenarios

At minimum:

- **Base** — expected demand and normal outcome realization.
- **Downside demand** — major pipeline shortfall.
- **Outcome underperformance** — variable fees materially below target.
- **Talent shock** — loss of several senior scarce professionals.
- **Client concentration event** — largest client exits or is conflicted out.
- **Professional claim** — material quality/cyber/liability event.
- **Atlas acceleration** — product adoption grows faster than Advisory capacity.
- **Capacity-constrained boom** — demand exceeds safe staffing capacity.

## 6. Practice plans

Each Practice Leader maintains a concise plan for:

- client problems and market demand;
- distinctive methods/credentials;
- talent depth/gaps;
- Atlas capabilities required;
- quality/risk themes;
- publications/market development;
- priority pursuits;
- cross-practice dependencies.

Practice plans do not create separate staffing empires.

## 7. Talent plan

The annual talent plan identifies:

- promotions;
- succession for senior professional/management roles;
- operating-line talent targets;
- external expert needs;
- associate intake;
- professional curriculum priorities;
- load balancing;
- likely carry grants from the existing eligible former-J2 population only.

Serving J2 names are never included as prospective hires or carry candidates.

## 8. Investment budget

Advisory explicitly budgets nonbillable investment for:

- methods;
- knowledge/case learning;
- quality/evaluation;
- Atlas integrations/tooling owned by Advisory;
- sector research;
- professional education;
- client-safe demos/synthetic cases;
- recruiting and market development.

The firm does not require every hour of institutional improvement to be attached to a client code.

## 9. Matter portfolio review

Quarterly, the President and senior professional leadership review the active portfolio for:

- matters the firm should stop;
- overloaded Matter Principals;
- concentration/conflict changes;
- value metrics likely to fail;
- quality-risk upgrades;
- transfer slippage;
- client dependencies being mischaracterized as Sable Harbor failure or vice versa;
- opportunities to reduce unnecessary labor through Atlas/agents.

## 10. Annual quality and reputation review

The year-end review integrates financial results with:

- quality report;
- client feedback;
- claims;
- thought-leadership quality;
- talent health;
- Atlas product contribution;
- independence/conflict outcomes;
- J2-boundary compliance.

Senior compensation and leadership evaluation should reflect this balanced record.

## 11. Board/enterprise reporting

Advisory reports to enterprise oversight at a level sufficient to understand:

- performance;
- risk;
- major clients/concentration;
- material claims;
- significant outcome-fee exposure;
- carry economics;
- Atlas dependency;
- talent/succession;
- compliance with J2 boundary and enterprise authorities.

Client-specific confidential detail is limited to what oversight actually requires.

## 12. Standard

A Tier 1 practice should be able to show a serious governing body how growth, quality, risk, talent, product leverage and professional economics fit together. The scorecard exists to make that system visible without reducing professional judgment to a dashboard score.
