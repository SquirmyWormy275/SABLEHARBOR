# ATLAS MERIDIAN — PROFESSIONAL PLATFORM AND CLIENT SYSTEM

**Document ID:** `SH-ATL-016`  
**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Effective:** September 8, 2026  
**State:** `LOCKED DIRECTION`  
**Product owner:** Atlas Meridian product organization  
**Related:** `ATL-001`–`ATL-015`; `SH-ADV-ATL-DR-001`; `SH-ADV-001`

## 1. Product identity

Atlas Meridian remains what its canon established: a system designed to **investigate, not merely answer**.

The September 8 closeout extends that product insight into its mature commercial role.

Atlas Meridian is simultaneously:

- a client-licensed commercial product;
- the digital front door for Sable Harbor Advisory clients;
- the professional matter system used by Advisory;
- the environment in which problem-specific agent constellations are composed, governed, observed and transferred.

This does not make Atlas Meridian a consulting organization.

## 2. Atlas organization

The dedicated Atlas Meridian organization is a **product organization**.

Its people are product developers and the supporting disciplines required to create and operate a serious enterprise product, including as needed:

- product management;
- product engineering;
- design;
- agent/runtime engineering;
- AI/model evaluation;
- data and knowledge architecture;
- workflow and orchestration engineering;
- security;
- tenancy and entitlements;
- reliability and operations;
- integration and connector engineering;
- developer platform/tooling;
- transfer/export tooling;
- customer/product support;
- documentation and product education;
- product analytics and quality.

Atlas product personnel are not counted as Advisory professionals merely because Advisory uses Atlas intensely.

### Boundary

Atlas product people may:

- support product incidents on matters;
- participate in requirements discovery;
- support design partners;
- build generalized capability discovered through client work;
- support secure configuration or integration where product expertise is required.

They do not automatically:

- own client professional conclusions;
- become the Matter Principal;
- substitute for an Advisory bench member;
- receive client authority because they control the platform;
- convert client-specific confidential material into product features without the required rights and controls.

## 3. Two-plane architecture

Atlas uses a two-plane design.

### 3.1 Sable Harbor professional plane

The professional plane is the firm-controlled environment that may contain:

- proprietary orchestration;
- Sable Harbor professional methods;
- internal model and agent policies;
- internal evaluation infrastructure;
- protected cross-matter learning;
- firm-only knowledge;
- protected prompts and system instructions;
- internal research and test corpora;
- proprietary workflow components;
- internal professional review tools;
- confidential Sable Harbor operating knowledge;
- controls that should not be exposed or transferred merely because they were used on a client matter.

The professional plane is not client property.

### 3.2 Client plane

The client plane is the governed environment the client may use directly and may continue to use after an Advisory matter.

It may contain:

- client data and evidence;
- client system connectors;
- client-specific entity and relationship models;
- matter records;
- requests and responses;
- decisions and action records;
- assumptions and uncertainties;
- client-specific workflows;
- client-specific agents;
- client-specific memory;
- tests and evaluation suites created for the client's transferred capability;
- operating runbooks;
- outcome baseline and measurement records;
- licensed product functionality;
- client-safe provenance.

The client plane must be excellent enough to stand on its own. It is not a deliberately crippled teaser for Advisory.

### 3.3 The 70/30 heuristic

The `70/30` language is a design heuristic: a substantial portion of the total Sable Harbor professional system remains proprietary while a meaningful client-safe capability is intentionally transferable and operable by the client.

It is not:

- a code split;
- an ownership ratio;
- a data-rights ratio;
- a revenue-share formula;
- a mandatory quantitative architecture rule.

## 4. Atlas as the client relationship system

For an Advisory relationship, Atlas is more than an analysis interface.

It should support the complete client-side matter lifecycle where appropriate.

### 4.1 Intake and secure transfer

- secure file upload;
- bulk evidence delivery;
- source registration;
- connector-based transfer;
- request lists;
- chain-of-custody/provenance metadata;
- data-rights and classification metadata;
- versioning and supersession;
- retention/disposition state.

### 4.2 Matter workspace

- problem statement;
- authority boundary;
- stakeholders and decision owner;
- issue/question register;
- evidence map;
- model and assumption register;
- contradictions and unresolved gaps;
- decision record;
- work products;
- client comments and disposition;
- milestones;
- outcome/value schedule;
- transfer state.

### 4.3 Client collaboration

Atlas should allow the client to see enough of the work to understand what Sable Harbor is doing without exposing firm-only machinery.

Useful patterns include:

- evidence requests;
- review queues;
- structured questions;
- finding disposition;
- decision preparation;
- scenario comparison;
- action tracking;
- acceptance testing;
- transferred-agent operation.

## 5. Matter constellations

A matter may instantiate a constellation of specialized agents and tools.

The constellation is built around the problem rather than around a fixed catalog of digital roles.

### Required governance metadata

Atlas should preserve, at minimum:

- agent/tool identity;
- version;
- model/runtime;
- granted data scope;
- granted action scope;
- stop conditions;
- human owner;
- input evidence;
- outputs;
- review/adoption state;
- errors and exceptions;
- changes to instructions or tooling;
- transferability classification.

### Human accountability

Agents may research, reconcile, model, test, draft, monitor and execute bounded workflows. They do not erase human accountability for:

- professional judgment;
- conclusions;
- operating authority;
- capital authority;
- unsafe conditions;
- external commitments;
- client promises;
- high-consequence escalation.

## 6. Client-ownable derivatives

The default engineering objective for a transferable matter is **forkability without leakage**.

At close, Sable Harbor should be able to derive a hardened client package from the matter constellation.

The package may include:

- client-specific agents;
- client-specific workflow logic;
- client-safe prompts/instructions;
- connectors;
- schemas;
- tests;
- monitoring;
- operating runbooks;
- client-specific memory;
- evaluation criteria;
- change procedures;
- owner/maintainer assignments;
- version and dependency records.

### Transfer acceptance standard

A transferred machine capability is accepted only when:

1. the client has a named owner;
2. required rights and licenses are clear;
3. the client can run the capability in its permitted environment;
4. operating limits and stop conditions are known;
5. tests pass;
6. monitoring is in place where necessary;
7. the client understands what is automated and what still requires human judgment;
8. Sable Harbor proprietary components not licensed for transfer are absent;
9. the client can distinguish its owned material from licensed Atlas product capability;
10. failure and support paths are explicit.

## 7. Professional plane protection

A client does not acquire Sable Harbor's professional plane by purchasing a matter.

Protected material may include:

- cross-client memory;
- unrelated matter patterns;
- Sable Harbor operating-business secrets;
- J2 records;
- internal benchmarks and evaluation sets;
- firm-wide agent infrastructure;
- proprietary professional methods;
- internal controls and review systems;
- unrelated source code;
- internal incident history;
- internal institutional memory.

Protection must not become an excuse to withhold what the engagement promised to transfer.

## 8. Commercial modes

### Tell me

The client needs the answer, judgment or intervention but does not need to operate the full workflow itself.

Atlas functions as the matter portal and record while Sable Harbor uses the deeper professional plane.

### Work with me

The client and Sable Harbor jointly work in a client-visible environment. The client sees evidence, models, decisions, progress and transferred capability while protected firm-only machinery remains behind the boundary.

### Let me do it myself

The client licenses Atlas and operates the client plane without a standing Advisory matter. It may later escalate a consequential problem into Advisory using the same underlying client environment and evidence base.

## 9. Product/advisory flywheel

The intended flywheel is:

**Client work exposes hard problems → Advisory learns where professional capability is needed → Atlas product developers identify generalized product opportunities → product capability improves → clients can do more themselves → harder problems surface → Advisory is engaged at a higher level.**

The flywheel must not violate confidentiality or productize client IP without rights.

## 10. Relationship to Foundry Field

Foundry Field remains the flagship deployable operational product and represented terrain.

Where a client uses both products:

- Foundry Field may represent operational reality, systems and workflows;
- Atlas Meridian may investigate across that represented terrain and other authorized sources;
- Advisory may use both to conduct a matter;
- the client may continue using both after Advisory exits.

Neither product is merely a feature of the other.

## 11. Product quality standard

Atlas Meridian should be judged on whether it can support consequential professional work without becoming a black box.

Required qualities include:

- provenance;
- temporal integrity;
- evidence traceability;
- uncertainty representation;
- model/agent versioning;
- authority boundaries;
- explainable action history;
- export/transfer capability;
- tenancy and entitlement isolation;
- secure client collaboration;
- reproducibility of material calculations and outcome measurements;
- graceful admission of missing evidence;
- human decision ownership.

The mature product should make a serious client more capable on its own, not merely more dependent on Sable Harbor.
