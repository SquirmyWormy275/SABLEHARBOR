## 1. Product identity

Atlas Meridian remains what its canon established: a system designed to **investigate, not merely answer**.

Atlas Meridian is simultaneously:

- a client-licensed commercial product;
- the digital front door for Sable Harbor Advisory clients;
- the professional matter system used by Advisory;
- the environment in which problem-specific agent constellations are composed, governed, observed and transferred.

This does not make Atlas Meridian a consulting organization.

## 2. Atlas organization

The dedicated Atlas Meridian organization is a **product organization**.

Its people are product developers and supporting product disciplines, including as needed:

- product management;
- product engineering;
- design;
- agent/runtime engineering;
- AI/model evaluation;
- data and knowledge architecture;
- workflow/orchestration engineering;
- security;
- tenancy and entitlements;
- reliability and operations;
- integration/connector engineering;
- developer platform/tooling;
- transfer/export tooling;
- customer/product support;
- documentation/product education;
- product analytics and quality.

Atlas product personnel are not counted as Advisory professionals merely because Advisory uses Atlas intensely.

### Boundary

Atlas product people may support incidents, requirements discovery, design partners, generalized feature development, secure configuration and integration. They do not automatically own client professional conclusions, become Matter Principal, substitute for the Advisory bench, receive client operating authority, or convert client confidential material into product features without required rights.

## 3. Two-plane architecture

Atlas uses a two-plane design.

### 3.1 Sable Harbor professional plane

The professional plane may contain:

- proprietary orchestration;
- Sable Harbor professional methods;
- internal model/agent policies;
- internal evaluation infrastructure;
- protected cross-matter learning;
- firm-only knowledge;
- protected prompts/system instructions;
- research/test corpora;
- proprietary workflow components;
- professional review tools;
- confidential Sable Harbor operating knowledge;
- internal controls not transferred merely because they were used on a client matter.

The professional plane is not client property.

### 3.2 Client plane

The client plane is the governed environment the client may use directly and may continue using after an Advisory matter. It may contain:

- client data/evidence;
- client system connectors;
- client-specific entity/relationship models;
- matter records;
- requests/responses;
- decisions/action records;
- assumptions/uncertainties;
- client-specific workflows and agents;
- client-specific memory;
- evaluation suites;
- runbooks;
- outcome baseline/measurement records;
- licensed product functionality;
- client-safe provenance.

The client plane must be excellent enough to stand on its own. It is not a deliberately crippled teaser for Advisory.

### 3.3 The 70/30 heuristic

The `70/30` language is a design heuristic: substantial Sable Harbor professional capability remains proprietary while meaningful client-safe capability is intentionally transferable and operable by the client.

It is not a code split, ownership ratio, data-rights ratio, revenue-share formula or mandatory quantitative architecture rule.

## 5. Matter constellations

A matter may instantiate specialized agents and tools around the problem rather than a fixed catalog of digital roles.

Atlas preserves at minimum:

- agent/tool identity;
- version;
- model/runtime;
- granted data/action scope;
- stop conditions;
- human owner;
- input evidence;
- outputs;
- review/adoption state;
- errors/exceptions;
- instruction/tooling changes;
- transferability classification.

Agents may research, reconcile, model, test, draft, monitor and execute bounded workflows. They do not erase human accountability for professional judgment, conclusions, operating/capital authority, unsafe conditions, external commitments or high-consequence escalation.

## 6. Client-ownable derivatives

The default engineering objective for a transferable matter is **forkability without leakage**.

A hardened client package may include:

- client-specific agents;
- workflow logic;
- client-safe prompts/instructions;
- connectors;
- schemas;
- tests;
- monitoring;
- runbooks;
- client-specific memory;
- evaluation criteria;
- change procedures;
- owner/maintainer assignments;
- version/dependency records.

A transferred machine capability is accepted only when the client has an owner, rights/licenses are clear, the client can run it in the permitted environment, limits/stop conditions are known, tests pass, monitoring exists where required, human judgment boundaries are understood, unlicensed Sable Harbor components are absent, ownership/license distinctions are clear, and failure/support paths are explicit.

## 7. Professional plane protection

A client does not acquire Sable Harbor's professional plane by purchasing a matter.

Protected material may include:

- cross-client memory;
- unrelated matter patterns;
- Sable Harbor operating-business secrets;
- J2 records;
- internal benchmarks/evaluation sets;
- firm-wide agent infrastructure;
- proprietary professional methods;
- internal controls/review systems;
- unrelated source code;
- incident history;
- internal institutional memory.

Protection must not become an excuse to withhold what the engagement promised to transfer.

## 8. Commercial modes

### Tell me
The client needs the answer, judgment or intervention but does not need to operate the full workflow itself.

### Work with me
The client and Sable Harbor jointly operate in a client-visible environment while protected firm-only machinery remains behind the boundary.

### Let me do it myself
The client licenses Atlas and operates the client plane without a standing Advisory matter. A consequential problem may later escalate into Advisory using the same client environment and evidence base.

## 9. Product editions and licensing

Commercial editions, tenant model, institutional pricing, support tiers, APIs/connectors, security baseline, data export and release management are controlled by [`ATLAS_MERIDIAN_COMMERCIAL_PRODUCT_STANDARD.md`](ATLAS_MERIDIAN_COMMERCIAL_PRODUCT_STANDARD.md).

The edition model is:

- **Atlas Meridian Core** — secure evidence, provenance, investigation/matter workspace, controlled agents and transferable workflows;
- **Atlas Meridian Enterprise** — enterprise tenancy, advanced entitlements, connectors, APIs, evaluation, audit export, SSO/SCIM and governance;
- **Atlas Meridian Professional** — Sable Harbor-controlled professional plane, not generally sold as a client edition;
- **Atlas Meridian Managed** — client-plane capability operated by Sable Harbor under managed-continuity terms.

Default licensing is institutional/tenant-based, not primarily per seat, token or consulting hour.

## 10. Product/advisory flywheel

The intended flywheel is:

**Client work exposes hard problems → Advisory identifies where professional capability is needed → Atlas product developers identify generalized product opportunities → Atlas improves → clients can do more themselves → harder problems surface → Advisory is engaged at a higher level.**

The flywheel must not violate confidentiality or productize client IP without rights.

## 11. Relationship to Foundry Field

Foundry Field remains the flagship deployable operational product and represented terrain.

Where a client uses both products:

- Foundry Field may represent operational reality, systems and workflows;
- Atlas Meridian may investigate across that represented terrain and other authorized sources;
- Advisory may use both to conduct a matter;
- the client may continue using both after Advisory exits.

Neither product is merely a feature of the other.

## 12. IP, data and method boundary

The four-class transfer model in `SH-ADV-007` applies to every Atlas matter artifact:

- client-owned;
- licensed Sable Harbor capability;
- Sable Harbor reserved professional IP;
- restricted/nontransferable third-party or institutional material.

Transferred agent/workflow packages must not silently call Sable Harbor-only memory or Professional-plane resources unless a continuing dependency is explicitly licensed.

## 13. Product quality standard

Atlas Meridian is judged on whether it supports consequential professional work without becoming a black box.

Required qualities include:

- provenance;
- temporal integrity;
- evidence traceability;
- uncertainty representation;
- model/agent versioning;
- authority boundaries;
- explainable action history;
- export/transfer capability;
- tenant/entitlement isolation;
- secure collaboration;
- reproducibility of material calculations/outcome measurements;
- graceful admission of missing evidence;
- human decision ownership.

## 14. Strategic standard

Atlas should make Advisory more powerful without making Advisory necessary for routine use. The product succeeds when clients can do more themselves, transferred capability survives, harder questions surface, and Advisory is engaged because the problem is consequential—not because the software was intentionally crippled.
