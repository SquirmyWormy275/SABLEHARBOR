# SABLE HARBOR ADVISORY — TALENT DEVELOPMENT AND PERFORMANCE STANDARD

**Document ID:** `SH-ADV-012`  
**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Effective:** September 9, 2026  
**State:** `LOCKED DIRECTION`  
**Owner:** President, Sable Harbor Advisory with People & Culture and Professional Quality  
**Related:** `SH-ADV-003`–`005`; enterprise People & Culture doctrine

## 1. Talent model

Advisory is built around professional depth, operating credibility, judgment and the ability to leave a client more capable. It does not optimize for a large analyst pyramid.

The firm recruits four primary talent archetypes:

1. experienced operators and builders from Sable Harbor business lines;
2. qualifying former J2 professionals;
3. external domain experts, technical builders and institutional specialists;
4. high-slope early-career professionals who can be apprenticed into real judgment.

## 2. Recruiting priority

When two candidates are equally strong analytically, the firm should generally prefer the person with deeper evidence of having owned real consequences.

Signals include:

- operated physical systems;
- owned production, maintenance, logistics, reclamation, capital, field or safety outcomes;
- built technical systems that survived real use;
- made consequential decisions under incomplete evidence;
- led professional/intelligence work where conclusions could be wrong and challenged;
- can teach clients how to reason, not merely present conclusions.

## 3. Operating-line veterans

Operating-line veterans are a premium talent category. Advisory may recruit them directly by name and compensate them at the top of the non-carry market where justified.

The firm should not force an exceptional mine operator, engineer, dispatcher, technical builder or reclamation leader to acquire a conventional consulting résumé before their operating expertise is respected.

## 4. External hires

External hires may join at any professional level based on demonstrated capability. Titles are not mapped mechanically from prior-firm titles.

Experienced-hire assessment includes:

- judgment case;
- evidence/source exercise;
- client communication;
- domain depth;
- implementation/transfer scenario;
- ethical/conflict scenario;
- technical/agent fluency appropriate to role.

## 5. Early-career intake

Advisory may maintain a small Associate Professional intake. The program is intentionally selective and apprenticeship-heavy.

Associates rotate through:

- evidence and investigation;
- operating/field exposure;
- Atlas/agent workflows;
- client working sessions;
- implementation;
- quality review;
- transfer and closeout.

The firm does not use associates as cheap document-production capacity.

## 6. Apprenticeship

Each Associate/Senior Professional has:

- a line manager for employment/development;
- a professional sponsor for craft and long-term growth;
- matter-specific feedback from principals;
- an annual evidence-based development plan.

The sponsor may sit outside the person's management chain.

## 7. Performance dimensions

Annual performance is assessed across eight dimensions:

| Dimension | What good looks like |
|---|---|
| Professional judgment | recognizes what matters, handles uncertainty, makes defensible calls |
| Evidence discipline | sources, provenance, contradiction and limitations are handled correctly |
| Client impact | outcomes, decisions and capability endure after Sable Harbor leaves |
| Operating/technical depth | understands how systems work in reality, not only conceptually |
| Transfer | teaches, documents and leaves usable capability |
| Institutional contribution | methods, Atlas learning, quality improvements, mentoring |
| Commercial stewardship | scopes/prices responsibly, protects margin without distorting work |
| Integrity | conflicts, confidentiality, dissent, economics and authority handled correctly |

## 8. Ratings

The firm uses a five-level descriptive scale:

- **Exceptional** — repeatedly sets a firm-level standard.
- **Exceeds** — consistently performs above level.
- **Strong** — fully effective at level.
- **Developing** — meaningful gaps but credible path.
- **Unsatisfactory** — material persistent failure at level or integrity/quality issue.

Forced distributions are prohibited. A small firm should not invent poor performers because a curve demands it.

## 9. Promotion readiness

Promotion requires sustained next-level evidence, not one heroic matter. The candidate should demonstrate:

- broader problem ownership;
- more difficult judgment;
- greater client trust;
- ability to develop others;
- professional quality under pressure;
- increased independence;
- stronger transfer and implementation capability;
- commercial maturity appropriate to level.

## 10. Principal case

Promotion to Principal requires a formal case with:

- three representative matters;
- one example where the candidate changed their mind based on evidence;
- one difficult client or ethical judgment;
- client outcome/transfer evidence;
- reviewer feedback;
- economics/stewardship record;
- sponsor statement;
- panel interview.

No revenue threshold alone can create Principal eligibility.

## 11. Distinguished Principal

Distinguished Principal is rare and not a retirement title. The person should be someone the firm trusts with its hardest professional questions.

Evidence normally includes:

- recognized mastery in one or more domains;
- repeated consequential matter leadership;
- ability to challenge senior clients and Sable Harbor leaders;
- method/institutional contribution;
- demonstrated mentorship;
- professional reputation independent of management span.

## 12. Sales and origination credit

Origination is recognized but balanced against delivery and stewardship.

Compensation systems should distinguish:

- relationship origination;
- matter origination;
- professional delivery leadership;
- account stewardship;
- method/product contribution;
- people development.

A person cannot maximize compensation simply by selling work others must repair.

## 13. Utilization

Utilization is a capacity diagnostic, not a professional score.

The firm monitors chargeable/professional load to detect overstaffing, burnout and economics. It does not use utilization as the dominant promotion metric. Time invested in method, product feedback, quality, mentorship and learning may be valuable even when not client-billed.

## 14. Compensation architecture

Cash compensation consists of:

- market-competitive base salary;
- annual performance bonus;
- role/market premiums where scarce capability warrants;
- matter or retention awards where approved;
- carry only for qualifying former J2 professionals.

Operating-line veterans may receive cash compensation competitive with or exceeding carry-eligible peers at equivalent professional contribution. Carry eligibility is not a caste system.

## 15. Learning curriculum

Advisory maintains a professional curriculum covering:

- problem framing;
- evidence/provenance;
- economics and value measurement;
- client authority and governance;
- operational systems;
- Atlas Meridian;
- agent evaluation and controls;
- contracting/commercial judgment;
- conflict and independence;
- implementation/change;
- professional writing and executive communication;
- transfer/closeout.

Learning is grounded in sanitized matter cases and operating-business experience where rights permit.

## 16. Field exposure

Professionals working on physical operations should receive real field exposure proportionate to their role. The firm does not allow an elegant remote model to substitute indefinitely for understanding how the operation actually behaves.

## 17. Mobility

Advisory may pull from operating lines by name subject to transition discipline. Professionals may also return to operating lines where the move makes institutional sense, except where Orientation-derived line-authority restrictions apply.

Serving J2 personnel remain fully protected by the non-solicitation firewall.

## 18. Alumni and departures

Good departures are treated professionally. The firm preserves client continuity, IP/confidentiality, matter handoff and alumni relationships without pretending every departure is betrayal.

Carry treatment for eligible former J2 professionals follows `SH-ADV-005`.

## 19. Burnout and load

Matter intensity is monitored directly. Exceptional work does not require institutionalized exhaustion. Repeated overload, travel burden, crisis staffing and partner bottlenecks are treated as operating failures to solve, not rites of passage.

## 20. Talent test

A candidate from a mine, a data lab, J2, a military/intelligence career, a software product team or a Tier 1 consultancy should all be able to see a credible path to elite professional standing in Advisory based on what they can actually do—not on whether their résumé resembles a traditional consulting template.
