## 1. Quality objective

Advisory quality means that a reasonable sophisticated client, reviewer, regulator or internal professional can understand what was asked, what evidence was used, what assumptions were made, what Sable Harbor concluded, what remains uncertain, what was implemented, how success was measured, and what capability was transferred.

Quality is not synonymous with polish. A beautiful deliverable that hides weak evidence is low quality.

## 2. Non-negotiable professional duties

Every Advisory professional must:

- preserve source and evidence integrity;
- distinguish fact, inference, model output, estimate and professional judgment;
- disclose material uncertainty;
- avoid purchasing or selling conclusions;
- protect confidential information;
- respect client and Sable Harbor authority boundaries;
- escalate unsafe, unlawful or materially misleading work;
- correct material errors when discovered;
- avoid manipulating outcome economics;
- document enough of the matter to support review and future reconstruction.

## 3. Matter quality classification

Each matter receives a quality-risk tier at acceptance and is re-tiered when conditions change.

### Tier Q1 — Standard
Bounded scope, ordinary business consequence, mature method, limited variable economics.

### Tier Q2 — Elevated
Material financial/operating consequence, novel method, sensitive data, competitor conflict, material agent autonomy, or significant outcome fee.

### Tier Q3 — Consequential
Safety, regulated operation, major capital, public consequence, litigation/investigation proximity, high-value transaction, strategic enterprise decision, or unusually large variable economics.

### Tier Q4 — Exceptional
Potentially existential client consequence, severe safety/public risk, novel high-consequence AI/agent use, major adversarial dispute, or matter requiring CEO/reserved-authority escalation.

The tier determines review depth, staffing seniority, evidence standards, model validation, agent controls, and closure requirements.

## 4. Review standard

- Q1 may use supervisory review.
- Q2 requires an identified Independent Reviewer for material conclusions.
- Q3 requires formal independent review at problem framing, pre-intervention, and pre-close.
- Q4 requires a bespoke review plan approved in the Reserved Matter Room.

Review is documented in Atlas Meridian. Reviewers record challenge, disposition, unresolved dissent, and material limitations.

## 5. Evidence standard

A professional conclusion should be traceable to its supporting evidence and assumptions.

Minimum expectations:

- source identity and provenance;
- time relevance;
- known gaps;
- contradictory evidence;
- transformation/calculation lineage where material;
- model/agent version where used;
- professional adoption or rejection of machine outputs;
- client confirmations where relied upon;
- limitations on reproducibility.

Unsupported precision is treated as an error, not sophistication.

## 6. AI and agent quality

Agentic systems are professional tools, not autonomous bearers of responsibility.

For material agent use, the matter record captures:

- agent identity/version;
- model/runtime;
- instructions and changes;
- tool permissions;
- data entitlements;
- human owner;
- stop conditions;
- tests/evaluation results;
- material outputs adopted;
- material failures or hallucinations;
- transfer classification.

High-consequence actions require explicit human authority unless a separately approved controlled automation standard exists.

## 7. Model risk

Material models must be proportionately validated for:

- conceptual soundness;
- input integrity;
- sensitivity;
- calibration;
- temporal relevance;
- implementation errors;
- scenario limitations;
- misuse risk.

A model that is useful for scenario exploration may be unsuitable for a contractual outcome calculation. The matter record states the intended use.

## 8. Outcome integrity

No person whose compensation is materially affected by an outcome fee may unilaterally control baseline restatement, attribution, payout calculation or final certification.

The Value Office records:

- baseline;
- target;
- metric definition;
- data source;
- normalization;
- attribution;
- exclusions;
- client dependencies;
- measurement period;
- final result;
- limitations/disputes.

## 9. Client communications

Material communications should separate:

- what Sable Harbor knows;
- what Sable Harbor believes;
- what Sable Harbor recommends;
- what the client decided;
- what remains unresolved.

Client pressure to simplify uncertainty does not permit the team to erase it.

## 10. Error correction

When a material error is discovered:

1. contain unsafe or misleading downstream use;
2. notify the Matter Principal;
3. determine client notification obligations promptly;
4. correct the matter record without deleting historical state;
5. assess whether outcome economics or invoices were affected;
6. assess whether other matters or product components share the defect;
7. capture the lesson in the professional quality system.

Silent correction of a consequential conclusion is prohibited.

## 11. Complaints and concerns

Clients and professionals need a route to raise quality, ethics, confidentiality, retaliation, billing, conflict or safety concerns outside the immediate engagement chain.

Material complaints are logged, triaged, investigated and dispositioned. Retaliation for good-faith professional dissent is prohibited.

## 12. Independence of review

A reviewer must not be economically or organizationally dependent on the exact conclusion under review to a degree that would reasonably impair challenge. Where a senior relationship owner is conflicted, review is assigned outside that relationship.

## 13. Professional records

Advisory preserves sufficient matter records to support:

- client obligations;
- professional review;
- outcome verification;
- claims defense;
- institutional learning;
- regulatory/legal obligations;
- point-in-time reconstruction.

Retention duration and deletion are governed by the enterprise retention standard when finalized. Until then, no one destroys material matter evidence merely for convenience.

## 14. Security and privacy

Advisory uses least-necessary access consistent with useful professional work. Sensitive material is classified and entitlement-controlled. Client credentials, secrets and restricted source data are not placed into uncontrolled prompts, tools or personal workspaces.

## 15. Safety and operating authority

Advisory recommendations do not transfer operating authority unless a contract and enterprise authority instrument explicitly establish otherwise. Client operators retain immediate operational accountability. Sable Harbor professionals have stop/escalate obligations when their work could contribute to unsafe conditions.

## 16. Ethics in commercial conduct

Professionals may not:

- misrepresent credentials;
- conceal subcontractors or material tool dependencies where disclosure is required;
- inflate addressable value to raise fees;
- create artificial urgency;
- sell a capability the firm cannot responsibly staff;
- exploit confidential information from another client or J2;
- structure a supposedly independent analysis around a predetermined answer;
- hide known defects in transferred capability.

## 17. Post-matter review

Q2–Q4 matters receive a structured after-action review covering:

- outcome versus thesis;
- quality/review findings;
- what failed;
- what surprised the team;
- client transfer success;
- Atlas/agent incidents;
- pricing/attribution quality;
- reusable generalized learning;
- IP/confidentiality constraints on reuse.

Negative results are preserved. The firm does not rewrite a failed engagement into a success narrative merely because the client relationship continues.

## 18. Annual quality report

Professional quality leadership produces an annual internal report to the President and enterprise oversight summarizing:

- matter volumes by tier;
- review findings;
- complaints;
- material errors/restatements;
- agent/model incidents;
- outcome-fee disputes;
- confidentiality/conflict events;
- transfer failures;
- recurring root causes;
- remediation status.

The purpose is institutional learning, not scorekeeping theater.

## 19. Standard of proof

For consequential client work, Sable Harbor's reputation should rest on an observable discipline: evidence survives challenge, uncertainty remains visible, economics do not purchase conclusions, and the client can reconstruct how the firm arrived at the result.
