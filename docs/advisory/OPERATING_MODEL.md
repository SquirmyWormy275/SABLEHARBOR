## 1. Mandate

Sable Harbor Advisory exists to solve consequential client problems and leave behind more capability than it found.

It is not a staffing business, a slide-production business, a generic transformation office, or a commercialized version of J2. It combines human judgment, hard operating experience, Atlas Meridian, and problem-specific agent constellations to create measurable interventions and durable client capability.

The commercial promise is:

> **Bring Sable Harbor a consequential problem. We will establish what is actually happening, determine what can responsibly be known, intervene where we have accepted authority, prove what changed, and transfer the capability the client needs to continue without us.**

## 2. Three practices, one professional bench

### Intelligence Advisory

**Client question:** *What is actually going on, what should we believe, and what should we do about it?*

Typical matters include major operating anomalies, asset/network intelligence, acquisition and capital decision support, cross-system investigations, complex failure reconstruction, decision support under contradictory evidence, enterprise dependency investigations and high-consequence diligence.

The practice does not sell omniscience. It makes uncertainty legible and decision-useful.

### Intelligence Capability

**Client question:** *How do we build an institution that can know what matters before the answer becomes obvious?*

Typical matters include enterprise intelligence functions, question/collection architecture, challenge mechanisms, executive orientation, institutional memory/provenance, investigation workflows, education/formation and Atlas implementation for intelligence work.

Sable Harbor may use J2 experience as reference architecture. It does not copy internal J2 structures mechanically or transfer protected J2 records.

### Operational Excellence

**Client question:** *How do we run this hard physical system materially better?*

Typical matters include logistics/network optimization, mining/processing operations, maintenance/reliability, reclamation/remediation-function design, field operations, supply chain, operating-model redesign, process capability, field qualification, capital readiness and operational data/decision flows.

Operational Excellence is staffed heavily from people who have actually operated difficult systems. It transfers client-appropriate capability while protecting Sable Harbor-specific methods, recipes, data, trade secrets and operating-business IP.

## 5. The matter as unit of production

A **matter** is the smallest accountable commercial unit. It has:

- a client;
- consequential problem;
- defined scope and authority boundary;
- one Matter Principal;
- Acceptance Principal;
- quality tier;
- commercial class/value or capability model;
- Atlas Meridian workspace;
- staffing logic;
- evidence/provenance model;
- explicit outcome/capability criteria;
- transfer requirement or explicit managed-continuity exception;
- closure criteria.

Practices classify expertise. They do not own the client problem once the matter is formed.

## 6. Standard matter lifecycle

The controlled lifecycle is:

**Qualify → Frame → Mobilize → Investigate/Build → Intervene → Prove → Transfer/Close**

Detailed execution is controlled by `SH-ADV-011`.

### Qualify
Determine client/problem fit, conflicts, competence, authority, economics, data feasibility and whether Sable Harbor should accept the matter.

### Frame
Establish the actual question, decision owner, authority, scope, evidence perimeter, value/capability thesis, review plan and transfer promise.

### Mobilize
Assemble the smallest credible team, open the Atlas matter, establish source/request registers, agent/tool plan, security controls, work cadence and acceptance milestones.

### Investigate / Build
Acquire and organize evidence, test competing explanations, build the matter/capability model, preserve contradiction and uncertainty, and create the intervention/operating system required.

### Intervene
Act only within accepted authority. Intervention may mean operational change, model/process redesign, system configuration, field pilot, governance change, capability build or decision support.

### Prove
Measure or test what changed. Value Office certifies measurable-value outcomes; capability matters pass acceptance tests; Decision Matters meet decision-grade review; managed-continuity matters meet service obligations.

### Transfer / Close
Transfer promised client-owned/licensed capability, complete the Transfer Certificate, preserve required records, document limitations, establish support/exit and close the matter. A clean exit is a success state.

## 7. AI-native production model

Every suitable matter may instantiate a problem-specific **agent constellation** inside Atlas Meridian.

Possible functions include evidence intake/classification, entity resolution, temporal reconstruction, source querying, model building, scenario generation, calculation/reconciliation, contradiction detection, literature synthesis, workflow execution, test generation, provenance checking, transfer-document generation and monitoring.

Humans remain accountable for professional conclusions, accepted authority, consequential actions, unsafe conditions, client commitments and escalation.

## 8. Relationship to Atlas Meridian

Atlas Meridian is both a commercial client product and Advisory's professional substrate.

For Advisory clients it is the coherent digital front door for secure transfer, evidence, collaboration, matter records, outcome measurement, workflows, agent activity and capability transfer.

The Atlas product organization builds and operates the product. Advisory owns professional work. Product developers may support requirements, incidents and design partnerships but are not a shadow consulting bench.

Atlas commercial editions, tenant model, licensing, support, evaluation, export and security are controlled by `SH-ATL-017`.

## 9. Relationship to Foundry Field

Foundry Field remains Sable Harbor's flagship deployable operational product and a major source of represented operational terrain.

Advisory may recommend Foundry Field where appropriate, use licensed Foundry Field representation as input to Atlas-driven work, identify product needs through client work, and help clients exploit Foundry Field through properly scoped matters.

Foundry Field is not subordinated to Advisory, and Advisory is not merely an implementation wrapper for Foundry Field.

## 10. Relationship to J2

J2 and Advisory share institutional ancestry but different missions.

- J2 serves Sable Harbor's internal intelligence institution.
- Advisory serves clients.
- J2 may not be commercially tasked by Advisory.
- Advisory may not recruit or individually cultivate serving J2 personnel.
- Former J2 professionals may later join under explicit gates.
- J2 records, protected internal methods/access and raw Collection are not client deliverables or commercial source material.
- Advisory may adapt transferable lessons from Sable Harbor's intelligence method without presenting client capability as a literal copy of J2.

## 11. Pricing and economics

Advisory uses Measurable Value, Capability, Decision and Managed Continuity matter classes.

The normal minimum professional matter fee is $150,000. Measurable Value Matters use verified addressable-value bands and a 60% committed / 40% variable default, subject to controlled adjustment. Decision Matters are fixed price where contingent economics would impair independence. Atlas product charges remain separately visible.

Detailed pricing and contract mechanics are controlled by `SH-ADV-009`; the synthetic red team is `SH-ADV-010`.

## 12. Transfer and IP

Every material artifact is classified as client-owned, licensed Sable Harbor capability, reserved Sable Harbor professional IP, or restricted/nontransferable material.

A transferred capability must be genuinely operable by the client. Sable Harbor may sell continuing support, managed service and Atlas licensing, but it may not manufacture hidden dependency where transfer was promised.
