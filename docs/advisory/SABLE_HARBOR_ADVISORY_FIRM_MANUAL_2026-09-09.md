## Executive premise

Sable Harbor Advisory is designed as a modern professional institution built for an AI-native operating environment. Its fundamental economic and professional unit is the **matter**: a consequential client problem with a named decision owner, explicit authority boundary, measurable or testable outcome, accountable Matter Principal, independent quality controls, governed Atlas Meridian workspace, and defined transfer/exit state.

The firm does not sell a labor pyramid. It does not define quality by billable hours, slide volume, junior leverage, seats or token consumption. It sells judgment, intervention, measurable value, decision usefulness and durable client capability.

Its operating system combines:

- real operating experience;
- professional judgment;
- institutional intelligence methods;
- Atlas Meridian as product and professional substrate;
- problem-specific agent constellations;
- independent outcome verification;
- transfer rather than dependency;
- elite professional career paths independent of line-management status;
- strict separation from serving J2 personnel.

This manual is the integrated business artifact. Detailed standards control where more specific.

# I. FIRM IDENTITY

## 1. Business model

Sable Harbor Advisory operates as a business line of the existing controlling Sable Harbor contracting entity unless and until a later approved transaction establishes a separate legal entity.

The accepted operating name is **Sable Harbor Advisory** subject to ordinary external legal/trademark execution. The firm is led by a President. The individual appointment is a personnel decision and does not alter the operating model.

## 2. Three practices, one bench

### Intelligence Advisory
Establish what is happening, what can responsibly be believed, what matters, and what a consequential decision should take into account.

### Intelligence Capability
Build a client's durable intelligence/J2-like capability: question intake, collection, analysis, challenge, authority, governance, Atlas workflows, agents, training and institutional memory appropriate to that client.

### Operational Excellence
Improve physical and technical operations using Sable Harbor's real operating experience across logistics, mining, field systems, reclamation, industrial programs and adjacent environments without transferring unrelated Sable Harbor trade secrets.

The three practices are market/craft communities. They do not own separate staffing pyramids. The client problem draws from one common bench.

# II. PROFESSIONAL GOVERNANCE

## 3. Dual hierarchy

Advisory maintains equal-status management and professional ladders.

### Management
President → Managing Director → Director → Manager.

Management owns business P&L, workforce, budget, capacity and operating commitments.

### Professional
Distinguished Principal → Senior Principal → Principal → Senior Professional → Professional → Associate Professional.

The professional ladder owns matter judgment, quality, method, review, reputation, mentorship and difficult client problem leadership.

A Distinguished Principal may be one of the highest-paid people in Advisory without commanding a large organization.

## 4. Former Orientation Officers

Former Orientation Officers can rise to the highest professional level, lead matters, own professional judgment, review, mentor and receive carry. They do not regain prohibited line authority through title engineering, de facto headcount control or equivalent management rights.

## 5. Standard matter roles

Every consequential matter assigns:

- Relationship Principal;
- Matter Principal;
- Acceptance Principal;
- Independent Reviewer as required;
- Value Office Lead where outcome economics apply;
- Transfer Owner;
- Atlas Matter Steward.

Professional authority, commercial origination and management authority are intentionally separable.

# III. CLIENT AND MATTER ACCEPTANCE

## 6. Client selection

Advisory accepts work only when it can perform competently, independently, lawfully, securely and without compromising another client, a Sable Harbor operating business or J2.

Acceptance decisions are categorized:

- Accept;
- Accept with Conditions;
- Hold;
- Decline.

## 7. Conflict posture

Competitor work can be accepted only when confidentiality, staffing, Atlas entitlements, professional independence and Sable Harbor operating-business boundaries remain credible.

J2 is never treated as a commercial information source. Protected J2 access, raw Collection, source identities, Orientation records and restricted institutional memory are outside the Advisory value proposition.

## 8. Conclusion integrity

Clients may purchase work, judgment, analysis, implementation and measurable outcomes. They may not purchase a preferred conclusion.

Where advocacy is the actual assignment, it must be labeled honestly rather than disguised as independent analysis.

# IV. MATTER LIFECYCLE

## 9. Seven-stage delivery model

**Qualify → Frame → Mobilize → Investigate/Build → Intervene → Prove → Transfer/Close**

The model loops when evidence changes the problem. It is not a rigid waterfall.

## 10. Matter charter

The charter defines:

- consequential question;
- client decision owner;
- outcome/capability state;
- Sable Harbor authority/non-authority;
- scope/exclusions;
- evidence/data perimeter;
- hypotheses and uncertainties;
- quality tier;
- review plan;
- economics;
- transfer obligation;
- stop/redesign triggers.

## 11. Delivery style

The default client interaction is evidence-based working sessions, operating interventions, prototypes and decision preparation. Presentations are used when they are the right instrument, not as the definition of progress.

## 12. Prove before close

Measurable Value Matters require Value Office certification. Capability Matters require acceptance tests. Decision Matters require decision-grade professional review. Managed Continuity matters require service-level/outcome evidence.

A matter is not complete because a final presentation occurred.

# V. PRICING AND ECONOMICS

## 13. Matter classes

1. Measurable Value Matter.
2. Capability Matter.
3. Decision Matter.
4. Managed Continuity Matter.

## 14. Measurable-value bands

| Verified addressable value | Target total fee |
|---:|---:|
| Under $5M | 10–15% |
| $5M–$25M | 7–12% |
| $25M–$100M | 4–8% |
| $100M–$500M | 2–4% |
| Over $500M | 0.5–2.0% |

The normal minimum professional matter fee is $150,000.

## 15. 60/40 default

The working default for Measurable Value Matters is 60% committed / 40% variable, with flexibility between 40/60 and 80/20 when attribution, client dependency or risk requires.

Default variable curve:

- below 70% of target: 0%;
- 70%: 25%;
- 85%: 60%;
- 100%: 100%;
- 120%+: up to 125%, subject to contract cap.

## 16. Value Office

The Value Office independently certifies baseline, normalization, target, attribution, client dependencies, measurement period, outcome and variable-fee calculation. A Matter Principal cannot grade their own outcome fee.

## 17. Decision independence

Decision Matters are fixed priced when a contingent fee would create an incentive to recommend a particular transaction or conclusion.

## 18. Financial management

Advisory tracks matter P&L, backlog, reserves, collections, scarce-capability capacity, quality cost, Atlas dependencies and carry economics internally. The client-facing price remains value/capability based.

# VI. CARRY AND TALENT

## 19. Carry purpose

Carry is a long-duration economic participation mechanism available only to qualifying former J2 professionals. It creates a path to exceptional wealth without creating management authority.

## 20. Eligibility gates

- **Orientation:** full commissioned tour + one qualifying fellowship.
- **Judgment:** five years as Judgment Officer + two years actual operating-business service; JAG does not substitute.
- **Contact:** four years substantive Contact leadership with people and collection outcome accountability.
- **Education:** five years in Education + two years substantive ownership of formation/curriculum/fellowship/doctrine/evaluation systems.
- **J2 Headquarters:** no independent eligibility route; must qualify through an underlying professional gate.

No serving J2 person may be recruited, courted, pre-negotiated, pre-cleared or individually discussed for carry.

## 21. Carry mechanics

Default plan:

- contractual phantom carry;
- 20% of Excess Advisory Economic Profit after losses, reserves and hurdle;
- hurdle = greater of 10% of allocated operating capital or enterprise cost-of-capital charge;
- 10,000 notional units;
- five-year vesting, 20% annually;
- annual Finance-certified distribution;
- rolling holdback;
- good/bad leaver treatment;
- clawback/malus;
- no automatic board, vote, line authority or IP rights;
- no personal fixed percentage of a matter outcome fee.

## 22. External and operating-line talent

Carry restriction does not make outsiders or operators second class. Operating-line veterans are a premium talent category and may be among the highest-paid non-carry professionals. External hires can reach any professional level based on demonstrated capability.

## 23. Early-career model

Associates are apprenticed into judgment, evidence, field reality, Atlas/agents, implementation and transfer—not into repetitive formatting work machines can already perform.

# VII. QUALITY, RISK AND ETHICS

## 24. Q1–Q4 quality tiers

Matters are classified from standard to exceptional consequence. The tier drives review depth, seniority, model/agent controls and closure requirements.

## 25. Evidence discipline

Material conclusions preserve source, provenance, time relevance, gaps, contradictions, transformation lineage, model/agent version and professional adoption/rejection of machine output.

## 26. AI/agent control

Agents have identity, version, model/runtime, data/action scope, human owner, stop conditions, evaluations, monitoring, change history and transfer class. Human professional accountability remains explicit.

## 27. Error correction

Material errors are contained, corrected transparently, assessed for client/outcome impact, preserved historically and fed into institutional learning.

## 28. Professional dissent

Good-faith challenge is protected. Unresolved material dissent can remain in the matter record. Artificial consensus is not a quality objective.

# VIII. ATLAS MERIDIAN

## 29. Product identity

Atlas Meridian is:

- a commercial enterprise product;
- Advisory's client front door;
- the professional matter system;
- the environment for governed agent constellations and transferable client capability.

The Atlas team is a product organization, not an Advisory bench.

## 30. Two planes

### Professional plane
Sable Harbor-controlled orchestration, methods, cross-matter learning, internal evaluations, quality tools and protected firm capability.

### Client plane
Client evidence, matter records, decisions, workflows, agents, tests, runbooks, outcome data and licensed product capability.

The 70/30 concept is a heuristic about the boundary, not ownership math.

## 31. Editions

- Atlas Meridian Core.
- Atlas Meridian Enterprise.
- Atlas Meridian Professional.
- Atlas Meridian Managed.

Licensing is institutional/tenant based, not primarily per seat or token.

Operating calibration:

- Core: $150K–$350K annual;
- Enterprise: $350K–$1.25M annual;
- Managed: Enterprise + managed service.

## 32. Transferable agents

Client-transferred agents/workflows require identity, version, instructions, tools, model dependency, data/action entitlements, owner, stop conditions, evaluations, monitoring, transfer class and rollback.

No transferred package may silently depend on Professional-plane capability the client does not license.

## 33. Export and exit

Clients can obtain promised client-owned data, matter records and transferred capability in documented forms. Atlas should retain clients because it is valuable, not because Sable Harbor withholds their operating artifacts.

# IX. IP AND METHOD TRANSFER

## 34. Four classes

1. Client-owned.
2. Licensed Sable Harbor capability.
3. Reserved Sable Harbor professional IP.
4. Restricted/nontransferable material.

## 35. Operational Excellence boundary

Advisory can teach a client how to build reclamation, logistics, maintenance, field or other operating capability while withholding unrelated Sable Harbor mine plans, proprietary technical details, internal vendor economics or other secrets that are not necessary to transfer the competence.

The client gets the capability, not a photocopy of Sable Harbor's private operating playbook.

## 36. No dependency theater

If the engagement promises transfer, Sable Harbor must provide the instructions, tests, artifacts, ownership and runbooks required for the client to operate. Continuing managed service is valid only when it is explicit rather than hidden dependency.

---

# X. KNOWLEDGE AND INSTITUTIONAL LEARNING

## 37. Case learning

After-action records preserve what the team believed, what changed, what failed, the outcome, transfer result, pricing lesson, model/agent lesson and reuse constraints.

## 38. Cross-client learning

Atlas Professional may generalize lessons only with rights/provenance controls. Client matter memory and generalized firm knowledge remain distinct.

## 39. J2 and operating-business boundaries

Professional skills can transfer. Protected J2 content and operating-business trade secrets do not become Advisory knowledge merely because a professional encountered them.

---

# XI. MARKET AND CLIENT PORTFOLIO

## 40. Target market

Advisory prioritizes institutions with real operational/technical complexity, fragmented evidence, consequential decisions and a willingness to engage with uncomfortable truth.

## 41. Pursuit discipline

The firm kills weak pursuits early. A major pursuit asks whether the problem is consequential, Sable Harbor is distinctive, the buyer has authority, the client can support implementation, economics are credible, conflicts are manageable and capacity exists.

## 42. Credentials and thought leadership

Credentials are evidence of actual problems/outcomes where disclosure is permitted. Synthetic cases remain labeled synthetic. Thought leadership is subject to evidence and confidentiality discipline.

# XII. MANAGEMENT SYSTEM

## 43. Monthly scorecard

Executive management reviews:

- backlog and pipeline;
- variable-fee exposure;
- client concentration;
- matter economics and DSO;
- Q1–Q4 portfolio;
- transfer success/failure;
- quality findings and complaints;
- model/agent incidents;
- scarce-capability load;
- hiring/attrition;
- Atlas product flywheel;
- carry-pool forecast.

## 44. Annual plan

The annual plan includes market thesis, practice priorities, financial scenarios, Atlas dependencies, talent/succession, operating-line recruiting, J2-firewall assurance, quality/risk priorities, internal investment and carry forecast.

## 45. Institutional standard

A Tier 1 professional firm is not defined by headcount, expensive offices, a partnership vocabulary or slide polish. It is defined by whether clients and professionals can see a coherent institution behind every engagement.

Sable Harbor Advisory's standard is therefore:

- clear authority;
- defensible judgment;
- independent review;
- explicit economics;
- disciplined conflicts;
- protected client information;
- real operating expertise;
- governed AI/agents;
- durable capability transfer;
- institutional memory with provenance;
- professional careers that reward mastery rather than only management span;
- a product platform strong enough to outlast the engagement.

That is the firm.
