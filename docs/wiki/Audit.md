# Audit practice

[Wiki home](Home.md) · [All files](Files.md) · [Internal Audit](departments/internal-audit.md) · [Risk & Compliance](departments/risk-compliance.md)

The archive offers accounting cases, control-testing examples and an interactive audit workspace. Choose a document exercise to work through the records at your own pace, or use the workspace to practice requesting evidence, interviewing company contacts, selecting samples and preparing workpapers.

## Choose an exercise

| Work | Start with | What you produce |
|---|---|---|
| Invoice and revenue testing | [Invoice exercise](../reader/exercises/INVOICE.md) | A trace from contract and invoice to the journal, with reconciliation notes. |
| Acquisition accounting | [Acquisition exercise](../reader/exercises/ACQUISITION.md) | A reconciliation of consideration and accounting support. |
| Contract obligations | [Contract exercise](../reader/exercises/CONTRACTS.md) | A review of terms, approvals and the available execution evidence. |
| Control testing | [Three CCF procedures](../../enterprise/ccf/PROCEDURES.md) | Test results and a record of exceptions, remediation and independent retesting. |
| Assessment planning | [CCF assessment workbench](../../enterprise/ccf/assurance/README.md) | Evidence requests, test workpapers and a comparison of selected framework requirements. |
| An audit engagement | [Audit workspace guide](../audit-suite/LOCAL_WORKSPACE.md) | Evidence requests, collected originals, samples, notes, findings and reviewed workpapers. |

The exercises identify their records and periods. Keep those together when choosing samples or reconciling figures. The assessment workbench includes reference test plans that have yet to be performed; the completed synthetic examples have their own results.

## Use the audit workspace

The delivered local workspace has separate CLEAN and MESSY company branches. On the configured workstation, run `sableharbor-workspace start`, wait for `READY`, then open CLEAN at `http://127.0.0.1:8782` or MESSY at `http://127.0.0.1:8783`. The [local guide](../audit-suite/LOCAL_WORKSPACE.md) describes access and the preserved histories. A public checkout needs the separately supplied private company sources and operator setup in the [application README](../../audit_suite_web/README.md).

Begin with the engagement scope and controls. Create an evidence request, issue it to the relevant owner, then browse the authorized company source records and collect the available original. Use the retained version when inspecting or downloading evidence. From there, select a population and samples, record your procedure, discuss questions with the responsible contact and prepare your findings and workpapers.

Learners work with their authorized company records. Instructors manage scope and assessments; authorized reviewers have a separate review role. Instructor Key material remains restricted to its authorized audience. Local model conversations and voice are optional configured services; generated replies support the exercise and require the reader’s judgment.

## Follow the evidence

An audit conclusion needs a defined scope, period, procedure and population, together with the evidence inspected and the results obtained. The workspace retains originals, versions and dated work so a reviewer can follow those choices. Earlier exceptions remain in the history when remediation or a later test is recorded.

The [accounting evidence packages](../finance/evidence/coverage/README.md) provide transaction support. The [reference source inventory](../../enterprise/ccf/assurance/reference_data/README.md) identifies the framework sources used for assessment preparation. The [runtime assurance guide](../../enterprise/runtime/docs/ASSURANCE_SCOPE.md) describes the systems and recovery evidence covered by its examples.

These are fictional training cases. Their results apply to the records and conditions tested; they do not issue a professional assurance opinion or certification.

## Audit responsibilities

[Internal Audit](departments/internal-audit.md) independently evaluates company governance and controls under the [Audit & Compliance Committee charter](../governance/committees/AUDIT_AND_COMPLIANCE_COMMITTEE_CHARTER.md). [Risk & Compliance](departments/risk-compliance.md) coordinates the framework, while management owns controls and remediation. [Finance](departments/finance.md) keeps the accounting records used in financial review.
