# Procurement and vendor support

Procurement and vendor support helps teams source goods and services, manage suppliers and coordinate technology and supply-chain dependencies. The service register connects a need with the planned delivery arrangement and relevant contract record.

<a id="reading-guide"></a>
<a id="what-remains-unknown"></a>

## Purchasing and supplier relationships

The records distinguish a cost estimate, selected provider, executed agreement and installed service. Following those stages shows which commitments have been made and what still needs to happen before a service can be used.
