Safety and environmental governance sets shared requirements where enterprise oversight is needed. Mine, railway, laboratory and recovery teams apply them in their own technical setting and remain responsible for the work at their sites.

<a id="what-remains-unknown"></a>

## Safety and environmental responsibilities

The business records describe operating permissions, qualifications and engineering requirements. Those records are the starting point for checking whether a particular activity is ready to proceed safely.
