# Corporate Secretary

<img src="../../../assets/brand/logos/sable-harbor__reverse-horizontal.png" alt="Sable Harbor approved identity" width="360">

[Wiki home](../Home.md) · [Departments](README.md) · [All files](../Files.md)

The Corporate Secretary arranges Board business, maintains formal corporate records and coordinates entity administration. The office also brings together the governance work for continuity and crisis response: who must be informed, who can act and how decisions are recorded.

<a id="reading-guide"></a>
<a id="what-remains-unknown"></a>

## Board work and corporate records

Recovery plans and technical test results support that work. A record of a completed recovery exercise is needed to show what was restored, by whom and under which conditions.
