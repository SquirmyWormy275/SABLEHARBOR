Technology Services runs employee technology, shared platforms, developer systems, compute infrastructure and reliability services. Product engineering belongs to the businesses that build the products. Shared identity, security and delegated authority support the work across both.

## Employee technology and shared infrastructure

The runtime records describe the accepted design and its implementation dependencies. Supplier acceptance, installed services and production qualification are documented stages of putting that design into operation.
