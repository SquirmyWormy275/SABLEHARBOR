# Finance

<img src="../../../assets/brand/logos/sable-harbor__reverse-horizontal.png" alt="Sable Harbor approved identity" width="360">

[Wiki home](../Home.md) · [Departments](README.md) · [All files](../Files.md)

Finance keeps the company’s economic records, prepares financial reporting, manages treasury and maintains common accounting standards. It also analyzes capital proposals. Business leaders make operating decisions within their authority; Finance supplies the accounts and financial analysis needed to evaluate them.

<a id="reading-guide"></a>
<a id="what-remains-unknown"></a>

## Accounts, cash and capital

The exercises follow an economic event through supporting documents, journals and reconciliations. Historical reconstructions and conditional forecasts identify their periods and assumptions. The [parent-tax workpaper](../../finance/evidence/company-closeout/ADOPTED_PARENT_TAX.md) records the accepted fictional tax treatment; external filings require their own evidence.
