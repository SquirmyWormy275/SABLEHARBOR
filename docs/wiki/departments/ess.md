# Enterprise Support Services

<img src="../../../assets/brand/logos/sable-harbor__reverse-horizontal.png" alt="Sable Harbor approved identity" width="360">

[Wiki home](../Home.md) · [Departments](README.md) · [All files](../Files.md)

Enterprise Support Services coordinates the administration, budgets, staffing support and facilities used by the corporate support functions. Finance, Legal, Technology and the other professional officers retain responsibility for their work. Internal Audit has its own functional accountability, and J2 sits outside ESS.

<a id="reading-guide"></a>
<a id="what-remains-unknown"></a>

## Shared services

The service plans describe how support is organized. Supplier selection, contracts and installed services are recorded separately, so a budget assumption can be followed through to the service it is meant to fund.
