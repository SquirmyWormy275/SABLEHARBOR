# Judgment

[Wiki home](../Home.md) · [Departments](README.md) · [All files](../Files.md)

Judgment Officers investigate defined problems and test possible explanations against evidence. Their living Problem Books preserve the question, sources, alternatives, reasoning and dissent. Judgment Watch screens incoming material and connects it with relevant work; the assigned officer owns the investigation.

## Investigating a company problem

Investigations inform decisions by the responsible executive or operating owner. The personnel records identify appointments, and source permissions govern which records an officer can inspect.
