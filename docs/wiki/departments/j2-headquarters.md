# J2 Headquarters

<img src="../../../assets/brand/logos/j2__primary-horizontal.png" alt="J2 approved identity" width="240">

[Wiki home](../Home.md) · [Departments](README.md) · [All files](../Files.md)

J2 Headquarters coordinates intelligence priorities, professional standards, staffing and administrative support. The Head of J2 reports administratively to the CEO and has protected access to the Board. The individual arms retain responsibility for their collection, investigation, briefing, field and teaching work.

## Coordinating J2

Jonathan Goldstryker leads J2, Amanda Chenahot is Deputy Head, and Miriam Solano is Chief of Staff. Miriam joined Sable Harbor in 2019. The headquarters establishment provides 28 billets; the [leadership guide](../../j2/README.md#current-leadership) introduces the current leaders and links their professional profiles and appointment records.
