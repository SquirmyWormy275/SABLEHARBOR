# People & Culture

People & Culture supports employees from recruitment through their working lives: pay and benefits, employee relations, personnel records, mobility and career development. Its service approach calls for recurring administrative problems to be resolved so people can concentrate on their work.

<a id="reading-guide"></a>
<a id="what-remains-unknown"></a>

## Joining, working and developing

It administers required learning, while subject owners set the requirements and J2 Education designs the teaching. The [people charts](../../organization/README.md) show recorded names, roles and joining years. Staffing plans describe authorized positions and planning assumptions. Appointment records show changes in someone’s responsibilities over time.
