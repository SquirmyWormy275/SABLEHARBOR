Risk & Compliance helps management identify exposure, coordinate policies and maintain the Common Controls Framework. Operating managers own their risks, controls and remediation. Internal Audit independently examines that work.

<a id="reading-guide"></a>
<a id="what-remains-unknown"></a>

## Risks, policies and controls

The [assessment workbench](../../../enterprise/ccf/assurance/README.md) connects requirements with proposed controls, evidence requests and test plans. Its reference preparation includes unfinished procedures and review work. Assessment conclusions depend on the evidence and tests completed for the selected scope and period.
