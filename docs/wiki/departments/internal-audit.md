Internal Audit examines governance, controls and operations independently of the managers responsible for them. It sets its audit scope, methods, findings and communications under its charter and the oversight of the Audit & Compliance Committee.

<a id="reading-guide"></a>
<a id="what-remains-unknown"></a>

## Independent review

An audit starts with a question, a period and a population to test. Workpapers record the procedure, selected evidence, results and review. Management remains responsible for its controls and remediation. The [audit practice guide](../Audit.md) brings together the training workspace, accounting cases and assessment tools.
