# Corporate History

Sable Harbor's accepted history begins with Blackridge as the upstream wound, continues through services-led formation in Sacramento in 2016, the Original Eight, Foundry and Foundry Field, the Evalon/Emberline/Willow lineage, Atlas Meridian, Pale Sun and Red Wash, Project Cradle, ARU/BS&T, and the emerging Advisory line.

The controlling account is the [Corporate Lore Canon v0.2](https://github.com/SquirmyWormy275/SABLEHARBOR/blob/main/docs/canon/SABLE_HARBOR_CORPORATE_LORE_CANON_v0.2.md). The [organizational lineage](https://github.com/SquirmyWormy275/SABLEHARBOR/blob/main/docs/organization/ORGANIZATIONAL_LINEAGE_2015_2026.md) provides a publication-oriented chronology.

Exact legal entities, transactions, reporting lines, headcount, reserves, routes, physical assets, and quantitative history remain open or scenario-controlled unless a controlling source explicitly locks them.
