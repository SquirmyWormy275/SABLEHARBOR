# Company history and The Crossing

Sable Harbor began as a services-led mining-data and operating-consulting business. Its history follows the move to reusable software and, later, a broader portfolio of industrial and professional work. The [organizational history](../../organization/ORGANIZATIONAL_LINEAGE_2015_2026_v0.3.1.md) brings that development together.

| Period | What changed | Read the evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 2016–2018 | Incorporated in Sacramento as a services-led mining-data and operating-consulting business; the reusable Foundry substrate begins in 2018. | [Corporate lore, sections 4 and 6](../../canon/SABLE_HARBOR_CORPORATE_LORE_CANON_v0.3.1.md) |
| 2019 | Customers seek continuing access to representations developed in engagements, while deployments remain highly dependent on specialists. | Lore section 6.5 |
| 2020–2021 | COVID travel restrictions expose that dependency. The Crossing produces a remotely deployable workflow; Foundry Field becomes the commercial identity and is standardized in 2021. | Lore sections 6.6–6.7; [Foundry/Foundry Field distinction](../../organization/FOUNDRY_AND_FOUNDRY_FIELD.md) |
| 2020–2022 | Klein's experimental outpost evolves into Willow; Emberline is a distinct coal-facing program. | [Research history](Research-History.md), [controlling Klein decision](../../canon/KLEIN_NAME_AND_IDENTITY_2026-09-07.md) |
| 2021–2024 | Financing, Board membership and the CEO/Chair separation develop over time. | [Board evolution](../../governance/BOARD_EVOLUTION_2016_2026.md), [current Board/capital record](../../governance/BOARD_AND_CAPITAL_GOVERNANCE_v1.0.1.md) |
| 2025–2026 | Cradle's reference work and the current business/management structure acquire their recorded scope. | [Cradle closeout](../../canon/CRADLE_CLOSEOUT_2026-09-06.md), [headquarters closeout](../../canon/CORPORATE_HEADQUARTERS_CLOSEOUT_2026-09-03.md) |

The archive's retrospective narrative is not a contemporaneous incident log or an audited historical financial series.
