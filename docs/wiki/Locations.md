# Locations and facilities

[Wiki home](Home.md) · [Businesses](businesses/README.md) · [Maps and floor plans](../../geospatial/maps/facilities/ARTIFACT_INDEX.md)

Sable Harbor's main operating base is Sacramento. Its businesses also use regional offices, industrial facilities, customer sites and external hosting providers. This directory describes the current setting; individual drawings show their own edition and whether they are studies, proposals or occupied accommodation.

## Offices and working bases

| Place | Work based there |
|---|---|
| Sacramento, California | Corporate leadership, shared services and J2 headquarters. Foundry, Atlas Meridian and Advisory use shared accommodation. |
| Reno, Nevada | Shared office and engineering accommodation for Foundry and customer-delivery work. This office is separate from the Tahoe-Reno hosting arrangement. |
| Elko, Nevada | A shared field base for mine-customer work, equipment staging and temporary project attendance. |
| Tucson, Arizona | Shared engineering and analysis accommodation for geology, mining science and Atlas Meridian. |
| Sacramento metropolitan region | J2 Education's residential learning and conference campus, separate from the headquarters office floor. |

Foundry Field works across Reno, Sacramento and customer sites. Atlas Meridian works across Tucson, Sacramento and deployed engagements. Neither needs a separate corporate campus to have an established working location.

## Industrial and research operations

| Business or facility | Location and purpose |
|---|---|
| [Pale Sun / Red Wash](businesses/Pale-Sun-Red-Wash.md) | The Red Wash uranium mine is in the Great Divide Basin / Red Desert, north of Wamsutter in Sweetwater County, Wyoming. This is Pale Sun's mining operation. |
| [American Resource Utility / BS&T](businesses/American-Resource-Utility.md) | Taylor, Wamsutter and Rawlins, Wyoming, with the individual terminals, warehouses and railway facilities shown in the industrial maps. |
| [Willow / the Fort](businesses/Willow.md) | Hazelwood, Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania. The research compound includes the Big Shed, Small Shed, White Shed and outdoor Museum. |
| [Cradle / Bedford](businesses/Cradle.md) | The Fairmont / White Hall area of West Virginia. Bedford supports research, development, assembly and intermediate upgrading on a selected 15.27-acre fictional redevelopment site. |
| Cradle's external hosts | Kelly Gang Mining hosts Stream 17 in Tasmania; Demotte Reclamation Services hosts the separate mine-water recovery deployment in north-central West Virginia. These are host operations, not Sable Harbor-owned mines or treatment companies. |

Red Wash is road-served; it has no mine railway spur. Uranium transport remains subject to its custody and release requirements. The former Carbon County siting is historical, not the current mine location.

## Hosting and construction

Switch Tahoe-Reno is the selected primary hosting provider; IDACORE Boise is the selected independent recovery provider. Selection is not evidence that contracts were executed or the services went live.

The owned Northern Nevada data-center project is at acquired-land and preconstruction stage. Proposed building plans do not depict a completed or commissioned facility. See the [runtime estate guide](../../enterprise/runtime/README.md) for the implementation record.
