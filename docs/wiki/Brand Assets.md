# Brand Assets and Collateral

The repository stores one production logo or lockup per file, with outlined SVG as source of truth and matched PNG renderings. The collateral suite includes corporate letterheads, memorandum, report, presentation, standards, provenance, validation, and packaged distributions.

- [Brand system](https://github.com/SquirmyWormy275/SABLEHARBOR/blob/main/assets/brand/README.md)
- [Logo directory](https://github.com/SquirmyWormy275/SABLEHARBOR/tree/main/assets/brand/logos)
- [Collateral directory](https://github.com/SquirmyWormy275/SABLEHARBOR/tree/main/assets/brand/collateral)
- [Brand standards](https://github.com/SquirmyWormy275/SABLEHARBOR/blob/main/assets/brand/BRAND_STANDARDS.md)
- [Font provenance](https://github.com/SquirmyWormy275/SABLEHARBOR/blob/main/assets/brand/FONT_PROVENANCE.md)

Corporate stationery is available for adaptation. Unit-specific letterheads are not accepted until separately generated, proofed, registered, and checksummed.
