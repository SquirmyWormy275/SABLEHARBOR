# Start here

[Wiki home](Home.md) · [Businesses](businesses/README.md) · [Departments](departments/README.md) · [Document library](Library.md)

Sable Harbor is a fictional company whose records you can explore and work with. Get to know a business, follow the documents behind a decision, or try an accounting or controls exercise. You do not need to install anything to read the Wiki.

## Explore the company

Choose a [business](businesses/README.md) that interests you. Its introduction explains what it does; its reading guide leads to the detailed records about people, finances, and places. The [department directory](departments/README.md) covers the corporate teams that support and review that work.

For the longer story, read [company history](subjects/History.md), [the people](subjects/People.md), and [the research lineage](subjects/Research-History.md). Former projects, external hosts, and separate cases have their own pages so they are not mistaken for current Sable Harbor businesses.

## Complete a practical exercise

| Exercise | What you work through | Starting point |
|---|---|---|
| Trace an invoice | Connect an invoice to its contract terms, dated movements, and journal entries. | [Invoice exercise](../reader/exercises/INVOICE.md) |
| Review an acquisition | Reconcile the purchase consideration and accounting records covered by the exercise, noting any missing support. | [Acquisition exercise](../reader/exercises/ACQUISITION.md) |
| Review contract obligations | Read the agreement, identify its obligations, and check the available evidence of what was done. | [Contract exercise](../reader/exercises/CONTRACTS.md) |
| Examine a control | Work through the example records, test procedure, result, and independent retest. | [CCF procedures](../../enterprise/ccf/PROCEDURES.md) |

Each guide names the records, explains the steps, and describes what to produce. Use files for the same release, scenario, unit, and period. Do not mix snapshots just to make a reconciliation work. When a document is missing, say so in your conclusion.

## Find the supporting record

The [reading room](https://github.com/SquirmyWormy275/SABLEHARBOR/wiki/Reading) groups full source documents by topic. Use the [document library](Library.md) to find PDFs, workbooks, and other formats too. Each reading edition keeps the original document's date, status, and qualifications, and links to the exact repository version used for publication.

Use **On this page** to jump through a long article and **Related reading** to explore connected subjects. Related-reading links are suggestions for what to read next, not statements about reporting lines or ownership.

## Know what a record establishes

Before drawing a conclusion, check what you are looking at: a proposal, an accepted company decision, a record of work performed, or a model. The [short glossary](Glossary.md) explains the terminology. Each article's **What remains unknown** section identifies questions the available records cannot answer.

The [open questions register](Open-Questions.md) brings together missing evidence and unresolved decisions. When records disagree, the [source and format guide](../reader/SOURCES_AND_FORMATS.md) explains which one takes precedence.

## Download and reproduce

Read Markdown in the browser. Open PDFs for formatted publications and download spreadsheets to work with the calculations. The [release guides](../reader/USE_CASES.md#downloads-and-tools) link to complete exercise packages.

To build the models or query the data, follow the [contribution guide](../../CONTRIBUTING.md). You do not need to learn the build tools or publication process just to explore the company.
