# Emberline

**Classification:** historical coal-focused business line  
**Operational period:** active through 2025; enduring work absorbed in 2026  
**Dossier state:** archival review candidate

Emberline was the commercial, customer, and field-experimentation coal program associated with—but not identical to—Evalon's advanced-engineering outpost. Its enduring work is absorbed into Foundry, Willow, field operations, and Advisory rather than preserved as a standalone 2026 division.

## Status and scope

- Canon: historical role, relation to Evalon, and absorption are locked.
- Emberline must not appear as a current legal entity or operating business line.
- Standalone historical database, asset/project inventory, financial archive, and letterhead package: **NOT MATERIALIZED**.
