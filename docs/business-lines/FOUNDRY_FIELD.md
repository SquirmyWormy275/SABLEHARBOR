## Business and output

Sable Harbor’s flagship deployable product, built on the Foundry substrate. Foundry preserves identity, meaning, provenance and authority in context; Foundry Field makes those foundations useful in operating work.

Deployable applications for operating, maintenance, reconciliation and exception workflows. Application names remain working descriptions where the source has not locked a commercial name. Product subscriptions, deployment acceptance and ongoing support are distinct obligations.

## Operating lifecycle

Qualify customer and information rights → agree scope and acceptance → configure and validate → deploy under the customer’s operating authority → support, renew, expand or retire. A successful demonstration does not authorize production.
