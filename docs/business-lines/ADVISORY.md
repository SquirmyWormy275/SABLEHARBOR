## Business and output

Sable Harbor Advisory operates three named practices on one common bench: Intelligence Advisory, Intelligence Capability and Operational Excellence. It commercializes approved capability and methods, including transferable client workflows and agent constellations. It sits adjacent to J2 and has its own commercial delivery accountability. It is not the internal Junction Advisory Group.

Outcome-scoped diagnosis, workflow/capability build, implementation and transfer. The unit of production is the matter. Its arc is Qualify, Frame, Mobilize, Investigate / Build, Intervene, Prove, Transfer / Close, with iteration where evidence changes the problem. Atlas Meridian is the client work portal and professional delivery system. Product-to-Advisory and Advisory-to-product referrals are tracked as commercial origins, not additional businesses.

## Operating lifecycle

Qualify client and conflicts → specify outcome, acceptance and information rights → authorize engagement and staff capacity → perform/evidence milestones → obtain independent review and client acceptance where applicable → certify variable economics through the Value Office → invoice earned consideration → transfer capability and close support obligations.

## Organization and authority

Advisory uses dual equal-status management and professional ladders. Distinguished Principal is the highest non-line professional rank. Practice leadership develops craft, reputation and market presence across one common bench; practice labels do not create staffing silos.

Use role/skill capacity, assignment fractions, approved internal time and contractor terms. The President role is settled; the individual appointment remains pending. Serving J2 personnel cannot enter an Advisory recruiting/application pipeline or discuss future Advisory roles or economics. Former qualifying J2 service may establish carry eligibility, never an automatic allocation. Orientation’s permanent anti-line-authority restrictions remain controlling.

J2 owns its method and internal judgments; Advisory owns commercial delivery. Only approved doctrine moves outward and appropriately cleared lessons return inward. Commercial pressure cannot determine J2 judgments or turn client fee approval into internal decision authority.

## Financial architecture

Bill outcomes and accepted milestones, not hours. Time measures delivery cost, utilization, unbilled effort and capacity. Record change orders, rejected acceptance, rework, discounts, milestone receivables, collections and margin by engagement. Atlas licensing is commercially separate from Advisory matter fees.

For Measurable Value Matters, the operating default is 60% committed / 40% variable, with an approved 40/60–80/20 range where attribution and risk justify it. Variable economics follow the controlled payout curve and require independent Value Office certification. Capability Matters use milestone/acceptance economics with meaningful consideration retained through transfer acceptance. Decision Matters use fixed pricing where contingency would impair independence. The normal minimum professional matter fee is $150,000 excluding Atlas licensing and approved pass-throughs.

Matter Principal, Acceptance Principal and Independent Reviewer duties remain separate; rejected outcomes and uncertified variable fees cannot be recognized as completed performance.

## Carry architecture

Carry is a contractual phantom participation instrument reserved to qualifying former J2 professionals. The operating design uses 10,000 notional units and a pool equal to 20% of Excess Advisory Economic Profit after loss carryforward, required reserves and the annual hurdle. Vesting is five years at 20% per completed year, with holdback, malus/clawback and leaver treatment governed by the carry standard.

Eligibility is separate from allocation. Orientation, Judgment, Contact and Education have explicit service gates. J2 Headquarters service alone does not independently create eligibility. Carry creates economics, not voting rights, board rights, line authority or ownership of Advisory IP.

## Atlas Meridian relationship

Atlas Meridian is both a commercial enterprise product and Advisory's professional/client platform. Its dedicated team is a product organization, not a consulting staffing pool. Atlas Product owns the platform, roadmap, reliability and product support; Advisory owns the professional problem, conclusions, intervention, outcome and transfer.

Current commercial calibration uses institutional/tenant pricing rather than seat, token or consulting-hour pricing. Core is calibrated at $150,000–$350,000 annually and Enterprise at $350,000–$1.25 million annually; Managed combines Enterprise licensing with a separately scoped managed service. Final quotes depend on deployment, support and security requirements.
