## Business and output

Advisory operates three named practices on one common bench: Intelligence Advisory, Intelligence Capability and Operational Excellence. It commercializes approved capability and methods, including transferable client workflows and agent constellations. It sits adjacent to J2 and has its own commercial delivery accountability. It is not the internal Junction Advisory Group.

Outcome-scoped diagnosis, workflow/capability build, implementation and transfer. The unit of production is the matter. Its arc is Frame, Observe, Understand, Intervene, Prove, Transfer and Exit / Remain Reachable. Atlas is the client work portal and delivery system. Product-to-Advisory and Advisory-to-product referrals are tracked as commercial origins, not additional businesses.

## Operating lifecycle

Qualify client and conflicts → specify outcome, acceptance and information rights → authorize engagement and staff capacity → perform/evidence milestones → obtain independent client acceptance → invoice agreed outcome → transfer capability and close support obligations.

## Organization and authority

Use role/skill capacity, assignment fractions, approved internal time and contractor terms. The President role is settled; the individual remains open. Serving J2 personnel cannot enter an Advisory recruiting/application pipeline or discuss future Advisory roles or economics. Former qualifying J2 service may establish carry eligibility, never an automatic allocation. Orientation’s permanent anti-line-authority restrictions remain controlling.

J2 owns its method and internal judgments; Advisory owns commercial delivery. Only approved doctrine moves outward and appropriately cleared lessons return inward. Commercial pressure cannot determine J2 judgments or turn client fee approval into internal decision authority.

## Financial architecture

Bill outcomes and accepted milestones, not hours. Time measures delivery cost, utilization, unbilled effort and capacity. Record change orders, rejected acceptance, rework, discounts, milestone receivables, collections and margin by engagement. Atlas licensing is commercially separate from Advisory matter fees. The proposed measurable-value default is 60% committed and 40% outcome-variable, with an independent Value Office approving immutable baselines, attribution and capped payouts. Capability and Decision Matters have different fee logic. Matter Principal, Acceptance Principal and Independent Reviewer duties remain separate; rejected outcomes and uncertified variable fees cannot be recognized as completed performance.

## Natural records and controls

Conflict and rights review; engagement letter and outcome schedule; staffing assignment; working record; client acceptance/rejection; change order; invoice and receipt; transfer inventory; lessons release and feedback.

Priority failure modes: Hours silently becoming revenue, pressure to sell a favorable judgment, unapproved J2 methods disclosed, a client receiving another client’s working files, unclear acceptance and uncompensated rework.
