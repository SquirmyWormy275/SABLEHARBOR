## Business and output

Advisory commercializes approved capability and methods, including transferable client workflows and agent constellations. It sits adjacent to J2 and has its own commercial delivery accountability. It is not the internal Junction Advisory Group.

Outcome-scoped diagnosis, workflow/capability build, implementation and transfer. Offering categories organize the model; they do not lock permanent practice names. Atlas is the client work portal and delivery system. Product-to-Advisory and Advisory-to-product referrals are tracked as commercial origins, not additional businesses.

## Operating lifecycle

Qualify client and conflicts → specify outcome, acceptance and information rights → authorize engagement and staff capacity → perform/evidence milestones → obtain independent client acceptance → invoice agreed outcome → transfer capability and close support obligations.

## Organization and authority

Use role/skill capacity, assignment fractions, approved internal time and contractor terms. Do not appoint a named permanent head by inference. Borrowed J2 specialists retain source-role constraints; Orientation Officer career prohibitions are specific and remain controlling.

J2 owns its method and internal judgments; Advisory owns commercial delivery. Only approved doctrine moves outward and appropriately cleared lessons return inward. Commercial pressure cannot determine J2 judgments or turn client fee approval into internal decision authority.

## Financial architecture

Bill outcomes and accepted milestones, not hours. Time measures delivery cost, utilization, unbilled effort and capacity. Record change orders, rejected acceptance, rework, discounts, milestone receivables, collections and margin by engagement. Bundled Atlas/Foundry fees require explicit contract allocation so cross-selling does not duplicate revenue.

## Natural records and controls

Conflict and rights review; engagement letter and outcome schedule; staffing assignment; working record; client acceptance/rejection; change order; invoice and receipt; transfer inventory; lessons release and feedback.

Priority failure modes: Hours silently becoming revenue, pressure to sell a favorable judgment, unapproved J2 methods disclosed, a client receiving another client’s working files, unclear acceptance and uncompensated rework.

The [local control and interface register](BUSINESS_FINANCE_AND_ALEXANDRIA_INTERFACES.md) connects owner roles, evidence populations and existing CCF controls. Listed evidence requirements are not assertions that real controls operated.

## Remaining boundaries

Exact practice names, permanent leadership and price cards are not locked here. Model outcome/acceptance behavior and economics now; retain these limited permanent-design decisions explicitly.

Numerical successor assumptions remain publicly synthetic and separately versioned. Released v0.1 and industrial v2 evidence retains its original source boundary.
