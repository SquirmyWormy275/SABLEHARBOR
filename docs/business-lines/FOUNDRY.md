# Foundry

**Classification:** product substrate underlying Foundry Field  
**Dossier state:** review candidate  
**Current business-line home:** Foundry Field

Foundry is the reusable representational and integration substrate that emerged from repeated field engagements. Foundry Field is the deployable business line built on it. The distinction is canonically significant: the substrate is not silently promoted into a separate operating company.

## Status and scope

- Canon: locked name, lineage, and distinction from Foundry Field.
- Separate legal entity, leader, headcount, P&L, and operating inventory: not established.
- Standalone Foundry database, financial package, and unit letterhead: **NOT MATERIALIZED**.
