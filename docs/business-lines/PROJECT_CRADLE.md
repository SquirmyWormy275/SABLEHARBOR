## Business and output

Cradle recovers useful material from designated side streams while preserving the host’s primary operation. Its accepted architecture includes Stream 17 at Kelly Gang Mining, the Demotte mine-water intervention and the separate Bedford facility.

Stream 17 produces mixed rare-earth concentrate for specialist downstream sale. The equipment belongs to Cradle; rights and captured-material title follow the agreed recovery boundary. Demotte’s controlled AMD slipstream is a learning and recovery intervention, not ownership of the host treatment plant.

## Operating lifecycle

Authorize designated stream and stop/bypass conditions → measure feed and assay → recover and establish captured-material title → identify intermediate lots → receive/process at Bedford where applicable → downstream assay and acceptance → invoice → settle realized-value host share.

## Organization and authority

Kelly Gang Mining and Demotte Reclamation Services are external hosts. Bedford is a fictional Fairmont-area development/refurbishment/analytical/upgrading site, not the Demotte treatment site or a mine. Host personnel are not Sable Harbor payroll.

Demotte owns ordinary treatment, compliance and stop authority; hard bypass always remains available. Cradle recovery authority does not supersede the host. No new uranium custody qualification is implied.

## Financial architecture

Mineral tonnes × contained grade × availability × recovery is distinct from AMD water volume × dissolved concentration × recovery. Track fouling/downtime, reagents, energy, transport, assay, acceptance, payable product, costed unsold inventory, host share and payment timing. Gen-1 fouling and Gen-2 learning must affect operations. Do not backsolve selling price from an allocated enterprise revenue target.

## Natural records and controls

Stream-rights scope; bypass/stop log; feed and assay; module run and maintenance; captured lot; Bedford batch and custody movement; downstream acceptance; sales invoice; host settlement; inventory rollforward and disposal.

Priority failure modes: Conflating water with mineral feed, treating all contained material as payable sale, unsupported product title, selling before assay/acceptance, missing host-share liabilities, and counting unsold intermediate as cash.
