# SABLE HARBOR — CCF CONTROL OBJECTIVES v0.1

**Framework:** `SH-CCF`  
**Version:** 0.1.0  
**Date:** September 2, 2026  
**Status:** FOUNDATION POPULATION

These objectives are native Sable Harbor business/control objectives. External frameworks may map to them later but did not define them.

## GOV — Corporate Governance & Oversight
- **SH-OBJ-GOV-001:** The board and its committees maintain documented mandates, memberships, independence expectations, meeting cadence, information rights, and retained authority.
- **SH-OBJ-GOV-002:** Reserved matters and delegated authorities are explicit, current, and distinguish board, management, operating, technical, scientific, and capital authority.
- **SH-OBJ-GOV-003:** Material strategy, risk, financial, technology, operational, legal, and people matters are escalated to the appropriate governance body in time for meaningful oversight.
- **SH-OBJ-GOV-004:** Governance records preserve decisions, dissent, approvals, recusals, follow-up actions, and effective dates without rewriting historical state.

## ERM — Enterprise Risk Management
- **SH-OBJ-ERM-001:** Material enterprise and local risks are identified in cause-event-impact terms with accountable ownership and affected objectives.
- **SH-OBJ-ERM-002:** Risk appetite, tolerance, escalation thresholds, and acceptance authorities are defined and periodically reconsidered as Sable Harbor changes.
- **SH-OBJ-ERM-003:** Inherent and residual risk are evaluated using current evidence, and material changes are reported through appropriate governance channels.
- **SH-OBJ-ERM-004:** Emerging, cross-functional, acquisition, model, and physical-operating risks are not lost between organizational boundaries.

## ETH — Ethics, Conduct & Fraud Risk
- **SH-OBJ-ETH-001:** Personnel and directors understand expected conduct, conflicts, anti-bribery, fraud, reporting, and non-retaliation expectations.
- **SH-OBJ-ETH-002:** Potential misconduct and conflicts are disclosed, triaged, investigated, resolved, and retained with appropriate independence and confidentiality.
- **SH-OBJ-ETH-003:** Fraud and related-party risks are considered in financial, procurement, payroll, capital, contracting, and acquisition processes.

## POL — Policy & Control Governance
- **SH-OBJ-POL-001:** Policies, standards, procedures, and controls have accountable owners, approval authority, effective dates, review cycles, and version history.
- **SH-OBJ-POL-002:** Control exceptions and waivers are explicit, risk-assessed, time-bounded where appropriate, approved at the correct level, and closed with evidence.
- **SH-OBJ-POL-003:** Common controls and local implementations are distinguishable, and local variation is documented rather than hidden.

## LEG — Legal, Regulatory & Contractual
- **SH-OBJ-LEG-001:** Applicable legal, regulatory, license, permit, contractual, customer, and program obligations are identified and assigned to accountable owners.
- **SH-OBJ-LEG-002:** Contracts and legal commitments receive review and approval proportionate to risk and authority before execution.
- **SH-OBJ-LEG-003:** Legal holds, claims, litigation, regulatory inquiries, and material contractual disputes are preserved and escalated appropriately.

## PPL — People, HR & Workforce
- **SH-OBJ-PPL-001:** Hiring, onboarding, role assignment, performance, compensation, transfer, and termination actions are authorized, documented, and reflected in dependent systems.
- **SH-OBJ-PPL-002:** Personnel and contractors are screened and engaged according to role, legal, customer, security, and field requirements.
- **SH-OBJ-PPL-003:** Sensitive employment and compensation changes are independently reviewed where needed and reconciled to payroll/access outcomes.
- **SH-OBJ-PPL-004:** Workforce records, succession, key-person dependencies, and critical staffing risks are maintained sufficiently for operations and governance.

## TRN — Training & Competence
- **SH-OBJ-TRN-001:** Mandatory baseline and role-specific training is identified, assigned, completed, and monitored.
- **SH-OBJ-TRN-002:** Personnel performing safety-, security-, finance-, audit-, model-, or field-critical work have demonstrable competence and required qualifications.
- **SH-OBJ-TRN-003:** Field qualification and experimental transfer requirements prevent laboratory or analytical success from becoming uncontrolled operating authority.

## IAM — Identity & Access Management
- **SH-OBJ-IAM-001:** User and non-human identities are uniquely attributable, appropriately authenticated, and provisioned only from authorized requests.
- **SH-OBJ-IAM-002:** Access reflects current job/business need and changes promptly after transfer, termination, role change, or contract end.
- **SH-OBJ-IAM-003:** Privileged access, service accounts, break-glass access, secrets, and high-risk entitlements receive enhanced governance and review.
- **SH-OBJ-IAM-004:** Periodic access reviews detect stale, excessive, conflicting, or inappropriate access and drive accountable remediation.

## SEC — Cybersecurity & Security Operations
- **SH-OBJ-SEC-001:** Security responsibilities, risk treatment, architecture, and monitoring are integrated into enterprise and product operations.
- **SH-OBJ-SEC-002:** Vulnerabilities and security weaknesses are identified, prioritized, remediated, accepted, or escalated based on actual exposure.
- **SH-OBJ-SEC-003:** Endpoint, network, cloud, SaaS, secrets, logging, and detection controls reduce and surface unauthorized activity.
- **SH-OBJ-SEC-004:** Material threats and security events transition into incident response without evidence loss or ownership ambiguity.

## DAT — Data Governance, Privacy & Information Lifecycle
- **SH-OBJ-DAT-001:** Information has identified ownership, classification, permitted use, quality expectations, and lifecycle requirements appropriate to context.
- **SH-OBJ-DAT-002:** Personal, customer, confidential, licensed, regulated, and restricted data is used, shared, retained, and disposed of according to applicable commitments.
- **SH-OBJ-DAT-003:** Data quality limitations, definitions, source authority, effective dates, and transformations remain visible where decisions depend on them.
- **SH-OBJ-DAT-004:** Data rights and licensing constraints are known before data is used for products, models, customer work, research, or external sharing.

## REC — Records, Provenance & Evidence Integrity
- **SH-OBJ-REC-001:** Material decisions, approvals, control performance, incidents, and transactions leave records sufficient to reconstruct what occurred.
- **SH-OBJ-REC-002:** Source provenance, extraction parameters, transformations, custody, retention limitations, and artifact integrity are preserved for evidence relied upon.
- **SH-OBJ-REC-003:** Records are retained and disposed of under defined schedules while legal, contractual, regulatory, and investigative holds override ordinary deletion when applicable.

## ENG — Engineering, SDLC & Change
- **SH-OBJ-ENG-001:** Product, code, infrastructure, model, configuration, and integration changes originate from defined need and accountable ownership.
- **SH-OBJ-ENG-002:** Changes receive proportionate review, testing, segregation, approval, deployment control, and rollback planning before production impact.
- **SH-OBJ-ENG-003:** Emergency changes are exceptional, attributable, constrained, and retrospectively reviewed rather than becoming an alternate normal process.
- **SH-OBJ-ENG-004:** Source code and build/release pipelines preserve integrity, traceability, and separation sufficient to prevent unauthorized production change.

## CFG — Technology Configuration & Asset Management
- **SH-OBJ-CFG-001:** Technology assets, services, environments, software, devices, and material configurations are inventoried and assigned ownership.
- **SH-OBJ-CFG-002:** Secure/reliable configuration baselines are defined for material systems and deviations are identified and governed.
- **SH-OBJ-CFG-003:** Patching, lifecycle, unsupported technology, secrets, certificates, and configuration drift are managed according to risk.

## OPS — IT & Service Operations
- **SH-OBJ-OPS-001:** Critical interfaces, jobs, pipelines, services, and operational procedures are monitored and failures are detected and owned.
- **SH-OBJ-OPS-002:** Capacity, performance, availability, dependency, and operational-health information supports timely intervention.
- **SH-OBJ-OPS-003:** Recurring operational failures transition into problem management and durable corrective action rather than repeated heroics.

## INC — Incident, Problem & Crisis Management
- **SH-OBJ-INC-001:** Events are triaged consistently and material incidents receive timely command, escalation, containment, investigation, communication, and recovery.
- **SH-OBJ-INC-002:** Incident records preserve chronology, evidence, decisions, impact, communications, and responsible parties.
- **SH-OBJ-INC-003:** Root causes, contributing conditions, lessons, and remediation are tracked through verified closure.
- **SH-OBJ-INC-004:** Crisis decisions distinguish immediate operating consequence from later technical, financial, legal, and governance conclusions.

## BCM — Business Continuity, Backup & Disaster Recovery
- **SH-OBJ-BCM-001:** Critical processes, systems, facilities, personnel, vendors, and recovery dependencies are identified through business impact analysis.
- **SH-OBJ-BCM-002:** Backup and recovery capabilities are configured, monitored, protected, tested, and aligned with actual recovery needs.
- **SH-OBJ-BCM-003:** Continuity and disaster-recovery plans are exercised, findings remediated, and material changes reflected in plans.

## TPR — Third-Party & Supply-Chain Risk
- **SH-OBJ-TPR-001:** Vendors, contractors, subservice organizations, and strategic partners are risk-tiered before or during engagement according to actual dependency and data/operating exposure.
- **SH-OBJ-TPR-002:** Contracts establish appropriate security, privacy, service, audit/evidence, data-rights, continuity, notification, and termination obligations.
- **SH-OBJ-TPR-003:** Material third parties are monitored for performance, risk change, concentration, incidents, and contract compliance.
- **SH-OBJ-TPR-004:** Third-party termination or transition removes access, returns/disposes data, preserves records, and manages continuity risk.

## PRD — Product Governance & Customer Service
- **SH-OBJ-PRD-001:** Product commitments, features, service boundaries, customer configurations, and implementation decisions are owned and supportable.
- **SH-OBJ-PRD-002:** Customer-impacting change and service issues are assessed, communicated, and resolved according to contractual and operational significance.
- **SH-OBJ-PRD-003:** Product roadmaps and exceptions do not silently create unsupported security, operational, data, or financial commitments.

## FND — Foundry / Foundry Field Data Representation
- **SH-OBJ-FND-001:** Foundry preserves relationships among source, meaning, authority, mapping, transformation, effective date, and later outcomes.
- **SH-OBJ-FND-002:** Foundry Field deployments identify source-system boundaries, mappings, customer definitions, validation responsibilities, and unresolved ambiguities.
- **SH-OBJ-FND-003:** Transformations and mappings are controlled, testable, reversible or explainable, and attributable to approved change.
- **SH-OBJ-FND-004:** Foundry represents disagreement and uncertainty rather than manufacturing false canonical truth.

## AIM — AI, Model & Decision-Support Governance
- **SH-OBJ-AIM-001:** Models and material analytical methods have identified purpose, owner, data lineage, version, validation state, limitations, and approved use.
- **SH-OBJ-AIM-002:** Independent challenge and validation are proportionate to decision consequence and model risk.
- **SH-OBJ-AIM-003:** Material model changes, training/evaluation changes, third-party model substitutions, and prompt/system changes are governed and monitored.
- **SH-OBJ-AIM-004:** Human decision authority, uncertainty, override, and prohibited/autonomous uses remain explicit for Atlas Meridian and other decision-support systems.

## RND — Research, Experimentation & Technology Transfer
- **SH-OBJ-RND-001:** Experiments begin from bounded questions, hypotheses, scope, ownership, data, and success/kill criteria.
- **SH-OBJ-RND-002:** Experimental evidence and negative results are preserved and evaluated without retroactive success narratives.
- **SH-OBJ-RND-003:** Transfer from experiment to production requires qualification, accountable operating ownership, change governance, and explicit acceptance of remaining uncertainty.

## FLD — Field Operations, Qualification & Safety
- **SH-OBJ-FLD-001:** Physical operating activity has a clearly accountable operating owner with stop/constrain authority for immediate conditions.
- **SH-OBJ-FLD-002:** New processes, technology, models, experiments, and changes are field-qualified before operating deployment when consequence warrants.
- **SH-OBJ-FLD-003:** Safety-, maintenance-, inspection-, operating-, and abnormal-condition records are complete enough to support immediate decisions and later investigation.

## ENV — Environmental, Permitting & Resource Compliance
- **SH-OBJ-ENV-001:** Environmental, resource, land, permit, monitoring, waste, material-handling, and remediation obligations are inventoried and assigned.
- **SH-OBJ-ENV-002:** Required monitoring, sampling, reporting, permit conditions, and corrective actions are performed, reviewed, and retained.
- **SH-OBJ-ENV-003:** Material environmental deviations and permit risks are escalated before operating or capital decisions make them harder to correct.

## FIN — Financial Governance & Close
- **SH-OBJ-FIN-001:** The chart of accounts, accounting policies, close calendar, responsibilities, and entity/consolidation boundaries support consistent financial reporting.
- **SH-OBJ-FIN-002:** Material accounts are reconciled and unusual differences investigated before financial statements are finalized.
- **SH-OBJ-FIN-003:** Journal entries, estimates, consolidations, eliminations, allocations, and management adjustments are supportable, authorized, and reviewed.
- **SH-OBJ-FIN-004:** Financial statements and management reports are reviewed for completeness, accuracy, consistency, and material unusual activity.

## REV — Revenue, Contract-to-Cash & Receivables
- **SH-OBJ-REV-001:** Customer master, contract, pricing, order, milestone, usage, acceptance, and billing inputs are authorized and consistent.
- **SH-OBJ-REV-002:** Revenue-recognition inputs and judgments reflect approved contracts, delivery state, modifications, and accounting policy.
- **SH-OBJ-REV-003:** Receivables, collections, credits, write-offs, refunds, and adjustments are monitored and authorized.

## PRC — Procurement, Payables & Spend
- **SH-OBJ-PRC-001:** Vendors are created/changed only from valid requests with appropriate ownership, tax/payment, conflict, and due-diligence information.
- **SH-OBJ-PRC-002:** Purchases and commitments are authorized before obligation and comply with delegated authority and required sourcing/review.
- **SH-OBJ-PRC-003:** Receipt, invoice, coding, approval, and payment evidence supports valid, complete, accurate, non-duplicate disbursement.
- **SH-OBJ-PRC-004:** High-risk, emergency, non-PO, and exception spend is visible and governed rather than hidden in ordinary processing.

## PAY — Payroll, Benefits & Equity Administration
- **SH-OBJ-PAY-001:** Payroll master-data, compensation, benefit, equity, time, and deduction changes originate from authorized personnel actions.
- **SH-OBJ-PAY-002:** Payroll processing is reviewed and reconciled to approved workforce and financial records before/after payment as appropriate.
- **SH-OBJ-PAY-003:** Sensitive payroll, banking, executive compensation, and equity administration activities have appropriate segregation and oversight.

## TRY — Treasury, Cash, Debt & Capital
- **SH-OBJ-TRY-001:** Bank, payment, treasury-system, debt, and investment access is restricted and periodically reviewed.
- **SH-OBJ-TRY-002:** Cash movements and financing actions are authorized, independently verified where risk warrants, and reconciled.
- **SH-OBJ-TRY-003:** Liquidity, debt, covenant, maturity, counterparty, and concentration risks are monitored and escalated.
- **SH-OBJ-TRY-004:** Capital requests document business case, alternatives, risk, authority, assumptions, and post-investment accountability.

## AST — Fixed Assets, Inventory & Custody
- **SH-OBJ-AST-001:** Material assets and inventory are recorded, classified, tagged/identified where appropriate, and assigned custody/ownership.
- **SH-OBJ-AST-002:** Physical counts, inspections, reconciliations, and movement/custody records identify loss, error, obsolescence, or unauthorized disposition.
- **SH-OBJ-AST-003:** Capitalization, depreciation/depletion, impairment, transfer, retirement, and disposal are supported and approved.

## TAX — Tax & Statutory Reporting
- **SH-OBJ-TAX-001:** Tax and statutory filing obligations by entity/jurisdiction are identified and calendared.
- **SH-OBJ-TAX-002:** Returns, provisions, nexus positions, indirect taxes, and statutory reports are prepared from reconciled data and reviewed before filing.
- **SH-OBJ-TAX-003:** Material positions, exposures, notices, and changes in tax/statutory status are escalated and documented.

## MNA — M&A, Integration & Intercompany
- **SH-OBJ-MNA-001:** Acquisitions/dispositions receive commercial, financial, legal, security, technology, people, operational, and control diligence proportionate to risk.
- **SH-OBJ-MNA-002:** Day-1 ownership, access, financial reporting, legal, banking, payroll, vendor, incident, and governance controls are explicit before transaction close or promptly thereafter.
- **SH-OBJ-MNA-003:** Inherited exceptions and local controls are cataloged, risk-assessed, and converged deliberately rather than automatically erased.
- **SH-OBJ-MNA-004:** Intercompany transactions, services, balances, allocations, settlements, and agreements are complete, supportable, and reconciled.

## ARU — ARU / BS&T Operating Controls
- **SH-OBJ-ARU-001:** ARU and BS&T preserve clear operating, legal, financial, asset, customer, and regulatory boundaries during integration.
- **SH-OBJ-ARU-002:** Freight/rail/utility operating records, waybills, custody, rates, assets, maintenance, and settlements are complete and attributable.
- **SH-OBJ-ARU-003:** Sable Harbor common controls are inherited where appropriate without suppressing controls necessary to ARU/BS&T operations.

## PSN — Pale Sun / Red Wash Controls
- **SH-OBJ-PSN-001:** Red Wash operates through accountable site/legal ownership distinct from analytical or research authority.
- **SH-OBJ-PSN-002:** Uranium/resource production, inventory, custody, sales, environmental, permitting, asset-retirement, and site-operating records are controlled and reconcilable.
- **SH-OBJ-PSN-003:** Federal/security obligations are adopted only when actual contracts, data, systems, or program boundaries make them applicable.

## CRD — Cradle Participation & Recovery Controls
- **SH-OBJ-CRD-001:** Host/operator, Sable Harbor, equipment, process-right, recovery-right, ownership, custody, and settlement boundaries are documented for each Cradle arrangement.
- **SH-OBJ-CRD-002:** Recovered material measurement, allocation, custody, valuation, settlement, and revenue/cost sharing are independently supportable.
- **SH-OBJ-CRD-003:** Cradle does not acquire implied host-mine operating authority merely because Sable Harbor technology or equipment is deployed there.

## ADV — Advisory & Professional Services
- **SH-OBJ-ADV-001:** Engagements are accepted only when scope, customer, conflicts, competence, data, pricing, authority, and deliverables are understood.
- **SH-OBJ-ADV-002:** Work is staffed, supervised, reviewed, delivered, and retained according to engagement risk and commitments.
- **SH-OBJ-ADV-003:** Advisory work does not create unrecognized operational, independence, product, data-rights, or assurance commitments.

## ASS — Assurance, Compliance & Control Monitoring
- **SH-OBJ-ASS-001:** Management control self-assessment and second-line monitoring are distinguished from independent assurance.
- **SH-OBJ-ASS-002:** Control testing uses defined populations, evidence, procedures, results, exceptions, and limitations sufficient for the conclusion actually stated.
- **SH-OBJ-ASS-003:** Findings, deficiencies, waivers, incidents, remediation, and risk acceptance are linked and tracked through accountable closure.
- **SH-OBJ-ASS-004:** External assurance and compliance requests receive controlled, supportable evidence without allowing an external framework to redefine Sable Harbor’s underlying control architecture.
