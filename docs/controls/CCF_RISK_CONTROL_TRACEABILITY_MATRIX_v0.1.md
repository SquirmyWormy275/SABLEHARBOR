## Traceability rules

- Risk-to-control relationships are many-to-many.
- The matrix identifies primary coverage only; local implementation registers can add controls.
- A risk remains in the register even if every mapped control is effective.
- Risk acceptance and control exceptions are linked but are not interchangeable.
- Material changes to business model, entity structure, systems, contracts, regulation, incidents, acquisitions or physical operations trigger traceability review.
