# CCF GOVERNANCE, J2, AND ALEXANDRIA INTEGRATION v0.1

**Version:** 0.1.0 | **Date:** September 2, 2026 | **Status:** CANON-DERIVED CONTROL MAPPING

The CCF consumes—not creates—the operating world now established in governance and J2 doctrine. This is a native Sable Harbor mapping, not a redesign around an external framework.

| Native capability | CCF control consequence | Natural evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Nine-seat board / five committees | Board composition, oversight, conflict, capital, technology/operations, human-capital controls | board/committee rosters, minutes, recusals, decisions |
| Authority envelope / reserved matters | Delegation, exposure-based escalation, anti-circumvention, entity/capital approval | approved envelopes, escalation records, decision traffic |
| Rapid adjudication / risk assumption | Timely adverse-finding handling without blanket veto; explicit risk ownership | findings, clocks, adjudications, Assumption of Risk records |
| Abstention / information deficiency | Board-intelligence and fitness health signal | reasoned abstentions, deficiency remediation |
| Decision quality / AAR | Learning and corrective-control change independent of outcome | forecasts, hot washes, AARs, changed assumptions/controls |
| Information access doctrine | Nature-based restrictions, question visibility, restriction ownership/review | access decisions, reviews, challenge/disposition records |
| Contact / Judgment / Orientation | Provenanced collection, accountable human judgment, institutional reorientation | requirements, evidence handoffs, Problem Books, judgments, EIB |
| JAG | Independent fresh-eye field learning without audit/command confusion | deployment records, human handoffs, follow-up observations |
| J2 Education | Effective compliance and role education | curriculum lineage, SME approval, completion/effectiveness evidence |
| Alexandria temporal integrity | Point-in-time knowledge, non-silent supersession, lineage | event/record/access/understanding/change timestamps, supersession links |
| Semaphore / Canon human authorship | Durable traffic and institutional knowledge remain human-owned | author identity, editorial review, references, correction history |
| Daedalus boundaries | AI segregation, bounded disclosure, no authoritative write/promotion | policy tests, entitlement/leakage tests, synthetic markings, memory-isolation evidence |

Internal Audit maturity remains OPEN. These controls can support later independent assurance without presuming its 2026 organizational form or translating Sable Harbor doctrine into SOC/ISO/NIST vocabulary.
