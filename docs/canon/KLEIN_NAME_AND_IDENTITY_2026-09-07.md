# Klein — name and historical identity

**Decision ID:** KLEIN-001
**Date:** September 7, 2026
**State:** LOCKED owner-selected name and logo, effective on acceptance into main
**Scope:** Editorial replacement of Evalon across the fictional Pittsburgh outpost's history and identity

## Accepted decision

The owner selected **Klein** after requesting a clean replacement for Evalon because the name was too easy to confuse with Emberline. After seeing the Klein logo, the owner instructed: “push the name changes and logo to the repo.” That instruction accepts the displayed artwork for repository use. The rejected Rivet proposal and other suggested names create no canon.

The outpost was always called **Klein** in the story. This is an editorial replacement, not an in-universe rebranding, acquisition, second organization, additional corporate entity, or new management event.

| Identity | Controlling distinction |
|---|---|
| Klein | Gid Voss's 2020–2022 Pittsburgh-area coal/industrial mandate and advanced-engineering outpost; the operating concept ends in 2022. |
| Willow | The bounded laboratory created from Klein's surviving people, equipment, methods and experimental capability in 2022. |
| Emberline | Caleb Hargrove's Charleston-centered commercial coal, field and paid-experiment program, formalized in 2022 and active through 2025. |

All established events, people, dates, the Sar-e-Sang incident, the Fort, the Museum, Theseus and the substantive September 6 Willow closeout survive. References to surviving labels, signs, equipment and the original crew now use Klein. No new 2026 P&L or operating division is created.

## Approved artwork

The selected source is [klein__primary-horizontal.png](../../assets/brand/logos/klein__primary-horizontal.png): a petrol-blue and graphite mechanical K, **KLEIN** wordmark and **COAL & INDUSTRIAL SOLUTIONS** descriptor. The [visual manifest](../../assets/brand/klein_visual_manifest.json) records its exact bytes and provenance; the [identity record](../../assets/brand/KLEIN_IDENTITY.md) records the generation prompt.

This supersedes the previous coal/stone/orange Evalon design and its `PEOPLE | GROUND | PROGRESS` line. It does not replace Willow or Emberline artwork. Do not synthesize additional approved variants or claim a vector master exists. The PNG is controlling source artwork.

The former `assets/brand/logos/evalon/evalon-logo-canonical.png` was a truncated 4,392-byte PNG and is removed from the current asset tree. Its previous bytes remain in commit `498421e4e209880e167a6357ac92d9d9031c0791`; the visual manifest records that retired path and hash. Repairing that superseded identity is no longer a delivery requirement.
