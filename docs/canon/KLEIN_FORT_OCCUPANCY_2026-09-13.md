## Approved history

The original Klein shop is the leased light-industrial building outside Pittsburgh documented in the **2021** narrative. Willow continues using those premises after its **2022** institutional recharter. The recharter itself does not cause a relocation, property transaction or in-world intermediate rename.

During **2024**, Willow transfers its principal Pittsburgh research operation to **separate premises**, the industrial compound known as **the Fort**. The move is staged: equipment is inventoried, transported and recommissioned rather than treated as continuously available throughout transfer. The old shop is vacated during that calendar year and is no longer occupied by the team from **January 1, 2025**. January 1 is an exclusion bound, not an invented move or lease-expiration day.

The retained Klein sign, marked equipment and selected retired prototypes move with the team. Their presence at the Fort establishes artifact continuity, not shared-parcel continuity. The Big Shed, Small Shed, White Shed and Museum keep their accepted functions. No individual component commissioning date is inferred from the relocation year.
