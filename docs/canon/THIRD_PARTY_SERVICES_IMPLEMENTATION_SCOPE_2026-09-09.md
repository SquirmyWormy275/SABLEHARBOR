## Scope preserved from the same session

The parent record remains controlling within its scope. This supplement preserves details actually approved in the same conversation but not separately represented in its consolidated table. It does not introduce new sourcing questions.

| Service detail | Approved treatment |
|---|---|
| Customer support and ticketing | Buy supported platforms; retain customer-service processes, communications, ownership and integrations internally. |
| Privacy operations | Buy supporting tools; retain purpose, authority and privacy accountability internally. |
| Records, retention and e-discovery | Buy supporting tools; retain records policy, legal holds and disposition authority internally. |
| Entity management and Corporate Secretary tooling | Buy routine software; retain all legal and corporate-governance authority internally. |
| Security awareness and phishing simulation | Internal capability involving J2 Education and Security. External tools/content are components, not an outsourced instructional function. |
| Data labeling and annotation | Internal work with controlled source access; do not send sensitive data to an outside labeling workforce. |

Purchased developer tools remain permitted and are preferred over recreating commodity Git, CI or artifact platforms. Internal pipeline, release, configuration and engineering authority does not mean developing a new source-control system.

J2 Education retains pedagogy, instructional design and delivery. Legal, Safety, HR, Compliance and Security retain their subject-matter requirements. The approximately 35 Education design billets are part of J2's establishment, not an additional workforce to add to the 237-billet J2 total.

Analytics remains internal without making J2 the superior of Finance, businesses or product engineering. Finance retains authoritative financial truth; businesses retain customer/product decisions and source rights; J2 retains institutional interpretation and disclosure authority. Shared data engineering supplies technical capability without acquiring those substantive authorities.

Sable Harbor-controlled key custody applies to its internal key service and retained infrastructure layers. It does not establish control of a SaaS provider's infrastructure master keys. The purchased-service register must identify the provider's actual cryptographic boundary before making that claim.

## Implementation authority and limits

The authorized tranche builds the three linked registers, six workload requirement records, recovery/capacity calculations, staffing requirements, five-year architecture comparisons, a staged ownership example, and a non-destructive finance bridge. It may propose numerical planning assumptions and test them. It may not describe those assumptions as measured demand, accepted service levels, selected vendors, occupied positions, executed contracts or installed facilities.

Exact providers, sites, contracts, hardware selections, staffing assignments, capital commitments, RTO/RPO acceptance and Alexandria runtime/disclosure/retention enforcement remain separate decisions or acceptance gates. No historical board meeting, transaction, operating evidence or release bytes are invented.

The withdrawn translation/localization question did not establish an enterprise international-sales program. It also did not establish a blanket domestic-only rule. Matter-specific translation already listed in the assessment remains a bounded possible external input, not a newly approved enterprise platform.
