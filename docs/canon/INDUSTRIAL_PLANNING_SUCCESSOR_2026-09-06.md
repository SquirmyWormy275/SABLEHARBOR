The owner authorized the proposed sequence: a 2027–2031 operating model and investment review, followed by deeper transaction evidence and whole-enterprise financial integration. Operating disruptions and a navigable case browser support the same deliverable. This record preserves that authority without fabricating a company board meeting or an approval of future capital, borrowing or regulatory qualification.

The successor must connect physical activity, customer renewals, payroll, purchased services, capital, working capital, debt, taxes, parent funding and intercompany eliminations. Each scenario must produce balanced financial statements and disclose both required funding and the limits of assumed financing. A funding requirement is not itself a committed facility. A balanced ledger is not independent evidence that its commercial assumptions will occur.

The interface spending already embedded in the accepted 2026 forecast remains part of the opening scenario. Prospective alternatives compare incremental decisions from that point; retrospective investment appraisal must be separately identified. Internal transfer revenue cannot become outside economic benefit merely because two legal entities recognize it.

The enterprise successor must state which old industrial calibration records it removes, which Core operating records it retains, and which industrial records it adds. Retained Core envelopes remain synthetic calibration. Operating lines do not become separate legal corporations for presentation convenience. Unresolved enterprise policy and statutory details outside the adopted industrial scope remain open.

The published industrial v1.0.0 and finance v0.1.0 source identities, immutable finance canon pins, approved artwork and historical records remain preserved. New dated source inputs, scenario manifests, journals, publications and releases identify their own source revision and epistemic scope. The September 6 planning model must not enter a September 5 participant snapshot as knowledge available then.

All generated supplier records, waybills, payroll evidence, work orders and bank reconciliations must identify whether they drive model calculations or reconstruct modeled events. Calibrated unit rates and invented counterparties are disclosed. No evaluator truth or private control-repository material belongs in the public successor.
