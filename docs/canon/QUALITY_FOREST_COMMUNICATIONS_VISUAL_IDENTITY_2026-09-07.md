# Quality Forest Communications — approved visual identity

**Decision ID:** QFC-VIS-001
**Date:** September 7, 2026
**State:** LOCKED owner-selected artwork, effective on acceptance into main
**Scope:** Historical visual identity of Ellis “Eli” Hoberg's forestry-communications company

## Owner decision

The owner supplied a Quality Forest Management logo as a visual reference and requested a Quality Forest Communications adaptation with an AN/PRC-117G radio and an antenna on the tree. The owner then directed that the yellow Q be retained, the radio fit wholly inside the Q, and the antenna become the classic 2055 SATCOM antenna. After viewing that revision, the owner instructed: **“Nice. Lock it for QFC.”** This final revision is the controlling artwork; the earlier incomplete concepts are not approved production forms.

The selected horizontal logo contains a green pine, a prominent golden-yellow serif **Q**, a small olive **AN/PRC-117G** within the Q's opening, and a tree-mounted depiction of the classic **AV2055-3 SATCOM antenna**, with its open radial reflector and crossed elements. A feed cable runs down the tree to the radio. Black brush-script wording reads **Quality / Forest / Communications** on a white field.

The [approved horizontal PNG](../../assets/brand/logos/quality-forest-communications__primary-horizontal.png) is **1821 × 864 pixels**, RGB, **1,099,423 bytes**. SHA-256: `d72708de214894d0184cc0c1211bcb99647c5df6bbf61a2323d668451f91060d`.

The [visual manifest](../../assets/brand/quality_forest_communications_visual_manifest.json) binds approval to the exact generated source bytes. The source is copied without redraw, recompression, recoloring, cropping or background alteration. Approval covers the complete horizontal artwork only. A separate tree/Q mark, vector master, transparent-background version or other production variant requires its own approval.

## Existing company and continuity

The [Willow/Klein closeout](WILLOW_KLEIN_CLOSEOUT_2026-09-06.md#eli-hoberg) continues to control the company history. Ellis “Eli” Hoberg founded Quality Forest Communications before joining Sable Harbor, building relay/repeater networks and practical communications architectures for dense forests and mountainous terrain. That experience precedes his underground RF and communications work at Klein and the Theseus rigs.

This artwork adds no founding date, ownership transfer, subsidiary, current business line or change to Hoberg's history. The equipment depictions are visual identity elements; they do not establish a military affiliation, manufacturer endorsement or new operating history. The user-supplied reference establishes the design brief, not a corporate relationship with the reference company.

The exact approved PNG controls over generic SVG-first and motif rules for this identity. The production inventory classifies QFC as a historical external company. No predecessor QFC logo was found in the repository production tree.
