## 1. Authority and evidence doctrine

This record selects the controlling Red Wash transaction and 2026 operating case in
the fictional Sable Harbor universe. It does not claim that Red Wash, its counterparties,
permits, employees, shipments, or financial results exist in the real world.

Four independent labels must not be collapsed:

- canon state (`LOCKED`, `PROVISIONAL`, `OPEN`, `SUPERSEDED`);
- record origin (`PUBLIC_SYNTHETIC_DIEGETIC` or `EXTERNAL_REALITY_ANCHOR`);
- evidence/model state (`DERIVED`, `SUPPORTED_ESTIMATE`, `PROVISIONAL_ASSUMPTION`,
  `SCENARIO`, `OPEN`, `CONFLICT`, or `SUPERSEDED`); and
- period role (`SYNTHETIC_CALIBRATION` through August 2026 or
  `MANAGEMENT_FORECAST` for September–December 2026).

There is no `ACTUAL` layer in this package. Locking a fictional case decision does not
turn a generated row into a historical actual. Real-world sources constrain the
synthetic model; they do not authenticate synthetic company facts.
