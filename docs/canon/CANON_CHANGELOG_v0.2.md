# SABLE HARBOR — CANON CHANGELOG v0.2

**Date:** August 31, 2026  
**Purpose:** Record every inherited canon statement materially clarified, expanded, or superseded by the corporate-lore pass.

No earlier canon is silently deleted. The original handover remains preserved in the repository.

---

## CHG-001 — Blackridge personnel boundary

**Prior direction:** The founders were described collectively as connected through Blackridge, leaving room to infer that Priya Raman, Jon Bell, or the broader Original Eight participated directly in the 2015 event.

**New canon:**

- Daniel Mercer had direct Blackridge exposure.
- Priya Raman became involved through Daniel’s post-event reconstruction and a shallow prior professional relationship.
- Jon Bell entered through Daniel and challenged the pair to prove recurrence and commercial value.
- Elena Torres, Marcus Reed, Dr. Maya Okafor, Caleb Hargrove, and Rachel Kim joined through later Sable Harbor work.

**State:** LOCKED  
**Reason:** Prevents retroactive insertion of every major company character into the Blackridge case and preserves Blackridge as independent upstream truth.

---

## CHG-002 — 2016–2018 founding incidents

**Prior direction:** Sable Harbor began as a services firm and built rough internal tools by 2018, but the specific bridge from Blackridge to Foundry was underdeveloped.

**New canon:** Three post-Blackridge customer incidents establish the recurring class of problem:

1. **The Forty-Seven Names** — identity is contextual and temporal.
2. **The Customer Is Right Twice** — reconciliation is not necessarily convergence.
3. **The Spreadsheet That Would Not Die** — the work is not always where the formal system says it is.

**State:** LOCKED  
**Reason:** Gives the founding thesis evidence, assembles the Original Eight through real work, and avoids a magical product leap.

## CHG-003 — Foundry versus Foundry Field

**Prior direction:** 2018 internal tools became Foundry Field v0.

**New canon:**

- The reusable relationship-and-meaning substrate begins in 2018 as Foundry or retrospective Foundry v0.
- Foundry Field becomes the formal remotely deployable commercial product identity during The Crossing and is standardized in 2021.

**State:** LOCKED clarification  
**Reason:** Preserves the 2018 technical origin while allowing the final product identity to emerge through 2020–2021 productization.

## CHG-004 — COVID-19 as an active Crossing catalyst

**Prior direction:** COVID halted travel and forced remote productization.

**New canon:** The deeper causal role is explicit: on-site Sable Harbor personnel had been standing in for missing software capability. Travel restrictions removed the human bridge, forcing customer-guided semantic mapping and remotely deployable Foundry Field.

**State:** LOCKED expansion  
**Reason:** Explains why the crisis changed the product rather than merely reducing services revenue.

---

## CHG-005 — Variation lifecycle

**Prior direction:** Shadow tools, exceptions, and technical scars were central.

**New canon:** Variation does not move linearly toward scar tissue. It can be retired, configured, supported, generalized, tolerated, become neutral operating change, or become innovation. Scar tissue is one unmanaged outcome.

**State:** LOCKED  
**Reason:** Corrects an overly Blackridge-shaped tendency to make every adaptation an impending failure.

---

## CHG-006 — Evalon, Emberline, and Willow

**Prior direction:** Project Emberline was a current coal-focused internal venture and R&D program centered in Charleston, with Caleb Hargrove as its natural leader.

**New canon:**

- Evalon was Gid Voss’s 2020–2022 Pittsburgh-area coal/industrial advanced-engineering outpost.
- Project Emberline was the formal 2022 Charleston-centered coal field and commercial venture led operationally by Caleb.
- The Sar-e-Sang incident forced a 2022 leadership review.
- Evalon ended as a business concept and its experimental capability was rechartered as Project Willow.
- Emberline continued through 2025 as paid coal R&D, field experimentation, and qualification work.
- Its enduring activities were absorbed into Foundry, Willow, field operations, and emerging Advisory.
- Emberline is not a standalone core business in August 2026.

**State:** LOCKED supersession  
**Reason:** Preserves the coal venture, Caleb’s role, and the Pittsburgh laboratory without duplicating two permanent experimental divisions.

## CHG-007 — Willow institutional identity

**Prior direction:** No separate mature cross-disciplinary laboratory was locked.

**New canon:** Project Willow is Sable Harbor’s bounded industrial skunk works. It pursues consequential questions no existing product, delivery team, or operating unit can own. It uses a question–belief–experiment–observation–decision loop and treats failure as useful when recorded, while treating drift into unsupported operations as unacceptable.

**State:** LOCKED  
**Reason:** Establishes the institutional lineage required for Atlas Meridian and later physical experimentation.

## CHG-008 — Atlas lineage and Atlas Meridian

**Prior direction:** Atlas Meridian existed as a 2022 thesis, rebooted in 2024, used design partners in 2025, and entered controlled launch in 2026.

**New canon:** That timeline remains, with a richer internal lineage:

- Atlas thesis: 2022–2023;
- Willow/Foundry model experiments and serious reboot: 2024;
- Hound, gauntlet, design partners, Owen Kessler, Layla Haddad, and Ranger: 2025;
- formal bridge program and Atlas Meridian product identity: 2026.

Atlas Meridian is an investigative decision-support product, not an autonomous answer or capital-decision engine.

**State:** LOCKED expansion  
**Reason:** Makes the product an earned convergence of customer questions, Foundry history, Willow experiments, and technological capability.

## CHG-009 — Pale Sun trajectory

**Prior direction:** Pale Sun began around 2026 as a uranium, nuclear-supply-chain, federal-procurement, and possible segregated-environment initiative.

**New canon:**

- The strategic-minerals inquiry begins in 2023–2024.
- Marianne “Mari” Varela leads a hostile kill study.

**Reason:** Gives Sable Harbor a physical mine, creates a real operating feedback loop, and establishes the distinction between representing a system and owning a constraint.

## CHG-011 — Deloraine Decision and Project Cradle

**Prior direction:** Rare earth elements were not a separate corporate line.

**New canon:** During the 2024 Australia/Tasmania trip, a debate at Little Green Men Brewing Co. in Deloraine splits one strategic-minerals thesis into two:

- Pale Sun pursues uranium asset ownership.
- Project Cradle pursues rare-earth recovery from process streams created by existing mines and plants.

The split is called **the Deloraine Decision**.

**State:** LOCKED  
**Reason:** Allows Sable Harbor to participate in rare earths without turning every strategic-minerals question into mine ownership.

## CHG-017 — Public repository and wiki posture

**Prior direction:** The preserved architecture handover described the canonical Sable Harbor repository as private.

**New canon and governance decision:**

- The repository is intentionally public to support a browsable institutional archive and public GitHub wiki.
- Versioned repository documents remain controlling canon; the wiki is a secondary public-facing reference and navigation layer.
- Public visibility does not create an open-source or content license.
- Hidden benchmark truth, evaluation oracles, credentials, unreleased answers, and restricted material remain outside the public repository and wiki.
- No standalone Easter-egg index, decoder, checklist, or exhaustive explanation page will be created.

**State:** LOCKED supersession  
**Reason:** Public accessibility and a rich wiki are deliberate project features, while canon authority, intellectual-property posture, spoiler control, and embedded lore still require explicit boundaries.
