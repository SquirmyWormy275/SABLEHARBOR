## 2. Cradle remains an REE-recovery business

### LOCKED

Project Cradle remains the Sable Harbor business/capability for recovering rare-earth-element value from process streams that another operator already creates or must already manage.

Its business thesis remains:

> **Find valuable rare-earth material that existing operations already concentrate unintentionally, then add the smallest recovery intervention that captures value without breaking the host operation.**

Cradle is not a conventional mine owner, not a general critical-minerals holding company, and not a vertically integrated magnet manufacturer.

The existing doctrine remains controlling:

> **Do not break the host system.**

and:

> **The orebody is not the business boundary.**

## 3. Stream 17 — first commercial reference case

### LOCKED

The provisional `Gunns` identity is replaced by **Kelly Gang Mining**.

Kelly Gang Mining is a fictional Tasmanian industrial-minerals / heavy-mineral operator. Its existing physical separation circuit creates a low-volume side stream internally designated **Stream 17**. The stream contains a materially enriched monazite-bearing heavy-mineral fraction even though the overall host feed is not an attractive standalone rare-earth orebody.

Cradle does not redesign the host mine or require the host to adopt a new mine thesis. It adds a bounded recovery step to Stream 17.

The Cradle intervention is designed so that:

- Kelly Gang Mining retains operating authority over the host plant;
- host production does not depend on Cradle equipment being available;
- Cradle equipment can be bypassed without stopping the host process;
- Cradle owns and maintains the recovery equipment used for the reference case;
- Cradle receives an exclusive recovery right to the designated Stream 17 material during the agreement term;
- title to recovered Cradle material transfers at the defined capture point;
- Cradle bears its direct recovery operating costs;
- Kelly Gang Mining receives negotiated participation in realized recovered-product value;
- Cradle sells a **mixed REE mineral concentrate** to a specialist downstream refiner rather than separating individual rare-earth oxides or manufacturing magnets at the host site.

The first arrangement is **modestly profitable on a direct basis and immaterial at Sable Harbor enterprise scale**. Exact price, percentage participation, tonnage and margin are intentionally not fixed as permanent lore numbers; quantitative case models must derive them from explicit feed, grade, recovery, product, price and cost assumptions rather than reverse-engineering a required revenue allocation.

This resolves `CRD-010` at the canon level.

## 4. Maeve Donnelly and the material-genealogy chain

### LOCKED

Wallaby remains a killed project. Maeve does not rescue it.

Maeve's decisive contribution begins when an experienced host operator casually describes the physical process in a way that conflicts with the official flowsheet and administrative stream categories. Maeve treats the discrepancy as evidence rather than operator error.

She reconstructs the actual material genealogy across:

> **source → physical process → stream → sampled lot → disposition**

The reconstruction shows that the existing sampling scheme follows administrative categories rather than the physical process that created concentration. The revised understanding changes Ned Kelly Mark II from a grade-finding exercise into a process-genealogy search.

The lesson survives Wallaby's death and becomes Cradle operating discipline.

For commercial recovery work, the genealogy extends through:

> **source → host process → designated stream → sample/assay → recovery run → captured intermediate → Bedford batch → outbound product → downstream acceptance → host settlement → financial record**

Every link preserves provenance, time, uncertainty, transformation and disposition. A later analytical or financial record may summarize the chain but may not erase it.

## 5. First U.S. deployment — Morrow Run

### LOCKED

Cradle's first U.S. reference deployment is an acid-mine-drainage recovery installation in north-central West Virginia operated in partnership with the fictional **Morrow Run Reclamation Services**.

Morrow Run operates ordinary treatment for water from a legacy underground coal complex. Environmental treatment and discharge obligations remain with the host operator. Cradle does not acquire the mine, the remediation liability or the host treatment system.

The first-generation Cradle unit is a containerized / skid-based **Gen 1 recovery module** connected to a controlled slipstream ahead of ordinary treatment.

Its interface rules are:

- the normal treatment path remains available at all times;
- a hard bypass returns the slipstream to ordinary treatment if Cradle equipment is unavailable or outside agreed operating limits;
- the host owns immediate water-treatment and compliance consequences;
- the host may stop or bypass Cradle when operating or environmental conditions require it;
- Cradle owns its recovery equipment, recovery procedure, maintenance and direct operating cost;
- Cradle's recovery right applies only to material captured through the defined intervention and does not create ownership of the host mine, water system or remediation asset;
- recovered intermediate is segregated, identified and transferred into Maeve's genealogy chain.

The Gen 1 deployment works but fouls more than expected under variable field chemistry. The result is not treated as failure or hidden as maintenance noise. It directly drives Gen 2 toward better continuous characterization, maintainability and operating-range awareness.

## 6. REE product boundary

### LOCKED direction

The Appalachian AMD opportunity is economically interesting because the recovered basket can contain meaningful magnet-critical and heavy/middle-REE value, including **dysprosium, terbium and yttrium**, with **neodymium and praseodymium** recovered as part of the broader basket where present.

Cradle does not claim that every deployment has the same element distribution. Feed chemistry remains site- and time-dependent and is measured rather than assumed.

In the 2026 operating model:

- field units produce a traceable REE-rich intermediate;
- Bedford upgrades and blends recovered material into a saleable mixed rare-earth concentrate / mixed-oxide product suitable for a specialist refiner;
- Cradle does **not** commercially separate every individual rare-earth element in-house;
- Cradle does **not** manufacture permanent magnets.

Future deeper separation is an investment option, not current canon.

## 7. Bedford

### LOCKED location and role; exact parcel IMPLEMENTATION

The Cradle U.S. recovery-development center is named **Bedford**.

Bedford is located in the **Fairmont area of north-central West Virginia** on a fictional redeveloped industrial brownfield. The working site scale is approximately **15–20 acres**.

Bedford is not a mine and is not the Morrow Run treatment site. It is the central development, integration and upgrading facility supporting distributed recovery deployments.

Bedford's 2026 role includes:

- analytical and process-development laboratory work;
- field-unit assembly, maintenance and refurbishment;
- controlled receipt and genealogy verification of recovered intermediates;
- blending and upgrading into saleable mixed REE products;
- secure material storage and outbound logistics;
- operator / deployment support and autopsy work on failed or degraded recovery runs.

Exact property polygon, street address, river/rail access, barge requirement and parcel history remain **implementation details for the geospatial package**, not unresolved Cradle business canon. The prior Belle / Kanawha Valley site direction is superseded.

## 10. Quantitative and software treatment

- eligible feed volume;

- availability and operating range;
- recovery efficiency;
- recovered product specification;
- accepted quantity;
- realized price;
- host participation;
- direct operating cost;
- maintenance and consumables;
- Bedford upgrading/logistics cost;
- working-capital timing;
- capital deployment and replacement.

Historical finance releases remain reproducible. A successor must use an explicit replacement bridge rather than double-counting old allocation-based Cradle activity and new bottom-up activity.

This implementation work is covered by the broader finance / standalone-unit workstream and is **not a remaining Cradle canon question**.
