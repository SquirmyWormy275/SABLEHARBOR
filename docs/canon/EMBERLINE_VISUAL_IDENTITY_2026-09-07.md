# Emberline — approved visual identity

**Decision ID:** EMB-VIS-001
**Date:** September 7, 2026
**State:** LOCKED owner-selected artwork, effective on acceptance into main
**Scope:** Emberline's historical visual identity

## Owner decision

The owner requested a more industrial, coal-like and old-school Emberline logo, then approved the displayed design with “nice. Lock it.” The exact approved image is now the controlling Emberline artwork.

The identity consists of heavy **EMBERLINE** slab-serif lettering, a coal ridge and layered seams inside an oxide-red roundel, the descriptor **COAL & INDUSTRIAL SERVICES**, and an ivory field. These are the selected visual elements, not a new legal company name or a change to the business model.

The [approved horizontal PNG](../../assets/brand/logos/emberline__primary-horizontal.png) is **2172 × 724 pixels**, RGB, **939,954 bytes**. SHA-256: `ddf4273bca6f9e0c9b050a1fc741b151550b86af5e2d2a98c46fdadfaf849312`.

The [visual manifest](../../assets/brand/emberline_visual_manifest.json) records approval, exact source integrity and the disposition of every earlier Emberline asset. The PNG is copied byte-for-byte from the image selected in this conversation. Its ivory background is intentional source content; this decision does not approve a transparent extraction, vector redraw, separate emblem, monochrome version or reverse variant.

## Supersession and continuity

The old geometric emblem, thin sans-serif wordmark, orange divider and “A HISTORICAL SABLE HARBOR LINE” treatment are superseded editorial artwork. All six earlier SVG/PNG files are preserved in the [historical artwork archive](../../assets/brand/history/emberline/2026-09-07/README.md), with their original checksums. They are removed from the current production inventory. This is an editorial design replacement, not an invented in-world rebranding event.

Emberline remains Caleb Hargrove's Charleston-centered coal field and commercial program, formalized in 2022 and active through 2025, with enduring work subsequently absorbed into Foundry, Willow, field operations and Advisory. Its historical status, people and dates do not change. Klein remains the distinct Pittsburgh outpost rechartered as Willow in 2022.

The selected imagery is an explicit approved-artwork exception to the generic motif guidance wherever that guidance would otherwise exclude this coal ridge. Later use must preserve the approved source; the older procedural brand generator and historical ZIP do not override this decision.
