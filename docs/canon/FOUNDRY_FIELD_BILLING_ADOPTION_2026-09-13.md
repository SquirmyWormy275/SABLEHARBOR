## Tax basis and financial boundary

The adopted transaction is standard prewritten software accessed remotely, with all 140
users in Sacramento city. It is neither bespoke development nor infrastructure on which the
customer runs its own software. No resale, reproduction or out-of-state-use exemption applies.
Customer digital purchases from SHI across all brands remain below $5 million in 2027.
The remote-sale sourcing rule uses the customer's Sacramento billing address.
These are newly authored commercial assumptions, not recovered source facts.
[Enacted SB 122](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/billNavClient.xhtml?bill_id=202520260SB122)
extends the relevant software tax rules from January 1, 2027.

Sales-tax reimbursement follows the agreement; the seller's absorbed tax is not deducted
from taxable gross receipts. [CDTFA Regulation 1700](https://cdtfa.ca.gov/lawguides/vol1/sutr/1700.html)
and [RTC 6012](https://cdtfa.ca.gov/lawguides/vol1/sutl/6012.html) support that treatment.
The [city's published 8.75% rate](https://www.cityofsacramento.gov/finance/budget/sales-tax-rate)
is carried forward as an explicit January 2027 planning assumption, researched September 13,
2026; this is not a verified future assessment. A changed future rate requires a successor.

The supplemental journal covers **January 31 issuance only**. It does not rewrite the pinned
financial release, assert tax remittance, or infer tax relief from later customer default.
The original February–October customer movements remain available in a separately labeled
historical-source reconciliation. Zero customer AR does not establish zero tax payable.
No later tax return, payment, bad-debt deduction or tax refund is represented by this packet.
