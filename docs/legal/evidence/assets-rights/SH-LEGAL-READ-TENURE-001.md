# Campus and operating-site tenure review

**Reader record:** SH-LEGAL-READ-TENURE-001 · **Reviewed:** September 12, 2026

**Source state:** MIXED RECORDED SITE STATES; plans do not establish title

This is a source-bound reading summary. The linked full instruments retain their own authority, effective dates and limitations. No new party, signature, payment, legal term or filing is established here.

## Sacramento

The integrated campus program and saved plans express accepted functions and explicit planning assumptions. The primary corporate/J2 location is Sacramento; that decision alone does not supply a lease, recorded parcel title or completed construction. [Source](../../../canon/CORPORATE_HEADQUARTERS_CLOSEOUT_2026-09-03.md).

## Willow and Bedford

Fort sheds/Museum and Bedford building functions are source-backed concepts. Read each plan title/status and exact site record before asserting measured floor area, ownership dates or occupation. [Source](../../../../geospatial/facilities/RESEARCH_CAMPUSES.md).

## Industrial sites

Keep mine, rail, terminal, trucking and warehouse functions with their accepted operating anchors. A structure plan cannot supply missing track engineering, underground workings, surveyed rights or an acquisition instrument. [Source](../../../../geospatial/facilities/INDUSTRIAL_FACILITIES.md).

## External and historical

Coverage includes external hosts, historical sites and unresolved locations. These dispositions are retained; a mapped counterparty does not become a subsidiary or owned workplace. [Source](../../../../geospatial/facilities/README.md).

## Accounting and record trace

Join facility/site/building ID and source status before allocating property cost. Seats/areas are planning capacity, not employee count, lease commitment or recognized PPE.

**Native identifiers / tables:** `geospatial/facilities/coverage/COVERAGE_MATRIX.json`, `geospatial/facilities/SPACE_REGISTER.json`.

## Missing evidence and limits

Exact unsupported deeds, leases, parcel surveys, occupancy dates and construction certificates remain individual source gaps; plans cannot fill them.
