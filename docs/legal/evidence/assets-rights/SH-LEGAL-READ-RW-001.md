# Red Wash purchase and closing-rights schedule

**Reader record:** SH-LEGAL-READ-RW-001 · **Reviewed:** September 12, 2026

**Source state:** LOCKED CASE TERMS; SYNTHETIC INSTRUMENTS

This is a source-bound reading summary. The linked full instruments retain their own authority, effective dates and limitations. No new party, signature, payment, legal term or filing is established here.

## Transfer and consideration

Pale Sun buys all RWH interests from Northstar on July 18, 2025. $42M operating assets + $4.5M current assets − $16M ARO − $2.5M other liabilities = $28M identifiable net assets and consideration; zero selected goodwill/transaction debt. [Source](../../../../industrial/transaction/01_RW_TRANSACTION_FILE.md).

## Seller allocation

$24.5M direct payment + $3M environmental/title escrow + $0.5M holdback = $28M. Escrow and holdback are included, not additional uses. The separate stabilization program is $11M: $8M capital and $3M expense. [Source](../../../../industrial/transaction/01_RW_TRANSACTION_FILE.md).

## Rights and deliveries

The equity instrument retains the mine operator and scheduled assets/permits/contracts. The bill-of-sale section covers listed assets, inventory, source records and transferable rights only as law permits; it is not proof of completed consent. [Source](../../../../red_wash/agreements/TRANSACTION_AND_COMMERCIAL_INSTRUMENTS.md).

## Risk and transition

The $3M escrow has a 36-month term for specified title/environmental matters. NMI transition assistance is up to six months at cost plus 5%, without operating, safety, personnel or capital authority. [Source](../../../../red_wash/agreements/TRANSACTION_AND_COMMERCIAL_INSTRUMENTS.md).

## Source discrepancy

Industrial and mine documents disagree on exact pre-close re-trade/walk-away dates. The higher-level canon locks the sequence and July 18 close, not those conflicting day-level details. [Source](../../../canon/RED_WASH_TRANSACTION_OPERATING_RECORD_2026-09-05_R2.md).

## Accounting and record trace

Reconcile consideration and escrow once. Keep current-cost closure estimate distinct from accounting ARO, and mine stabilization distinct from ARU catch-up capital.

**Native identifiers / tables:** `SH-IND-RW-TXN-001`, `SH-PS-RW-TOR-001`, `RWH`, `PS`.

## Missing evidence and limits

Individual title cures, permit/consent proof, bank confirmations and escrow releases are not demonstrated by a closing-delivery list. Preserve the date discrepancy for chronology exercises.
