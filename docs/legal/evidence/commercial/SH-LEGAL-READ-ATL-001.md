## License perimeter

Core and Enterprise are client editions; Professional remains the SH-controlled professional plane. Managed adds agreed operated client-plane services. Licensing is institutional/tenant based, not primarily per seat. [Source](../../../advisory/ATLAS_MERIDIAN_COMMERCIAL_PRODUCT_STANDARD.md).

## Price status

Illustrative market-testing annual bands are Core $150K–$350K and Enterprise $350K–$1.25M. Negotiation depends on scope, connectors, environments and support. These are not binding public offers or issued invoices. [Source](../../../advisory/ATLAS_MERIDIAN_COMMERCIAL_PRODUCT_STANDARD.md).

## Separation

Material implementation is priced separately. Client-transferred agents cannot depend silently on unlicensed Professional resources. Tenant, data, access, connector and export boundaries remain documented. [Source](../../../advisory/ATLAS_MERIDIAN_COMMERCIAL_PRODUCT_STANDARD.md).

## Amendment record

The operating successor supplies immutable contract versions with effective rate, seats, term, approval ID and applied month. Positive changes bill the remaining term; contraction/cancellation credits unearned claims rather than recognized service. [Source](../../../../enterprise/operations/docs/commercial.md).

## Service-credit boundary

Monthly modeled service demand and breach consequences are not uptime evidence or a negotiated contractual SLA. Earned-service credits reduce revenue and cannot reduce ARR/deferred revenue twice. [Source](../../../../enterprise/operations/docs/commercial.md).

## Accounting and record trace
