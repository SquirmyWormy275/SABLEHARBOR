## Agreement stack

MSA/PSA; required data/security addendum; separate Atlas license; matter SOW/charter; outcome schedule for measurable-value work or transfer schedule for capability work; jurisdiction/client addenda where required. [Source](../../../advisory/LEGAL_AND_CONTRACTING_TERM_SHEET.md).

## Required SOW

Name problem, deliverable, success criteria, authority/non-authority, client dependencies, milestones, committed/variable fees, Atlas charges, expenses, data assumptions, IP class, acceptance, change control and stop rights. [Source](../../../advisory/LEGAL_AND_CONTRACTING_TERM_SHEET.md).

## Commercial rules

Normal minimum professional fee $150,000, excluding Atlas, travel, third-party costs and taxes; exceptions need documented rationale. Measurable-value default is 60% committed / 40% variable, with approved scope and capped payout mechanics. [Source](../../../advisory/COMMERCIAL_CONTRACTING_AND_PRICING_STANDARD.md).

## Recognition evidence

Committed amounts follow milestone or monthly fixed billing. Variable fees are invoiced only after Value Office certification and contractual dispute procedures. Hours are internal cost/capacity evidence; they do not automatically create billable revenue. [Source](../../../advisory/COMMERCIAL_CONTRACTING_AND_PRICING_STANDARD.md).

## Transfer and exit

Separate client-owned, licensed SH capability, reserved professional IP and nontransferable third-party/institutional material. Unsafe/unlawful requests, falsification demands and material security/conflict issues can stop work. [Source](../../../advisory/IP_DATA_AND_METHOD_TRANSFER_STANDARD.md).

## Accounting and record trace

Trace matter class and accepted fee components separately from Atlas license and pass-through costs. Link certification/acceptance to the chosen released contract and invoice; no imagined executed SOW.
