# Taylor–Red Wash service terms

**Reader record:** SH-LEGAL-READ-IC-001 · **Reviewed:** September 12, 2026

**Source state:** SYNTHETIC_RECONSTRUCTED; uranium custody OPEN_GATED

This is a source-bound reading summary. The linked full instruments retain their own authority, effective dates and limitations. No new party, signature, payment, legal term or filing is established here.

## Parties and duration

ARU supplies terminal/trucking services; BS&T separately supplies rail; RWH is customer. Five-year term: July 7, 2026–July 6, 2031. Each service names its actual seller even when ARU administers billing. [Source](../../../../industrial/transaction/08_INTERCOMPANY_LOGISTICS_AGREEMENT.md).

## Price and quantity

Combined rates per car: acid $3,010; binder $2,390; lime $2,500; steel $2,725; process/MRO $1,900; qualified project freight $4,600. The 225-slot allowance contains 215 billable cars and ten unbilled contingencies; no minimum-volume or exclusive-carrier covenant. [Source](../../../../industrial/transaction/08_INTERCOMPANY_LOGISTICS_AGREEMENT.md).

## Service and exceptions

72 elapsed hours interchange-to-empty-release, 96 for steel; 36–48-hour operating goal. Customer-caused storage may incur $50/car/24 hours, prorated; provider delay earns no such charge. Baseline has zero chargeable dwell. [Source](../../../../industrial/transaction/08_INTERCOMPANY_LOGISTICS_AGREEMENT.md).

## Billing and exit

Monthly actual quantities and accepted rate versions; payment due within 30 calendar days. Thirty-day curable-breach period. Convenience termination after first year requires 180 days notice and carries no volume penalty. [Source](../../../../industrial/transaction/08_INTERCOMPANY_LOGISTICS_AGREEMENT.md).

## Asset and custody boundary

RWH owns the $3.25M mine program; ARU the $5.25M Taylor program. The $8.5M phase is separate from tariff revenue. Finished uranium concentrate and a mine rail spur are excluded. [Source](../../../../industrial/transaction/08_INTERCOMPANY_LOGISTICS_AGREEMENT.md).

## Accounting and record trace

Reconcile reciprocal invoice IDs once across rail, terminal and trucking sellers and the RWH buyer. Eliminate internal balances/income once; preserve external costs and owner depreciation.
