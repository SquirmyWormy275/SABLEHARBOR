## Your task

1. Reconcile buyer uses before fees to new debt and parent equity.
2. Separate parent cash including fees from stock consideration and retained finance leases.
3. Recompute book goodwill and independent tax goodwill; explain the difference.
4. Sum all 16 opening accounts and record the debit-credit difference.
5. Write an evidence conclusion distinguishing model reconciliation from signed closing and filing evidence.
