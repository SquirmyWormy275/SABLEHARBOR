## 1. Property and transaction identity

The [accepted runtime decision](../../../canon/RUNTIME_HOSTING_AND_DATA_CENTER_DECISIONS_2026-09-11.md) records a fictional 7.5-acre Northern Nevada planning parcel acquired for $3,000,000 on September 4, 2026. The [mock deed](../../MOCK_DEED_NORTHERN_NEVADA_DATA_CENTER_2026-09-04.md) describes the planning property and counterparty boundary; the [runtime finance reconciliation](../../../finance/RUNTIME_INFRASTRUCTURE_FINANCE_RECONCILIATION_2026-09-11.md) preserves unresolved settlement clearing. This packet does not reopen that accepted fictional acquisition or convert it into real-world title.

The grantee is Sable Harbor, LLC, accounting key SHI. The mock deed's TRIC Development Holdings is a fictional planning counterparty, not an assertion about an actual landowner. The planning anchor near 39.5450, −119.4550 identifies the model's geography only. No assessor parcel number, surveyed legal description, title-company file, recorded conveyance or real payment instruction is supplied.

The site's state remains land acquired / preconstruction in the planning universe. Survey, geotechnical, utility, fiber, design and permitting work do not establish a completed building, occupied data hall or commissioned critical plant. This settlement draft addresses the land event alone; Switch Reno and IDACORE Boise provider selection is neither land consideration nor an executed colocation order.

## 2. Proposed settlement instruction to the internal closing coordinator

**Instruction for completion:** transaction reference: __________________. Authorizing body/reference: __________________. Funding evidence reference: __________________. Actual authorization date: __________________. The clauses below apply only upon acceptance of this procedure and issuance of a separately authorized instruction. This copy releases no funds.

Subject to separate approval and evidence completion, the coordinator shall reconcile the accepted $3,000,000 planning consideration to the settlement source and the recipient entitled under an accepted instrument. The coordinator shall identify whether the supported counterpart is cash already settled, a specifically documented payable, financing, contribution or another accepted treatment. No category is selected by this draft. A balanced journal alone cannot identify the actual source of funds.

The coordinator shall prepare one land settlement statement with transaction reference, buyer identity, supported seller identity, effective event date, preparation date, consideration, adjustments, funding source and evidence references. Every adjustment shall have a source and a separate approval if it changes accepted economics. A blank field shall remain unresolved; it shall not become zero, an exemption or an assumed customary charge.

The instruction shall remain on hold until the funding and settlement counterpart are supported. Treasury shall not make a payment from a public mock deed, map coordinate, unsigned draft or analyst cost schedule. Legal shall confirm the identity and scope of the supporting instrument; Finance shall determine how the accepted event and new evidence relate to the existing clearing balance. This protocol supplies no authority to move money.

A source shall be evaluated for the assertion it supports. For example, an authorized budget supports a spending envelope, a settlement statement supports a stated closing calculation, and a payment record supports a specific transfer. None automatically establishes all the others. An accepted synthetic case record shall retain its fictional and reconstructed status rather than being labeled independent real evidence.

## 4. Proposed funding authorization form in narrative terms

The requesting function shall state the transaction ID, maximum proposed amount, purpose and exact source of the obligation. Finance shall identify the available authority and budget without equating budget with cash. Treasury shall identify the intended funding account or facility only in a separately controlled record, verify the recipient through the approved procedure and preserve the verification reference. No account number or operational endpoint shall appear in this public packet.

The authorizing body shall approve or reject the specific proposed funding, record conditions and identify any unresolved dependency. Approval shall not be inferred from the already accepted planning acquisition. Where historical settlement is being documented after the fact, the record shall explicitly say so and identify the evidence's actual creation and availability dates. A reconstructed authorization shall not be backdated to September 4.

Two distinct role reviews are proposed before releasing an instruction: Treasury verifies the funding/recipient record and Finance verifies amount, authority and accounting treatment. A person who prepares a changed recipient instruction shall not provide its sole verification. These are proposed procedural protections, not a claim that a particular control operated at the fictional closing. Material changes shall require a fresh documented review.

The $200,000 combined study forecast must not be duplicated as separate utility and survey invoices. Its ultimate allocation belongs in the accepted planning source. The Phase-I envelope is not an invoice, lender commitment or appropriation, and is not added to land consideration in a closing statement. Production IT hardware and colo service remain separate cost objects.

The land statement shall show gross consideration, supported adjustments and the resulting settlement amount only when every entered adjustment has a basis. If later evidence changes the settled amount, the reviewer shall distinguish a source correction, separate cost, timing difference or new proposed transaction. A mismatch shall not be hidden inside the construction budget or cleared by a generic miscellaneous expense.

## 6. Proposed accounting instruction

The existing development bridge debits land $3,000,000 and credits unresolved settlement clearing $3,000,000. That balanced overlay records the accepted planning acquisition without claiming cash payment, a loan or an accepted vendor payable. This packet supplies no second acquisition journal and does not authorize repeating the land debit.

If a separately accepted source later establishes the settlement counterpart, Finance shall prepare a correcting or completing entry against the existing clearing balance with its own evidence reference and effective date. The amount and account shall follow the accepted evidence. The preparer shall demonstrate that land remains recorded once and that no payment, liability or contribution is duplicated elsewhere in the enterprise books.

A proposed settlement reconciliation shall list the opening clearing balance, each supported adjustment, evidence date, posting date and remaining unresolved amount. If no evidence resolves the counterpart, the balance remains visible with an owner and next action. Aging alone is not a reason to classify it as debt, cash, payable, income or equity. The ledger's mathematical balance is not evidence of economic settlement.

## 7. Title, possession and construction reservations

Legal shall retain the mock deed's fictionality and no-real-APN statements in any derived settlement packet. Facilities shall not replace the synthetic planning envelope with a traced real parcel without accepted geometry and provenance. The coordinator shall not insert a metes-and-bounds description, easement recital or recording number solely to make this draft resemble a conventional closing package.

The accepted land event does not establish utilities, fiber commitments, permits, soil suitability or buildability. Those matters remain separate evidence and engineering dependencies. A settlement record cannot authorize vertical construction or confirm an in-service date. Building depreciation, equipment commissioning and occupancy remain outside this land-only instruction.

If an external property document is later supplied, Legal and Facilities shall identify its exact relationship to the fictional model before incorporating it. A real-world record shall not be treated as permission to assert ownership by a fictional company. The public repository shall retain only material appropriate for its synthetic case purpose and provenance boundaries.

## 8. Exceptions, amendment and review

A discrepancy in party, amount, property identity or source authority shall suspend the proposed instruction and return it to the responsible owner with a written question and the conflicting references. Ordinary documentary corrections shall preserve prior versions. Changes to acquisition economics, legal identity, financing or geographic status shall require a separately accepted decision rather than approval of this document's appearance.

Proposed review timing is a written owner update within ten business days after a material settlement evidence gap is assigned. That is a new administrative proposal, not a statutory deadline or a statement that a payment is overdue. The update shall identify the missing record, custodian, consequence and next action, without manufacturing a completion date.

The final review record shall identify accepted procedure clauses, unresolved financial fields and the exact supporting evidence. A later signed instruction shall have its actual authorization date and separately controlled recipient details. This draft remains unsigned, holds settlement unknown and preserves the accepted fictional acquisition. Its immediate use is to inspect the clearing entry, specify the evidence needed to resolve it and prevent false payment or construction assertions.
