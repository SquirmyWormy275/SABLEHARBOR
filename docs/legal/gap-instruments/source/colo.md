## 1. Incorporation and purpose

These proposed implementation schedules supplement **SH-RT-CONTRACT-001 / RT-MSA-001** and its existing Reno and Boise site orders. They do not create a second master services agreement or supersede its security, availability, liability, title or exit protections. The existing negotiated-deviation process remains controlling: a weakened protection requires an explicit clause reference and approval by Legal and the accountable risk owner. A completed operational form cannot amend the master merely by omission.

Sable Harbor, LLC remains the proposed customer identified in the existing dossier. Switch and IDACORE are selected provider brands, not verified contracting legal entities. No building assignment, reservation, provider acceptance, signed order or expenditure is established. These forms may be used to document a future quotation, acceptance and invoice, but a populated forecast field is not evidence that any of those events occurred. The source identifies procurement-pending capacity and zero reserved capacity. This supplement preserves that zero; actual accepted capacity remains unassigned. Planning requests do not establish a lease.

## 2. Reno order completion schedule

The provider's proposed order response shall identify its verified legal party, authority, selected building within the Switch TAHOE RENO Citadel Campus, enclosure identifier, usable IT load and all power delivery points. The existing 75 and 100 kW alternatives shall remain separately quoted alternatives until one is selected. Expansion to 200–250 kW requires stated lead time, price, availability condition and whether any reservation fee applies. An unpriced pathway is not an option already purchased.

The technical attachment shall show A and B feeds, each capable of the accepted load, protective-device and metering boundaries, cooling envelope, density restrictions, maintenance conditions and the accepted equipment list. Two carrier entrances and the initial 10 Gbps transport request with a 100 Gbps pathway require site-specific route evidence; brand or campus marketing is insufficient. Liquid-cooling compatibility and high-density deployment remain provider-confirmation fields. Customer equipment load shall not be doubled by summing redundant PSU ratings.

The order shall name the accepted installation milestones, technical acceptance date, billing commencement, proposed 24-month term, any explicitly agreed reservation charges and renewal selection. Until completed and accepted, none is an actual commencement date. The source $350/kW/month figure remains an analyst sensitivity, not a Switch quote; it shall not appear in a provider quotation column or payable invoice without separately supplied commercial evidence.

## 4. Commercial line-item schedule

Every completed quotation shall use one row per separately chargeable service and identify order ID, quote revision, service code, description, unit, quantity basis, currency, rate, included services, taxes, escalation, billing start, billing stop and source evidence. Required categories are enclosure/cabinet, usable power, cooling, setup, cross-connects, transit, private transport, out-of-band connectivity, remote hands, receiving/storage, deposit, prepayment, expansion reservation, cancellation, removal and migration overlap.

A blank price means unquoted. A zero price must state included or waived and identify the associated accepted rate. Power-inclusive pricing shall not receive a second utility or PUE charge without an expressly negotiated distinct service basis. The same installation shall not be recovered both in setup and an unlabelled recurring charge. Refundable deposits remain distinct from service expense and require release conditions; a modeled cash allowance is not a payment receipt.

The proposed review certificate shall record Procurement's quote completeness, Finance's calculation and tax review, Technology's delivery scope and Legal's counterparty and deviation review. A missing signature is a pending review, not inferred approval. The final accepted line schedule must retain rejected alternatives so a later reviewer can distinguish the chosen scope from capacity sensitivities without adding them together.

## 5. Technical acceptance instrument

The parties would complete one acceptance certificate per site order and defined delivery scope. It shall identify order/version, building/enclosure, asset and circuit inventory, requested and accepted usable capacity, measurement boundary, agreed equipment and test plan. Proposed test records include physical enclosure inspection, access authorization and revocation, A/B continuity at the agreed load, temperature envelope, meter completeness, carrier delivery, authenticated remote-hands workflow and receiving/custody reconciliation.

Electrical and network tests require provider approval, qualified personnel, written safety controls and rollback. This supplement does not authorize tenant staff to operate provider switchgear or remove live feeds. Each test record shall state planned procedure, actual date/time, operator, instrument, observations, pass criteria, result and source attachment. Blank results mean not performed. Simulated evidence shall be identified as simulated and cannot certify a production delivery point.

Technology and Security shall identify unresolved defects, their effect on safe use and whether partial acceptance is permitted. Acceptance of a bounded noncritical item shall state the excluded capacity and service. A failed enclosure cannot be diluted by passing results from another enclosure. A corrective retest shall retain the failed result and new evidence rather than overwrite history. Provider and customer authorized acceptance, not a generated PASS label, establishes contractual acceptance under the proposed order.

## 6. Commencement and invoice verification

Before a proposed invoice enters the payable process, Finance shall match its legal supplier, order revision, service period and line schedule to the accepted commercial record. Technical acceptance must support commencement unless an expressly accepted reservation fee applies. An installation milestone, arrival of customer equipment or forecast month is not by itself technical acceptance. No retroactive billing commencement is inferred to align the model with a provider bill.

Metered invoices shall attach meter IDs, interval dates, expected and received reading counts, completeness, average actual draw, peak and any contractual minimum or commitment. Missing intervals shall remain missing until the agreed estimation process records its method and affected dates. The verifier shall reperform quantity times rate using the accepted method, identify included power and cooling, and compare escalation to its stated anniversary and cap. Tax treatment remains an item requiring supplier documentation and review; the source does not establish a zero-tax entitlement.

The invoice review shall show gross lines, valid credits, disputed lines, supported payable amount and payment status separately. Invoice approval is not bank settlement. Customer disputes follow the existing sixty-day supported-discrepancy process and payment of undisputed amounts. A proposed correction shall retain the original invoice and supplier response; a locally altered PDF cannot become the supplier's corrected instrument.

## 7. Availability and service-credit certificate

The existing Schedule B governs eligibility, outage measurement and credit bands. This supplement supplies the evidence record: affected order/service, qualifying delivery point, first loss and stable restoration, total eligible minutes, qualifying unavailable minutes, exclusions with approvals, affected recurring-charge base, applicable band, calculated credit, submission date and provider disposition. No outage or credit is asserted by an unfilled certificate.

Power, network and application availability shall remain separate. Redundancy loss with surviving load remains an incident even where no qualifying outage occurs. Gross outage intervals and excluded intervals shall both be retained. The verifier shall count each affected interval once and shall not include unrelated healthy cabinets to dilute a failure. Source proposed credit bands remain 5%, 10%, 25% and 50%, capped at 50% of the affected recurring facility-charge base, with the original thresholds and exclusions unchanged.

A certificate requesting a credit is not proof of provider acceptance, offset or cash recovery. Record requested, accepted, invoiced and applied amounts separately. Three qualifying breaches in a rolling six months invoke only the proposed contractual termination route, subject to its actual acceptance; they do not certify that a provider has breached an executed agreement today.

## 8. Custody, incident and exit attachments

A receiving attachment shall identify shipment, package and asset serials, seal condition, custodian, handover time and discrepancies. Remote hands shall refer to an authenticated customer work order with permitted actions and explicit limits on data and keys. Facility custody grants neither logical access nor J2 authority. Security and incident timing remain as proposed in the existing master schedules; this supplement neither relaxes those targets nor claims historical compliance.

The exit attachment shall reconcile customer equipment and media, secure shipping, circuit cessation, access revocation, retained evidence, deposits and final charges during the existing proposed ninety-day coordinated transition. Customer title and key control remain intact. Deletion evidence shall specify scope, method and legal-hold exceptions. No lien, disposal, liability or insurance term is newly accepted here; material deviations return to the master negotiation register.

## 9. Completion and signature conditions

Before external use, Legal shall verify provider entity and signatory, Finance shall complete quote and tax fields, Technology shall confirm site-specific delivery and Security shall review access and evidence rights. Reliability shall identify recovery dependencies and unperformed tests. All dated result, signature, invoice number and settlement fields remain unestablished until supported by the relevant record.

The proposed schedules may be approved as templates without asserting that either provider accepted them. Actual execution must identify the accepted master, order and schedule revisions and all negotiated deviations. No new provider, alternate site, headquarters data center or customer-hosting commitment is created by completing the documentation package.
