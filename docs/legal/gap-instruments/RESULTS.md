## Content and preservation checks

Source reconciliation checks cover 237 assertions across all 17 packages.
Examples include the ARU cash/debt/fee bridge, eight retention awards and separate
consultancy, modeled ACT/365 debt convention, Red Wash membership-interest
consideration, campus land settlement, billing collection/credit/writeoff bridge,
and unactivated uranium services. The chronology instrument preserves conflicting
source dates for decision rather than inventing a resolved closing date.

The validator checks complete source text in PDF/HTML/SQLite, exact workbook
schedule cells, source/dependency hashes, all 17 gap IDs, file paths and links.
Focused tests reject missing clauses and altered amounts. Six additional
deliberate source mutations were rejected by the term checker during development.

Protected publication validation passes for all 30 approved artifacts. The
existing corporate publication builder retained all 131 hash-verified editions
without rerendering them. This task does not accept the designs held in PR #145
or the proposed billing identities in PR #138.
