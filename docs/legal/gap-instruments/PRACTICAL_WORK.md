# Case walkthroughs, reconciliations and decision import

The 17 instrument PDFs and their original source files remain the exact reviewed
bytes from commit `076485ccd954d3292fb5ad1940129027b6590284`. They are still drafts.
This extension adds review tools and public learning materials around them.

## Start with decisions

The [consolidated decision sheet](review-support/DECISIONS.md) brings together all
125 existing unresolved fields and draft terms. Use the
[workbook](review-support/decisions.xlsx) to filter related items and record your
own responses. Each item retains its stable field ID, exact structured-source
pointer and relevant draft clause. Grouping related items does not approve them
as a batch. Terms that restate accepted source facts retain their stated basis.

## Trace an amount before relying on it

The [accounting bridge](accounting/README.md) links draft clauses to the native
records that support their amounts. It distinguishes posted forecast journals,
modeled acquisition schedules, contractual authorizations and missing evidence.
A proposed contract does not create a journal entry. Different periods, entities
and scenario populations are never combined into an apparent company total.

## Practice with a complete evidence packet

The [four practice packets](practice/README.md) provide task instructions, selected
source evidence, blank working papers and separately labeled public worked
examples. Use the blank paper first. The worked examples explain calculations
and evidence limitations; they are not hidden assessment solutions or new canon.

## Follow a case into its reporting output

The [end-to-end walkthroughs](walkthroughs/README.md) trace selected transactions
through their native accounting records and identify where an evidence link stops.
The [five reconciliation workpapers](reconciliations/README.md) provide bounded
bank-clearing, receivables, payables, debt and fixed-asset exercises, with blank
papers and separately labeled public worked results. Their request lists explain
which missing records prevent a broader conclusion.

## Bring back a completed decision sheet

The [decision import guide](decision-import/README.md) explains how to validate a
filled copy of the existing review workbook and produce proposed source changes
and exact diffs. The importer writes a separate review folder. It does not change
repository sources, approve terms or infer replacements from ordinary comments.

## Compare an iteration

The [revision guide](revision-policy.md) explains how to generate clause redlines
and before/after/difference images for changed PDF pages. The comparison also
tracks HTML, workbooks, structured records and the manifest. Unchanged draft
bytes are not owner acceptance. When an actual acceptance record exists, a changed
covered file cannot inherit its earlier byte-specific acceptance.

## Offline access and the broader evidence workline

The portable bundle starts at `START_HERE.html` and retains the individual source
and publication files. Selected evidence and browser-reading companions are
included; deeper repository references are labeled online links. The
[release index](REVIEW_RELEASES.md) records availability and checksums. New bundles
belong in GitHub Releases, not as additional ZIPs in the Git tree.

The broader [evidence workbench](../../../evidence/closeout/README.md) remains the
entry point for runtime, geographic and repository-wide evidence. Its accepted PR #159
workline is preserved; this legal package does not replace its interface or
change its draft-note format. The two entry points link to the same underlying
repository sources.

New workbook and browser designs remain pending exact-file review. No user
response is inferred from the existence of a blank review cell, a passed check,
a downloaded bundle or an unchanged checksum.
