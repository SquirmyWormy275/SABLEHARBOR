# Close one ARU reporting period

**SH-CASE-CLOSE-01 · ARU_GROUP · January 2027 · base industrial forecast**

Public learning material. New brief design is draft for exact-file review.

## Situation

One reporting group has an opening trial balance, the complete month’s journal and a closing reporting package. All five reconciliation areas stay within this scope.

## Question

Can you reproduce the close and distinguish reconciled model balances from externally supported balances?

## Open these files

1. [ARU period-close instructions](../period-close/README.md) — Start with the period-close instructions and source population.
2. [ARU period-close working paper](../period-close/blank.xlsx) — Work from opening balances through activity, reconciliations and statements.
3. [Evidence-request tracking guide](../evidence-tracking/README.md) — Record follow-up evidence without changing accepted source facts.

## Submit

Submit the balanced closing trial balance, five reconciliation conclusions, proposed adjustments only where supported, and the financial statements. Identify unsupported external evidence explicitly.

## Scope limit

January 2027 is a forecast, not an actual completed close. Group reporting is not separate statutory books for every subsidiary. Do not invent aging, vouchers or adjusting entries to make the case look complete.
