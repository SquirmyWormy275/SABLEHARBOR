# Trace the Red Wash closure obligation

Public learning material. New brief design is draft for exact-file review.

## Situation

The acquisition assumes a closure liability. The successor opening and forecast closing balance reflect accretion and later movements.

## Question

Can the liability be traced across dates without treating a modeled estimate as independent engineering evidence?

## Scope limit

Escrow and holdback are consideration components, not extra purchase price or proven current cash. The calibrated cash-flow model does not independently corroborate its input liability.
