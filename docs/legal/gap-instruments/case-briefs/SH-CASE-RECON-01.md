# Practice five separate reconciliations

**SH-CASE-RECON-01 · January 2027 · base forecast · ARU_GROUP, Atlas Meridian and Willow**

Public learning material. New brief design is draft for exact-file review.

## Situation

Five selected workpapers cover bank clearing, receivables, payables, debt and one asset. They come from different reporting scopes.

## Question

Do each workpaper’s selected balances tie, and what stronger evidence is missing?

## Open these files

1. [Five-reconciliation instructions](../reconciliations/README.md) — Read the scope and evidence limitations for each case.
2. [Five-reconciliation working paper](../reconciliations/blank.xlsx) — Complete each of the five workpapers in a separate saved copy.
3. [Worked explanations](../reconciliations/WORKED.md) — Compare only after completing your calculations.

## Submit

Submit five separate reconciliation conclusions with differences and evidence requests. Do not combine these balances into a trial balance or period close.

## Scope limit

These are separate exercises. Willow covers FORT-TEST-RIG only. Modeled bank clearing is not a bank-issued statement; zero differences do not establish completeness.
