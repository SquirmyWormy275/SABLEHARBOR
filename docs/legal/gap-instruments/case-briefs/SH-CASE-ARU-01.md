# Account for the ARU acquisition

**SH-CASE-ARU-01 · ARU acquisition opening · 7 January 2026**

Public learning material. New brief design is draft for exact-file review.

## Situation

A stock purchase and refinancing establish a new opening trial balance. Fees, retained leases and buyer cash have different accounting treatments.

## Question

Do the funding bridge and opening entries agree, and what evidence would support the closing?

## Open these files

1. [Acquisition task and evidence](../practice/acquisition/TASK.md) — Read the task and exact selected evidence.
2. [Acquisition working paper](../practice/acquisition/blank.xlsx) — Complete the supplied acquisition working paper.
3. [Transaction walkthrough workbook](../walkthroughs/walkthroughs.xlsx) — Use ARU opening, Monthly trace and ARU closing only if extending into 2026.

## Submit

Submit the funding bridge, opening debit–credit check, book/tax goodwill comparison and a short evidence conclusion. Keep buyer costs separate from the ARU opening.

## Scope limit

Opening acquisition reconstruction. Post-cutoff 2026 activity is a management scenario; the separate 2027 forecast is not added to these books.
