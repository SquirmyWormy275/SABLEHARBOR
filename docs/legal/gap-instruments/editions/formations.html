1. Purpose and instrument boundary

This packet provides six separate internal consent and ownership-record drafts for the accepted industrial perimeter. It does not form a seventh company, issue a government certificate, or claim that a newly written consent existed at a historical closing. The company identities and ownership chain come from the internal formation record, entity register and industrial closeout. The chronology controls source event precision. Clauses described as proposed below are supplied for review; they do not amend accepted governing instruments merely by appearing here.

Upon separate adoption, the Corporate Secretary would retain each consent in its company's book with the actual adoption date, approving capacity, authority reference and attachments. A cross-reference to another company's approval is not that company's approval. A signatory must identify the capacity in which the person acts; a parent title alone supplies no subsidiary appointment. No reconstructed signature, seal, filing number, tax identifier or exact share count is supplied.

3. SHI — proposed member and governing-board consent

The following six consent texts become operative only upon the appropriate approving body’s actual adoption and completion of its entity-specific schedule. Each is an unexecuted draft. A proposed new subscription must be distinguished from acknowledgment of an already accepted historical interest; no duplicate issuance is authorized.

RESOLVED, subject to the completed authority and adoption record below, that the approving body acknowledges SHI as the existing parent and directs the Secretary to maintain its legal name as Sable Harbor, LLC. The stable accounting key SHI shall not be expanded into an Inc. suffix. The consent shall preserve the existing nine-member unitary board and independent Chair; it shall not add a financing, industrial or subsidiary representative seat.

The approving bodies shall confirm that the record of SHIH ownership belongs in SHI's investment register and that industrial acquisition funding must be traced through the appropriate source transaction. They shall authorize the Secretary to obtain missing certified records where needed, without treating an internal reconstruction as a state-certified copy. Before execution, Legal shall identify the governing agreement provisions and member or board approvals actually required; this draft does not decide the legal sufficiency of a vote.

SHI shall instruct Finance to keep its investment balance separate from SHIH's assets and expenses. No subsidiary permit, carrier authority, employment obligation or mine property shall transfer into SHI under this consent. A later recapitalization, distribution, guarantee or intercompany advance shall need its own economic record and authorization. Existing member rights shall continue without an inferred waiver from participation in this administrative cleanup.

4. SHIH — proposed sole-stockholder and organization consent

RESOLVED, that SHI, acting in its verified approving capacity, acknowledges the December 2, 2024 formation record and directs SHIH to maintain separate books, ownership records and delegated authorities. This is an internal continuity acknowledgment, not a second formation. Any proposed appointment instrument shall identify the office, appointee, effective date and source of delegated power separately; no unnamed officer is inserted by the drafting exercise.

SHIH shall maintain PS and ARU as separate wholly owned investments. It shall not merge their operating obligations, cash accounts or acquisition escrows. SHIH's holding purpose shall include receiving authorized parent investment and directing separately approved acquisition funding. This clause shall confer no authority to operate a railway or mine, dispatch employees, waive site safety controls or pledge subsidiary assets without the relevant company's approval.

A subscription schedule shall accompany the consent only after the Secretary establishes the authorized class, exact units or shares and issue consideration from accepted records. The accepted nominal organization concept is not a dollar amount. The register shall record those unresolved fields as unavailable, leaving a proposed issuance unposted until supplied. Parent funding already recognized in a transaction must not be issued a second time as supposed initial organization capital.

5. PS — proposed subscription and continuity consent

RESOLVED, that SHIH approves the subscription record for its entire accepted ownership interest in PS, solely to document the original organization subscription on the completed schedule; no additional interests shall be issued under this acknowledgment. PS shall accept the subscription only after its authorized recipient verifies the class, quantity, consideration, authority and receipt or valid noncash contribution basis. Until then this draft records the existing 100% relationship but does not manufacture the original subscription price or share denomination.

The organizational record shall preserve January 13, 2025 as the reconstructed formation date and distinguish the initial development roles from Evan Vilander's later presidency beginning November 3, 2025. Mari Varela's PS COO and RWH CEO capacities remain distinct offices held by one person. PS shall carry the RWH investment separately from platform payroll and service expense. Its ownership of RWH shall not substitute for RWH's permits, contracts or stop authority. Any subsequent investment contribution or service agreement shall identify amount, date, consideration and reciprocal accounting independently of the organization subscription. The consent shall expressly prohibit treating Red Wash's acquisition escrow as either PS's original subscription or ARU's later escrow.

9. Common proposed completion provisions

For each company, the Secretary would complete an instrument cover record identifying the exact approving body, actual adoption date, authority document, attached ownership ledger and unresolved exceptions. Legal would review the instrument in the correct entity form before signatures are requested. A proposed consent may be withdrawn or revised before adoption; withdrawal would not reverse the accepted ownership facts in Schedule A.

Finance would reconcile any supported capital contribution between investor and recipient before posting, stating whether it is new cash, transferred property, an existing investment or a correction. A blank amount is not zero. The Secretary would retain rejected versions and a concise supersession note, while the current book points to the approved version. No signature is requested or represented by publication of this packet.
