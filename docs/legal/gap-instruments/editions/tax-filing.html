2. Narrow official-reference check

The IRS instructions describe a joint section 338(h)(10) election and require participation by all shareholders when the target is an S corporation. They state an ordinary filing deadline on the fifteenth day of the ninth month following acquisition. Applying that stated rule to the accepted January 7, 2026 acquisition gives the already tracked October 15, 2026 date. This is a source-based calendar calculation, not a finding that eligibility, signatures or timely delivery exist. IRS Form 8023 instructions, October 2023 revision, reviewed September 12, 2026.

Form 8883 is the IRS allocation reporting instrument associated with the section 338 asset-acquisition treatment; its instructions should be consulted for the applicable reporting parties and return attachments. The internal allocation below is not a completed Form 8883. IRS Form 8883 instructions, October 2017 revision, reviewed September 12, 2026. Official sources govern actual requirements; this packet supplies no legal or tax opinion and does not reproduce a government form.

3. Proposed election-cooperation agreement

Subject to separate acceptance, SHIH and the four verified sellers would cooperate to prepare, review and, if eligibility and authority are confirmed, complete the intended joint election. Each party would provide information within its control needed to identify the acquisition, ownership, authority and agreed allocation. A party would promptly identify a material discrepancy rather than execute a statement known to conflict with its records.

The parties would designate the ARU Controller as the internal coordination owner and a qualified tax reviewer as the technical review owner. Coordination would not authorize the Controller to sign for a seller, certify another person's eligibility or waive a filing requirement. Each seller's verified signing capacity and applicable authority would be documented separately. A record that a signature packet was supplied internally would be indexed as such, not copied into a fictional IRS acknowledgment.

Proposed review timing is five business days for a party to identify discrepancies after receiving a complete review packet, with unresolved items escalated immediately if they jeopardize the tracked deadline. This is a new proposed coordination term, not a historical contract duty or an extension of any filing deadline. Silence would not be deemed consent, signature or proof of eligibility. An incomplete packet would state what is missing and who can supply it.

The parties would preserve consistent versions of the agreed acquisition facts and allocation workpaper. A change would identify the source, affected amount, reason, reviewer and consequences for each party's reporting. Neither party would require the other to use an unsupported value merely to preserve a desired tax result. Any disagreement would remain visible for qualified review before filing action.

5. Filing control and delivery workpaper

Before any authorized submission, the coordinator would identify the current official instructions, applicable deadline, correct recipients and the exact reviewed packet. A controlled submission copy would have a version, document inventory and reviewer authorization. This public workpaper does not contain tax identification numbers, private signatures or operational delivery instructions; the absence of those details prevents it from being mistaken for a ready-to-file packet.

The delivery record would distinguish preparation, technical review, authorized signature, dispatch, transmission or carrier evidence, receipt acknowledgment and any subsequent agency correspondence. Each event would carry its actual date, source reference and responsible person or role. No later event could be inferred merely because an earlier one was completed. A transmitted packet would not automatically establish a valid election, and an internally completed checklist would not establish transmission.

If evidence is unavailable, the coordinator would record the specific missing event and request the relevant original from its custodian. If the deadline appears at risk, the matter would be escalated for qualified advice; this draft offers no assumed extension, relief entitlement or backdated signature. The preserved source status would remain filing-ready unless an accepted source supports a later event.
