This is a proposed synthetic instrument for exact-content review. It has not been signed, adopted, delivered to a counterparty or made effective. Existing accepted facts cited below remain controlling; new clauses are proposed terms, not retrospective evidence.

1. Instruction and protected decision boundary

Enterprise Legal instructs a future retained qualified trademark adviser, once engagement is approved, to evaluate proposed real external use of the composite name Sable Harbor Advisory for the actual goods, services and markets specified in Schedule A. This is the proposed instruction text; no adviser has been appointed and no search has been performed under this document.

The accepted internal operating name remains Sable Harbor Advisory. The assignment is not to reopen that business-line identity or redesign its approved artwork as an administrative exercise. It is to determine what evidence, advice and decisions would be needed for external commercialization. A recommendation affecting external use must distinguish its legal/search basis from any proposed editorial canon change.

The current accepted status note records that external clearance, registrability, likelihood of confusion, filing and international clearance are not established. It preserves the preliminary screen's observation about the descriptive/weak standalone word Advisory without treating that observation as a full clearance opinion. The existing preliminary screen remains prior research, not a completed response to this instruction.

2. Scope of professional engagement

The proposed engagement shall cover the exact composite wording, the relationship to the house mark, intended presentation forms, relevant goods/services, actual target markets and channels, and the stage of use contemplated. Counsel shall identify the search scope appropriate to those facts and any significant exclusions before starting paid work. A broad label such as consulting shall not be treated as a complete statement of the intended business.

The client shall supply existing approved word/graphic references without altering their bytes. The adviser may discuss the legal implications of a presentation form but shall not create or approve replacement artwork under this engagement. Any later redesign shall require its own user review. A domain or social-handle search shall be reported as such, not described as a trademark clearance determination.

The engagement proposal shall identify adviser legal identity, responsible professional, jurisdictions covered, deliverables, fee basis, permitted third-party search costs and expected delivery date. Those fields remain unfilled. No law firm, budget or fee is invented in this public draft. Appointment and expenditure require the existing procurement/delegation route; this instruction does not create authority to engage counsel or incur fees by itself.

Counsel's accepted search specification shall identify the sources, jurisdictions, queried wording/variants, relevant goods/services, temporal cutoff and material limitations. It shall distinguish federal and state records, common-law/marketplace evidence, domains/social identifiers and relevant non-U.S. sources where included. The search scope must respond to actual intended use rather than assume that one database covers every issue.

For each relevant result, the adviser shall retain the source record identifier, observed status, wording, owner as stated by the source, goods/services, jurisdiction and retrieval date. A result's existence is not itself a conclusion about conflict. A search returning no relevant result shall state the query and limits; it shall not be converted into an unlimited statement that the name is available worldwide.

The business owner shall be able to distinguish what was searched from what was assessed. Screenshots, extracts and explanatory notes shall be linked to their source and version. A later database update shall not silently replace the record on which the earlier recommendation was based. Supplemental searches shall be labelled with their later cutoff and scope.

5. Requested advice and deliverable

The requested advice shall address the proposed use as actually described, the full composite mark and house-mark relationship, significant findings, remaining uncertainties, available responses and any recommended further search or filing strategy. It shall distinguish a search report from a reasoned clearance recommendation and from the separate outcome of any application.

Where counsel recommends a narrower goods/services statement, geographical limitation, changed presentation or delayed launch, the deliverable shall state the concrete change and its rationale. It shall not use a generic approval stamp that hides conditions. The advice shall identify material assumptions supplied by the business and the consequence if they change.

No likelihood-of-confusion analysis, registrability conclusion or legal opinion is supplied by this draft. The listed deliverables are instructions to obtain advice, not a substitute for it. The proposed process may yield a recommendation to proceed, proceed with conditions, obtain more evidence, or refrain from the proposed external use. None is selected here.

After receiving the agreed advice, the authorized business and Legal decision-makers shall record the specific proposed external use, the advice version considered, conditions, excluded uses and remaining actions. A decision to proceed shall be confined to that stated scope. It shall not imply registration or convert preliminary research into an external certificate.

7. Filing and later status records

If a filing is separately authorized, its record shall distinguish preparation, submission, acknowledgment, examination, registration, refusal, withdrawal and other actual reported states. An internal decision to file is not submission; an application is not a registration. The exact external evidence must support the state recorded. This form creates no application number, filing date, registration number, seal or government acknowledgment.

Any accepted use condition shall be carried into the relevant launch instructions. A later change in markets, goods/services, legal owner or presentation shall trigger a scoped review rather than relying indefinitely on advice for a different use. Periodic monitoring, if commissioned, shall have its own source and cost scope.

For repository delivery, the public status note may record the existence, scope and disposition of a completed review without publishing confidential advice. A synthetic exercise version must remain visibly synthetic and must not impersonate a real law firm's work. The existing status note remains controlling until an accepted successor identifies the exact external execution fact that changed.

8. Present disposition

This packet supplies a complete proposed instruction and practical search/decision record. It supplies no new search result, clear mark, legal owner for filing or external launch permission. The immediate next action is to define actual intended external use and appoint qualified counsel if real commercialization is pursued. If the universe remains an internal synthetic case, the accepted business-line name can continue in that role without inventing a clearance event merely to make the archive look complete.
