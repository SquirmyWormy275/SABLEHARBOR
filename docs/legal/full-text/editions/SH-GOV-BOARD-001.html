Director biographies

Kincaid joined in 2023 after a mining/heavy-industrial operating and governance career and became independent Chair when Daniel voluntarily separated the roles in 2024. Calder joined in 2022 as the independent reporting/control specialist; his mandate is distinct from Harry's capital mandate.

Evan Mercer is Chicago-connected and joined with Harrison Vale's 2021 financing. He asks whether deployment repeats, margins are durable, product focus holds, and the service/product relationship is disciplined. Voss joined with Wolf Ridge in 2022 and brings physical-asset, commodity, industrial-family-capital, and infrastructure judgment. Evan Mercer asks, “Does this scale?” Voss asks, “Does this survive the cycle?” Neither is a caricature or constituency veto-holder.

Galiullina was born in Ufa, Bashkir ASSR, Soviet Union, in the mid-1960s working chronology. Her late-Soviet applied-mathematics/computational-science formation survived a post-Soviet transition; she came to the United States around 2001–2002 and built a major second act at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign through research/postdoctoral/scientific-computing work and deep ties to computer science, scientific computing, and student science programs. Her posture is that computation helps understand physical reality; it does not replace it.

Van der Velde is a Dutch immigrant who grew up around DeMotte, Indiana and the agricultural Midwest. He studied feed/animal sciences at Kansas State, began in animal-feed/agricultural operations, accepted an opportunity at Harvard Business School rather than returning permanently to the farm, and moved into major ag-industrial finance/CFO leadership. His final fictional employer remains PROVISIONAL. He was the first substantial independent director in 2019.

  • 2021: Harrison Vale financing brings Evan Mercer.

Financing record

Harrison Vale Partners, a Chicago-anchored vertical enterprise/data/industrial-software growth investor, led approximately $48 million in 2021 after the Crossing-era breakthrough. Proceeds supported repeatability, scaling, productization, security/reliability, and commercial growth. The financing did not cause or rescue the Crossing.

Wolf Ridge Holdings, Chicago/Midwest permanent or very long-duration industrial capital with roots in family capital, infrastructure, resources, specialty manufacturing, and industrial technology, led approximately $135 million in 2022 at approximately $900 million post-money. There was no founder secondary; working post-round ownership was approximately 66.5% insiders and 33.5% outside capital.

Terms are genuinely balanced: no founder dictatorship, investor control, ordinary-operations veto, privileged commercial-information channel, or informal pressure through future financing.

Standing committees and charters

Each committee supports the board and does not displace lawful board or management authority. Committee chairs control agendas and report material conclusions and dissent. Investor-appointed directors act for Sable Harbor, disclose conflicts, and recuse where appropriate.

Formal separately controlled charters are published under committees/; the summaries below reconcile to those instruments.

Audit & Compliance

Members: Calder (Chair), Galiullina, Kincaid. Oversees financial reporting/control, compliance, whistleblowing, material adverse findings, and independent assurance interfaces. It does not settle the still-OPEN Internal Audit operating model.

Finance & Investment

Members: van der Velde (Chair), Voss, Evan Mercer, Daniel Mercer. Oversees capital allocation, liquidity, leverage, guarantees, major investment and acquisition economics, and financial exposure. It applies reserved matters without becoming an operating investment desk.

Technology & Operations

Members: Galiullina (Chair), Raman, Kincaid, Evan Mercer. Oversees material technology/model risk, cybersecurity posture, physical operational exposure, asset integrity, reliability, and the boundary between computation and reality.

Compensation & Human Capital

Members: Kincaid (Chair), Galiullina, van der Velde. Oversees executive compensation, succession, leadership fitness, human-capital philosophy, and cultural health, including decision-quality and respectful candor.

Governance & Nominating

Members: Kincaid (Chair), Calder, Bell. Oversees board composition, independence, conflicts, committee architecture, governance effectiveness, and nominations. It prevents continuity from becoming hereditary power.

Conflict integrity

Directors steward Sable Harbor, not appointing capital. Actual or apparent conflicts require disclosure, independent consideration, and recusal where appropriate. Harrison Vale and Wolf Ridge receive full board voice but no private operational veto, privileged commercial access, or special channel outside proper governance.

Supersession

The earlier PROVISIONAL financing/director proposal is retained as historical intellectual record and marked SUPERSEDED. It must not be used as current governance.
