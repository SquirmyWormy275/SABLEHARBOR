SABLE HARBOR ADVISORY — OPERATING NAME AND EXTERNAL CLEARANCE STATUS

Document ID: SH-LEGAL-ADV-001
Date: September 9, 2026
State: operating-name decision reconciled; external legal clearance remains execution
Related: SH-ADV-ATL-DR-003; PRELIMINARY_NAME_AND_MARK_SCREEN.md

1. Purpose

This status note prevents two different questions from being collapsed:

  1. What does the Sable Harbor business line call itself in canon and internal operating documentation?
  2. Has qualified trademark counsel cleared that name for real external commercial use?

The first question is resolved. The second is not represented as complete.

2. Operating-name decision

SH-ADV-ATL-DR-003 locks Sable Harbor Advisory as the operating/business-line name.

Accordingly, current Sable Harbor documents should not describe Advisory's business name as an open architecture question or continue to require selection of a different name merely to complete the business model.

This decision supersedes earlier open wording to that extent.

3. What the decision does not establish

The operating-name decision is not:

  • a U.S. trademark registration;
  • an attorney clearance opinion;
  • a registrability conclusion;
  • a likelihood-of-confusion opinion;
  • a freedom-to-operate conclusion;
  • a decision that ADVISORY alone is distinctive or protectable;
  • a completed international clearance search.

The September 1 preliminary screen remains a research aid and correctly observes that Advisory standing alone is highly descriptive/weak. That observation is compatible with using Sable Harbor Advisory as the accepted operating name while still requiring counsel review before real external launch.

4. External execution required

Before real external commercialization under the name, the operating business should obtain appropriate legal review of:

  • the full composite name Sable Harbor Advisory;
  • the relationship between the house mark and descriptive Advisory wording;
  • relevant consulting, software, analytics, technology and industrial-service goods/services;
  • federal and state records;
  • common-law and marketplace use;
  • domains and social identifiers where material;
  • filing/house-mark strategy if protection is sought;
  • relevant non-U.S. markets where commercial activity is planned.

Actual scope should be set by qualified counsel based on intended use.

5. Documentation rule

Current source documents should use the following distinction:

  • LOCKED operating name: Sable Harbor Advisory.
  • OPEN execution task: external trademark/legal clearance and any resulting filing or branding implementation.

Do not revert the operating-name question to OPEN merely because legal clearance remains outstanding. Conversely, do not describe the name as legally cleared merely because the repository has accepted it as canon.
