## Parent election: evidence and timing test

An LLC may elect corporate federal classification on Form 8832 without converting its state-law identity. Under the [IRS Form 8832 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i8832) and [Treasury Regulation § 301.7701-3(c)(1)(iii)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-301.7701-3), an ordinary election's effective date is no more than 75 days before filing or 12 months after it. The [IRS's 2009 late-election procedure](https://www.irs.gov/irb/2009-39_IRB) provided specified relief within three years and 75 days of the intended effective date, subject to consistent-return and reasonable-cause conditions. The [IRS late-election guidance](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/late-election-relief) describes further bounded routes and a private-letter-ruling route when simplified relief is unavailable.

**Application to the presented facts:** the fictional April 2016 submission would fall within the ordinary window *if an actual contemporaneous filing existed*. No such actual filing, acknowledgement or return history is in the repository. A first real request made in September 2026 for an April 2016 effective date would be outside both the ordinary 75-day window and the cited three-year-and-75-day simplified relief period. That does not prove the real classification is different, establish reasonable cause, or decide whether a ruling could be obtained. It means the synthetic acknowledgement cannot be used as evidence for real tax status. A real taxpayer and authorized tax adviser would need to test original records and any applicable relief route before making a real filing or historical-status claim.

The [ARU Form 8023 cooperation draft](gap-instruments/source/tax-filing.md) concerns a different entity and intended acquisition election. It does not supply SHI's Form 8832 evidence. No real tax identifier, signer, payment instruction or agency receipt should be added to the public synthetic archive to make this table appear complete.
