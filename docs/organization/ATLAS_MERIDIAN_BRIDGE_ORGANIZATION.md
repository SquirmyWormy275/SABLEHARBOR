## Interpretation

This chart is retained because the bridge program is part of Atlas Meridian's canon lineage. It must **not** be read as the current Atlas organization after September 8, 2026.

The bridge joined Willow lineage, Foundry representation, product leadership and cross-functional contributors long enough to establish what could repeat and where product boundaries belonged. That historical function remains valid.

The current organization is controlled by:

- [`WILLOW_AND_ATLAS_MERIDIAN.md`](WILLOW_AND_ATLAS_MERIDIAN.md), version 0.3.0;
- [`SH-ADV-ATL-DR-001`](../canon/ADVISORY_ATLAS_MERIDIAN_CLOSEOUT_2026-09-08.md);
- [`SH-ATL-016`](../advisory/ATLAS_MERIDIAN_PROFESSIONAL_PLATFORM.md).

Under the current design, Atlas Meridian's dedicated personnel are a **product-development organization**. They build and operate Atlas Meridian as a commercial enterprise product and as the professional platform used by Sable Harbor Advisory. They are not an Advisory consulting bench.

## Historical boundaries preserved

- Atlas emerged from Foundry representation, Willow experimentation, Hound/Ranger, the investigation gauntlet, field validation and the twelve-month bridge.
- Simone Vale's transition/product role remains part of that bridge history.
- “Investigate, do not merely answer” remains the product insight.
- Human decision ownership remains required.
- The old bridge diagram is not deleted or redrawn as though the 2026 product organization had always existed.
