# SABLE HARBOR — ORGANIZATION CHART GOVERNANCE

**Version:** 0.2.0  
**Canonical date:** August 31, 2026  
**Status:** Derived-document governance  
**Scope:** Repository README, repository documentation, and public wiki organization diagrams

## 1. Authority order

Organization charts summarize accepted canon. They do not create canon by visual implication.

1. detailed Blackridge canon, when present, for Blackridge-specific facts;
2. `SABLE_HARBOR_CANONICAL_ARCHITECTURE_HANDOVER.md` for inherited Sable Harbor context;
3. `SABLE_HARBOR_CORPORATE_LORE_CANON_v0.2.md` for the reconciled corporate-lore layer;
4. `DECISION_REGISTER.md` and the continuity audit;
5. `ORGANIZATION_MAP_REGISTER.json`;
6. repository diagrams;
7. wiki reproductions and summaries.

A chart that conflicts with a controlling source must be corrected. It does not supersede canon merely because it is easier to read.

## 2. Required chart metadata

Every diagram must state:

- chart ID and version;
- canonical date represented;
- map type;
- exact meaning of its edges;
- controlling canon sections or decision IDs;
- material OPEN, PROVISIONAL, HISTORICAL, or EMERGING elements.

## 3. Approved relationship vocabulary

Use explicit labels drawn from the canon:

- `underlying substrate`;
- `commercial product built on the substrate`;
- `product expression`;
- `represents the terrain`;
- `experimental lineage`;
- `bounded experimental capability`;
- `documented membership`;
- `documented contribution`;
- `program or operating leadership`;
- `domain authority`;
- `independent challenge`;
- `institutional seam`;
- `field-qualification gate`;
- `operating ownership`;
- `owns and operates in the narrative canon`;
- `acquired operator`;
- `operating component`;
- `external evidence source`;
- `historical absorption`;
- `method transfer`.

Use `reports to` only after the applicable reporting-line decision is explicitly locked.

## 4. Prohibited visual shortcuts

Do not:

- use a conventional top-down executive tree to imply reporting lines that remain open;
- place a unit inside another unit's box unless canon supports containment or an operating component relationship;
- collapse **Foundry** and **Foundry Field** into one object when the distinction matters;
- collapse **Pale Sun** and **Red Wash** into one object;
- collapse **ARU** and **BS&T** into one object;
- turn Rachel Sloane's institutional seam into supervision of Gid or Willow;
- represent Maya's independent challenge as subordinate product approval;
- represent Marcus's technical authority as a large management empire;
- show Evalon or Emberline as standalone August 31, 2026 divisions;
- show Advisory as fully formed;
- show Blackridge as a Sable Harbor business unit or the Original Eight as a Blackridge team;
- show Cradle as owning its host mine by default;
- present Walt Sutter as a Sable Harbor employee or oracle;
- present the provisional Northstar Resources or Martin Shaw names as settled current organization;
- replace OPEN leaders, titles, legal forms, or reporting lines with plausible inventions;
- use the prior provisional operating-model tree as controlling without reconciliation.

## 5. Solid, dashed, and undirected lines

Each chart defines its own edge semantics, but the package uses these defaults:

- **solid directed line:** documented lineage, operating ownership, acquisition, program leadership, or product relationship;
- **dashed directed line:** institutional interface, contribution, external evidence relationship, emerging practice, or method transfer;
- **solid undirected line:** documented membership or association where a reporting direction is not established.

The arrow's direction indicates the direction of the named relationship, not rank.

## 6. Legal and operating language

The canon states that Red Wash operates through a dedicated legal operating company while Pale Sun remains its strategic/business-line identity. Exact entity name, suffix, jurisdiction, financing, and transaction chain remain OPEN. Charts must preserve both truths.

Likewise, Sable Harbor acquired ARU and ARU remains a distinct operating company during integration, but the exact ownership chain between Sable Harbor, ARU, and BS&T remains open.

## 7. Wiki synchronization

Every wiki reproduction should include:

- repository source path;
- chart ID, version, and canonical date;
- the edge interpretation;
- a statement that the repository controls;
- all OPEN, PROVISIONAL, HISTORICAL, and EMERGING labels shown in the source.

Relationship changes must be proposed in the repository first.

## 8. Change review

Reject a chart update when it:

- adds a person, title, unit, subsidiary, or reporting line without source support;
- removes a status qualifier;
- turns documented participation into supervision;
- makes an OPEN structure appear settled;
- breaks the distinction among representation, deployment, experimentation, investigation, operation, recovery, logistics, and method transfer;
- turns a cultural or narrative relationship into legal ownership;
- conflicts with the register without updating and explaining the register change.
