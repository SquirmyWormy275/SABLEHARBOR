# SABLE HARBOR — ORGANIZATION CHART GOVERNANCE

**Version:** 0.1.0  
**Status:** Derived-document governance  
**Scope:** Repository and wiki organization diagrams

## 1. Authority

Organization charts are presentation artifacts. They summarize accepted canon but do not create canon by visual implication. The controlling order is:

1. versioned corporate canon;
2. the decision register and continuity audit;
3. the machine-readable organization map register;
4. these repository diagrams;
5. wiki reproductions and summaries.

A chart that conflicts with a controlling canon document must be corrected. It does not supersede the canon merely because it is easier to read.

## 2. Required labels

Every organization diagram must state:

- chart ID and version;
- canonical date represented;
- whether it is a legal, reporting, operating, authority, or historical map;
- what its edges mean;
- controlling source documents or decision IDs;
- material OPEN or PROVISIONAL elements.

## 3. Prohibited visual shortcuts

Do not:

- use a top-down tree to imply reporting lines that are not locked;
- put a unit inside another unit's box unless ownership or containment is supported;
- convert an institutional interface into a manager/subordinate relationship;
- omit “historical,” “emerging,” or “OPEN” labels to make the company look cleaner;
- show Evalon or Emberline as standalone August 31, 2026 divisions;
- show Advisory as fully formed;
- imply that Blackridge is a Sable Harbor business unit;
- treat the Original Eight as the Blackridge team;
- collapse Red Wash, ARU, and BS&T into software products;
- use the prior provisional operating-model tree as controlling without reconciliation.

## 4. Relationship vocabulary

Approved edge meanings include:

- `owns and operates`;
- `operating component`;
- `acquired operator`;
- `commercial product and substrate`;
- `bounded experimental capability`;
- `controlled product bridge`;
- `program lead`;
- `institutional seam`;
- `domain authority`;
- `experimental lineage`;
- `method transfer`;
- `historical absorption`.

The unqualified label `reports to` may be used only after the applicable reporting-line decision is locked.

## 5. Wiki synchronization

The wiki may reproduce Mermaid blocks from these files or embed rendered derivatives. Every wiki page should include:

- the source repository path;
- the chart version and canonical date;
- a statement that the repository controls;
- the chart's interpretation warning.

Wiki edits that change relationships must be proposed in the repository first.

## 6. Change review

A chart update should be rejected when it:

- adds a person, title, unit, subsidiary, or reporting line without a source decision;
- removes a status qualifier;
- makes an OPEN structure appear settled;
- breaks the distinction between representation, experimentation, investigation, operation, recovery, logistics, and method transfer;
- turns a narrative or cultural relationship into legal ownership;
- introduces hidden benchmark truth or nonpublic evaluation material.
