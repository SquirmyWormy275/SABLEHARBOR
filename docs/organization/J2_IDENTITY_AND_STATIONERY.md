# SABLE HARBOR — J2 IDENTITY AND STATIONERY

**Version:** 0.1.2
**Decision date:** September 2, 2026
**Repository reconciliation date:** September 5, 2026
**Status:** LOCKED identity; dedicated stationery implementation OPEN

## Identity

**J2** stands for **Judgment & Junction**.

J2 is an internal Sable Harbor enterprise directorate and institutional capability. It is not a separate legal entity and is not a customer-facing business line merely because it has its own identity system.

## Official logos

Two identity assets are approved and controlling, each retained in both vector and rendered raster form:

1. **J2 Mark** — three nested, bottom-aligned black circles crossed by a vertical black centerline.
   - Controlling PNG: `assets/brand/logos/j2__mark.png`
   - Vector derivative: `assets/brand/logos/j2__mark.svg`
2. **J2 Primary Horizontal** — the approved J2 mark, black divider, and block red `J2` wordmark.
   - Controlling PNG: `assets/brand/logos/j2__primary-horizontal.png`
   - Vector derivative: `assets/brand/logos/j2__primary-horizontal.svg`

The controlling PNGs are installed byte-for-byte from the user-approved source files. They control over every derivative, preview, package copy, or stationery rendering.

These two designs were approved by the user on September 2, 2026 and supersede all exploratory J2 logo mockups. Every exploratory concept or mockup is historical only—not a production identity asset—and must not appear in a production manifest, collateral package, letterhead, or publication.

## Stationery implementation state

J2 remains eligible for controlled stationery within the Sable Harbor corporate
document system, but the current repository distributes no dedicated J2 letterhead
template and no J2 stationery manifest. This implementation/package state does not
change the locked identity decision or make J2 a separate legal entity.

Any future J2 stationery release must follow `SH-GOV-DOC-001`, be separately
versioned, proofed, manifested, and published, and reference
`assets/brand/logos/j2__primary-horizontal.png` directly. It may not embed a redraw
or point to exploratory artwork.

## Use rules

- Use the **mark** for small-format identity, portals, badges, internal application furniture, org charts, and document furniture.
- Use the **primary horizontal** logo for J2 headers, briefings, letterhead, covers, internal portals, and formal J2 publications.
- Use the approved PNG as the controlling artwork; SVG is a vector derivative only.
- Do not replace the approved mark with generic intelligence, military, eye, target, radar, shield, compass, brain, network, or AI iconography.
- Do not recolor the black mark or substitute another red without a formal identity revision.
- Do not add slogans, seals, gradients, shadows, bevels, or decorative effects to the production identity.
- J2 documents remain Sable Harbor documents and must not imply legal independence.

## Relationship to the organization build

The identity package is intentionally established before the full J2 organization chart and operating model are complete. Later J2 organizational artifacts should reference these assets rather than recreate or reinterpret the identity.
