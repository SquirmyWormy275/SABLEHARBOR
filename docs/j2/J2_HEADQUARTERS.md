# J2 HEADQUARTERS DOCTRINE

**Document ID:** `SH-J2-HQ-001` | **Version:** 1.0.0 | **Effective:** September 2, 2026 | **State:** LOCKED DIRECTION

J2 Headquarters exists so Contact, Judgment, Orientation, JAG, and Education remain one institution rather than disconnected expert teams. It is deliberately small. Its legitimacy comes from stewardship and professional rigor, not a large command tower.

HQ is custodian of J2 doctrine, professional standards, training standards, Daedalus doctrine, cross-loop priorities, collection-priority arbitration when capacity conflicts, profession/talent stewardship, and institutional continuity. It ensures handoffs preserve context, recurring learning crosses loops, and local optimization does not fracture the whole.

Orientation sets broad enterprise intelligence priorities; Judgment Officers own problem-specific requirements; Contact owns collection method. HQ intervenes only where requirements conflict, scarce collection capacity requires choice, doctrine is disputed, or enterprise continuity is at risk. It does not replace those accountabilities with centralized tasking.

Earlier accepted direction contemplates a Chief Judgment & Junction Officer with administrative reporting to Daniel and protected board access. The existence and need for protected access are LOCKED DIRECTION. Exact title, reporting-line detail, deputy arrangement, HQ billets, and physical footprint remain OPEN. No named leader is invented.

HQ owns professional standards without turning J2 into a credential bureaucracy. It protects Judgment curiosity, Orientation independence, Contact provenance, JAG field truth, and Education quality. It also protects operating units from J2 mission creep: J2 does not command, punish, conduct personnel actions, or silently become Internal Audit.
