## Mission

J2 maintains an enterprise-level apparatus for intelligence, judgment, orientation, education, and learning worthy of Immaculate Intelligence: Sable Harbor asks the same important questions even when it does not share the same answers.

## Organization

J2 Headquarters is small and owns doctrine, professional/training standards, Daedalus doctrine, cross-loop priorities, scarce-collection arbitration, and continuity. Earlier direction for a Chief Judgment & Junction Officer reporting administratively to Daniel with protected board access remains LOCKED DIRECTION; exact title, reporting detail, deputies, billets, and footprint remain OPEN.

The three loops are **Contact** (reality to evidence), **Judgment** (evidence to warranted belief), and **Orientation** (judgment to enterprise reorientation and new questions). **Junction Advisory Group** and **Education** cross-cut the loops. This is a human context-and-responsibility system, not a queue or LLM pipeline.

## Boundaries

J2 does not command operations, vote for the board, replace line strategy, become Internal Audit, or create a parallel lore system. Orientation owns Canon and the EIB; human Judgment Officers own judgments; human Orientation Officers issue Semaphore traffic. Alexandria preserves and connects the work.

## Scale and open matters

Six active five-person JAG teams and approximately 20–25 commissioned Orientation Officers are accepted working scale. No final total J2 headcount is implied. Named permanent leaders, Contact/Judgment/Education/HQ staffing, and exact Orientation rank nomenclature remain OPEN/PROVISIONAL as registered in canon.
