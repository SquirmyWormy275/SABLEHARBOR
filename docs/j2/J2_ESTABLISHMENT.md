## Design principles

This is a design establishment, not an instruction to maximize occupancy. J2 deliberately carries professional depth, particularly in Contact, so that capability is available before a requirement becomes urgent.

Headquarters support is first class but subordinate to mission. J2 should be unusually rewarding to work in because its people receive excellent tools, administrative support, research resources, education, travel and field support, fellowships, sabbaticals, and strong workplace/hospitality support. These benefits do not alter professional independence or create line authority.

Contact uses a consulting-bench model rather than permanent problem silos. Judgment Officers retain individual problem ownership. Orientation remains a small commissioned profession. JAG remains six rotating five-person teams. Education is primarily enterprise-wide while also serving as the J2 schoolhouse and conference center.

Material changes to this structure require explicit canon action. Administrative convenience alone may not silently convert J2 into a larger command bureaucracy, an audit function, or a line-management apparatus.
