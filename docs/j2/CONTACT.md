# CONTACT — COLLECTION DOCTRINE

**Document ID:** `SH-J2-CONTACT-001` | **Version:** 1.0.0 | **State:** LOCKED DIRECTION
**Owner:** Contact | **Related:** collection management, Judgment Watch, JAG | **Cross-reference:** `CONTACT_COLLECTION_MANAGEMENT.md`; `JUDGMENT.md`

Contact is a collection apparatus, not a think tank and not self-directing. Orientation sets broad priority questions; Judgment Officers own problem-specific requirements/RFIs; HQ arbitrates scarce capacity; Contact selects lawful methods, sources, timing, and provenance and hands evidence onward without converting retrieval into institutional judgment.

Six disciplines are controlled: **systems collection** (queries, extracts, logs, operational/finance/Foundry sources); **human collection** (structured elicitation and time-bounded directed asks); **JAG collection** (field artifacts, observation, interviews, context); **external collection** (requirement-driven lawful OSINT, regulatory, market, commercial, expert sources); **exploitation** (incidents, bids, maintenance, interventions, capital decisions, outages, and failures while memory/artifacts are fresh); and **directed observation/evidence creation** (controlled tests, new measurement, structured observation/data capture/interview programs).

Collection management tracks active requirements, methods, clocks, gaps, status, and evidentiary handoff. Flash/Current/Active/Standing labels remain PROVISIONAL. Success means gaps closed, relevance, provenance, timeliness, and discriminating value—not report volume. Universal surveillance, fishing expeditions, and raw-event hoarding violate doctrine.
