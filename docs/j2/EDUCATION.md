# J2 EDUCATION

**Document ID:** `SH-J2-EDU-001` | **Version:** 1.0.0 | **State:** LOCKED
**Owner:** J2 Education | **Related:** Orientation, Canon, EIB, JAG, decision quality | **Cross-reference:** `ORIENTATION.md`; `../governance/DECISION_QUALITY_AND_AFTER_ACTION_LEARNING.md`

Education is a separate J2 arm tightly coupled to Orientation. It owns teaching practice: deep corporate and role education, business-line literacy, learning paths, cases, lessons learned, mandatory compliance education, and the conversion of important learning into usable instruction.

Legal, Safety, HR, and Compliance may own requirements, subject matter, and required legal content. J2 Education owns pedagogy, instructional/delivery design, relevance, engagement, and connection to real work. Sexual-harassment, workplace-violence, safety, compliance, assurance, and technical education receive the same standard of excellence; mandatory never means intentionally lifeless.

## Educational constitution

Education makes Sable Harbor legible to its people. Institutional literacy includes why the company exists, how its business lines actually operate, where authority sits, how evidence becomes judgment, how physical reality constrains models, and how lessons change institutional behavior. Deep learning and mandatory learning belong to one profession because both require respect for the learner and fidelity to reality.

Education converts doctrine and operational learning into instruction without claiming ownership of the underlying law, safety rule, control, technical truth, or business decision. Subject-matter owners approve required content. Education challenges incoherent, irrelevant, or pedagogically ineffective material and designs delivery, practice, assessment, reinforcement, and refresh.

## Learning products

The arm maintains role pathways, business-line literacy, technical deep dives, cases, field-derived lessons, leadership learning, and mandatory curricula. A case preserves uncertainty and decision context rather than flattening history into a moral with an obvious answer. When lawful, learners encounter the evidence, assumptions, dissent, and consequences that made the situation real.

Learning effectiveness is not completion alone. Evidence may include recall, application, changed field behavior, reduced repeated error, better escalation, improved question quality, and learner feedback. Education does not manipulate these into vanity metrics.

## Relationship to Orientation and JAG

Orientation identifies changes in institutional understanding that require education. JAG returns field context, reusable practices, failed interventions, and teaching material through human handoff. Education may support the final stewardship phase of a JAG tour and may send instructors into operating environments to understand the work being taught.

The exact Education staffing model, course platform, and detailed talent architecture remain OPEN. Those implementation choices cannot dilute the locked ownership of pedagogy.
