# J2 EDUCATION

**Document ID:** `SH-J2-EDU-001` | **Version:** 1.1.0 | **Effective:** September 3, 2026 | **State:** LOCKED
**Owner:** J2 Education | **Related:** Orientation, Canon, EIB, JAG, decision quality | **Cross-reference:** `ORIENTATION.md`; `ORIENTATION_OFFICER_PROFESSION.md`; `../governance/DECISION_QUALITY_AND_AFTER_ACTION_LEARNING.md`

Education is a separate J2 arm tightly coupled to Orientation. Its primary mission is enterprise-wide education, while it also serves as the professional schoolhouse for J2 itself. Education is both an institution and a physical place: a serious learning campus and conference center with classrooms, residential capacity, case rooms, mock executive and board environments, and space for convening across Sable Harbor.

Education owns teaching practice: deep corporate and role education, business-line literacy, learning paths, cases, lessons learned, mandatory compliance education, executive formation, J2 professional formation, exercises, and the conversion of important learning into usable instruction.

Legal, Safety, HR, and Compliance may own requirements, subject matter, and required legal content. J2 Education owns pedagogy, instructional/delivery design, relevance, engagement, and connection to real work. Sexual-harassment, workplace-violence, safety, compliance, assurance, and technical education receive the same standard of excellence; mandatory never means intentionally lifeless.

## Four missions

1. **Enterprise education.** Make Sable Harbor legible to its people and raise institutional literacy across business lines and headquarters.
2. **Executive formation.** Give newly appointed senior executives, business heads, corporate officers, and selected headquarters leaders a common grounding in Sable Harbor's institutions, decision system, history, responsibilities, and realities.
3. **J2 professional formation.** Train and continuously develop Contact, Judgment, Orientation, JAG, and Education professionals, including specialist schools, exercises, apprenticeships, and continuing professional education.
4. **Convening.** Operate a serious enterprise conference center where difficult problems, cross-business learning, after-action work, research, and professional communities can meet away from normal headquarters rhythms.

## Educational constitution

Education makes Sable Harbor legible to its people. Institutional literacy includes why the company exists, how its business lines actually operate, where authority sits, how evidence becomes judgment, how physical reality constrains models, and how lessons change institutional behavior. Deep learning and mandatory learning belong to one profession because both require respect for the learner and fidelity to reality.

Education converts doctrine and operational learning into instruction without claiming ownership of the underlying law, safety rule, control, technical truth, or business decision. Subject-matter owners approve required content. Education challenges incoherent, irrelevant, or pedagogically ineffective material and designs delivery, practice, assessment, reinforcement, and refresh.

## Program families

Education maintains five standing program families:

- **Sable Harbor Formation:** institutional literacy for headquarters staff, selected business leaders, and other roles whose work requires understanding the enterprise beyond a single function.
- **Executive Formation:** a residential, case-based program for newly appointed senior leaders. A one-week course is the default design point, with extensions where role complexity warrants.
- **J2 Professional Education:** entry formation and continuing education for Contact, Judgment, Orientation, JAG, and Education staff.
- **Enterprise Professional Programs:** advanced courses in reasoning, evidence, decision quality, postmortems, business-line literacy, technical subjects, and other capabilities that materially improve institutional performance.
- **Convenings and Exercises:** conferences, simulations, cross-business problem sessions, case immersions, and controlled decision exercises.

Residential attendance is not universal. It is expected for J2 entrants, new corporate officers, business heads, and selected headquarters roles where the value of immersion justifies removal from ordinary work.

## Learning products

The arm maintains role pathways, business-line literacy, technical deep dives, cases, field-derived lessons, leadership learning, and mandatory curricula. A case preserves uncertainty and decision context rather than flattening history into a moral with an obvious answer. When lawful, learners encounter the evidence, assumptions, dissent, and consequences that made the situation real.

Blackridge is part of the institutional case canon and may be used both openly and in disguised form where preserving uncertainty improves learning. Education may also build high-fidelity simulated boardrooms, executive offices, operations rooms, interview rooms, and crisis environments for professional formation and Orientation Assessment and Selection.

Learning effectiveness is not completion alone. Evidence may include recall, application, changed field behavior, reduced repeated error, better escalation, improved question quality, and learner feedback. Education does not manipulate these into vanity metrics.

## Staffing and faculty model

The permanent Education establishment is approximately **35 billets**. The permanent core includes the Head of Education, deputy leadership, senior faculty, instructors, exercise and case designers, program/registrar staff, learning operations, and campus/program support.

The permanent core is intentionally supplemented by rotating practitioners from Contact, Judgment, Orientation, JAG, headquarters functions, and operating businesses. Education does not build a giant detached faculty when current practitioners can teach from real work. Rotating faculty remain subject-matter contributors; Education retains ownership of pedagogy and instructional quality.

The campus itself may support substantially more people than the permanent thirty-five-person establishment because facilities, hospitality, events, and shared enterprise services can scale independently of the teaching corps.

## Relationship to Orientation and JAG

Orientation identifies changes in institutional understanding that require education. JAG returns field context, reusable practices, failed interventions, and teaching material through human handoff. Education may support the final stewardship phase of a JAG tour and may send instructors into operating environments to understand the work being taught.

Education owns the Assessment and Selection environment for Orientation candidates under the standards of the Orientation profession. It maintains the physical and instructional infrastructure, role-player pool, case library, safeguards, and exercise integrity while Orientation owns admission standards and final commissioning decisions.
