# ALEXANDRIA PROVENANCE AND LINEAGE

**Owner:** Orientation / Alexandria stewardship | **Related:** temporal integrity, connection, search, Daedalus | **Cross-reference:** `TEMPORAL_INTEGRITY.md`; `INSTITUTIONAL_MEMORY_AND_CONNECTION.md`; `SEARCH_AND_HISTORICAL_RECONSTRUCTION.md`

Nothing important should require blind trust. “Source that” must work from Canon, EIB, Judgment, decision, Semaphore traffic, or Daedalus synthesis toward underlying evidence wherever the user's access permits.

Lineage preserves the originating artifact or observation; collector and method; relevant event/record/access times; transformations; citations and references; author and accountable judgment owner; confidence and assumptions; supporting, contrary, missing, contested, and considered/rejected evidence; corrections; supersession; and downstream reliance.

Provenance is not a single “source URL.” It makes clear what a source is authoritative for and where authority ends. A finance ledger may establish what was booked without proving physical occurrence; a field observation may establish local conditions without proving enterprise prevalence; an expert may provide a hypothesis without institutional authority.

Dead ends remain useful. An abandoned hypothesis, rejected source, or superseded chain stays historically navigable when lawful because it explains why Sable Harbor once believed something and prevents repeated unproductive work.

Machine intelligence may suggest relationships, anomalies, or likely lineage. A human must decide whether those connections enter an institutional record. Daedalus visibly distinguishes source-supported statements, direct quotations/retrieval, transformations, and Daedalus inference. Where a user cannot access evidence, the system may reveal the existence and relevance of restricted material only when disclosure policy allows; provenance must not become a side channel.

Forward trace answers what later relied on an assumption, judgment, or decision. Backward trace reconstructs the evidence and reasoning supporting it. Branch views show competing interpretations and supersession without flattening disagreement into one folder or one “latest” file.
