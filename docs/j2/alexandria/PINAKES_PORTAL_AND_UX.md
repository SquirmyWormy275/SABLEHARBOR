Pinakes—named for Callimachus's catalog of Alexandria—is the **human-facing catalog and portal**, not primarily an invisible ontology. The earlier Pharos main-portal concept is SUPERSEDED.

The nine doors are: **EIB Archive** (every issued brief across time); **Finance** (authorized finance/analysis); **Judgment** (live/closed problems and judgments); **JAG Knowledge** (deployments, interventions, follow-up and reusable lessons); **Semaphore** (traffic corpus); **Canon** (current and historical institutional understanding); **Business Lines** (Foundry/Field, Willow, Atlas Meridian, Pale Sun/Red Wash, Cradle, ARU/BS&T, Advisory, Emberline history, and canon-supported lines without implying entities); **Collection** (authorized underlying Contact material); and **Education** (mandatory/deep learning, literacy, cases, pathways).

## Employee journey

An employee opens current or historical EIB, follows an item into its portal, and—where authorized—can source it, trace backward/forward, see correction/supersession, related judgments/decisions, timelines, branches, and point-in-time comparisons. Search spans full text, topics, time, business line, status, decisions, problems, and references. Rabbit holes are a feature.

Daedalus accompanies the user across doors and maintains a user-side working set. It can hold, pull, trace, reconstruct, challenge, compare, explore, and visualize without touching authoritative sources. Restricted detail is never disclosed through synthesis or inference.

The machine registry is `../structured/pinakes_portals.json`; it supports this human experience and is not a premature ontology.

## Catalog, not dashboard

Pinakes answers: Where can I enter the institutional record, what kind of material is there, who stewards it, what may I see, and how can I follow it? It does not reduce the company to executive metrics, arrange information by prestige, or pretend that nine tiles are the institution itself. It is the catalog and threshold of Alexandria.

The nine doors exist because they are the actual institutional environments Sable Harbor designed—not because nine is an ontology requirement. Adding, removing, or renaming a door therefore requires an institutional decision and registry change, not a UI preference.

## The nine doors

**EIB Archive** preserves every issued brief with its contemporary links and later supersession. **Finance** enters finance records and analytical environments under appropriate controls. **Judgment** exposes live and closed Problems, judgments, dissent, and appropriate work context. **JAG Knowledge** carries deployments, interventions, follow-up, field lessons, and reusable practices.

**Semaphore** enters durable traffic and its threads. **Canon** enters current best institutional understanding and its history. **Business Lines** enters Foundry/Foundry Field, Willow, Atlas Meridian, Pale Sun/Red Wash, Cradle, ARU/BS&T, Advisory, Emberline history, and other supported operating worlds without implying separate legal entities. **Collection** enters entitled raw/source material and is most heavily gated. **Education** enters required and deep learning, business literacy, cases, and pathways.

## Continuity of experience

From every item, authorized users can source it, trace backward and forward, see correction/supersession, inspect related judgments/decisions, compare time, and open relevant business or learning context. Navigation keeps the user aware of authoritative state, historical point, restrictions, and whether material is institutional or Daedalus-generated.

Daedalus accompanies rather than occupies a door. Its working set can cross portals within disclosure authority, but it cannot alter portal records or become a tenth system. The authoritative item remains untouched while the user explores.

## Employee acceptance standard

A new employee who knows only a plain-language concern must be able to enter Pinakes, discover the current enterprise questions, locate relevant institutional records, recognize access limits, follow provenance, view the controlled publication, and identify the human owner. If specialist repository knowledge is required, Pinakes has failed its human purpose.
