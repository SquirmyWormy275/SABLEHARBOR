Each user receives credentials, an individual Daedalus instance/context, and persistent segregated memory. Years of lawful use should create an exceptionally refined assistant for that person without cross-pollinating conversations, working sets, copied material, private synthesis, or user-specific memory.

System, model, and skill updates may change behavior globally. They do not merge user memory or content. Users may deliberately share content-free techniques, settings patterns, prompt/behavior practices, Markdown skill files, and workflow templates. A shareable package may not include private memory, copied Alexandria extracts, user conversations, or derived content. The principle is **share practice without bypassing discovery**.

## Investigative verbs

- **Hold:** maintain a user-controlled working set, such as “hold this cable.”
- **Pull:** retrieve potentially relevant material across entitled Alexandria space when the perfect query is unknown.
- **Trace/lineage:** move backward to evidence or forward to later reliance.
- **Historical reconstruction:** return to the institution's actual state of knowledge on a specified date without silent hindsight.
- **Challenge:** try to disprove a position by surfacing contradiction, dissent, strange assumptions, and overlooked evidence.
- **Explore:** identify anomalies and weak/unexpected connections without pretending they are conclusions.
- **Compare:** compare periods, business lines, judgments, decisions, assumptions, and branches.
- **Visualize:** build timelines, branching histories, connection maps, evidence lineage, and Git-like branches of institutional understanding.

Alexandria must remain fully usable through an ordinary desktop interface, with future augmented-reality (AR) and virtual-reality (VR) integration planned and supported by its architecture. Investigative visualization follows that direction without requiring headsets or spatial hardware for ordinary use. AR/VR is not required for v0.1, and no device, vendor, implementation, or delivery date is promised. This is not a claim of existing AR/VR capability.

All interfaces preserve the same access, disclosure, provenance, temporal-integrity, and authority boundaries. The workspace may persist user-selected synthetic artifacts, all marked non-authoritative and outside Alexandria. User export preserves marking, provenance references, and access restrictions. There is no automatic import or promotion back to the authoritative environment.

Universal read/bounded disclosure applies throughout. A working set cannot be used to launder restricted content into a less restricted artifact, compare unauthorized populations, or infer protected facts.
