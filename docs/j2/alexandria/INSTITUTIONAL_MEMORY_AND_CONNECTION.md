## Constitutional promise

Alexandria exists so Sable Harbor does not lose what it learns. Preservation means more than retaining documents. The environment preserves what the institution believed, when, why, with what confidence and dissent, and what later changed. A superseded state remains part of institutional memory rather than an embarrassment to erase.

## Connected institutional record

Sable Harbor's world is not a folder tree. A question can touch a Judgment Problem, Contact requirement, field observation, finance assumption, board decision, JAG deployment, business line, incident, lesson, course, Canon state, and later outcome. Alexandria maintains those relationships so a person can move through the institutional mind rather than guess which department filed the answer.

Connection is explicit and typed where useful: supports, contradicts, qualifies, relies on, supersedes, corrects, prompted, resulted in, observed at, applies to, taught by, and remains unresolved by. Relationships preserve author/source, time, confidence, access boundary, and status. The system avoids an ornate ontology whose complexity exceeds human value; connection serves navigation and reasoning.

Machine intelligence may propose a potential relationship, anomaly, or cluster. A suggestion remains synthetic until a human professional accepts it into the appropriate institutional artifact. Repeated co-occurrence is not causation, and graph proximity is not institutional belief.

## Memory of error

Later evidence can explain why an earlier belief failed, but it cannot silently edit the prior state. Contrary evidence, rejected paths, dead ends, and dissent remain traversable when lawful. This protects decision-quality review from hindsight and allows future officers to see whether a failed path was unreasonable, unknowable, or simply unlucky.

## Continuity

Human handovers, officer transfers, leadership succession, acquisitions, and business-line change use Alexandria to carry context. A record is not “preserved” if its links, provenance, access, or contemporary meaning are lost. Orientation stewards this continuity; owning functions remain responsible for human authorship and the substance of their records.
