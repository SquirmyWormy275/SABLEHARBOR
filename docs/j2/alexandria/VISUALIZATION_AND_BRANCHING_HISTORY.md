Visualization helps a person reason about institutional time and connection; it is not decorative dashboarding. Alexandria and Daedalus may present timelines, branching histories, connection maps, evidence lineage, assumption dependence, cross-business comparison, and Git-like branches of institutional understanding.

A timeline distinguishes event, record, access, understanding, and institutional-change times where material. A branch view shows competing interpretations, the evidence each relied upon, dissent, merge or supersession, and what remained unresolved. A lineage view moves from institutional statement toward sources and from an assumption toward later decisions and outcomes.

Visual prominence does not create authority. Node size, color, proximity, centrality, or machine-suggested connection must not imply truth, consensus, causation, importance, or access that the underlying record does not support. Every visual element remains traceable to its source/state, and restricted relationships cannot be exposed through shape or absence.

Daedalus can assemble persistent user-side investigative visuals. They are marked synthetic and non-authoritative, remain outside Alexandria, and cannot promote themselves into Canon, Semaphore, Judgment, or EIB. A human may independently author an institutional visual under the applicable process.

## Planned AR and VR interfaces

Future augmented-reality (AR) and virtual-reality (VR) integration is planned, and Alexandria's architecture must support it. Alexandria remains fully usable through an ordinary desktop interface; headsets and spatial hardware are never prerequisites for ordinary use. AR/VR is not a v0.1 dependency.

Spatial interaction must serve understanding: exploring relationships, chronology, physical locations, evidence lineage, and competing interpretations. The constitutional requirement is faithful, navigable representation of time, provenance, disagreement, and connection regardless of interface technology. AR and VR are optional interfaces for the user, not separate institutions or a change to Alexandria's authority.

The same access, disclosure, provenance, temporal-integrity, and authorship rules apply in every interface. A spatial view cannot expose a relationship the viewer is not entitled to see; visual prominence cannot imply truth or consensus. Daedalus-generated investigative visuals retain their non-authoritative marking and cannot promote themselves into institutional records.

No specific device, vendor, implementation, or delivery date is committed. The direction is planned, not a claim that AR or VR integration is already implemented.
