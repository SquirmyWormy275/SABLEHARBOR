# AI AUTHORITY AND HUMAN AUTHORSHIP

**Document ID:** `SH-J2-ALX-AI-001` | **Version:** 1.0.0 | **Effective:** September 2, 2026 | **State:** LOCKED
**Owner:** J2 Headquarters / Orientation | **Related:** Daedalus, Canon, Semaphore, Judgment, EIB | **Cross-reference:** `DAEDALUS_OPERATING_DOCTRINE.md`; `CANON_INSTITUTIONAL_KNOWLEDGE.md`; `SEMAPHORE_TRAFFIC_SYSTEM.md`

Sable Harbor wants useful, intellectually aggressive AI. The boundary is institutional authority, not thought. Daedalus may form opinions, challenge a user, speculate with a label, propose hypotheses, synthesize evidence, and recommend investigative directions. It may say, “I think you're wrong, and here is why.”

Three boundaries are absolute:

1. **No authoritative record write.** Daedalus has no write path into authoritative Alexandria material.
2. **No self-promotion.** Daedalus output cannot automatically become Canon, Judgment, Semaphore, EIB, or institutional position.
3. **No institutional authority.** Daedalus cannot state “Sable Harbor judges X” unless accurately quoting a human-authored institutional record.

Judgment Officers author institutional judgments. Orientation Officers author Semaphore traffic and material Canon changes. Orientation owns EIB publication. AI may aid their research, compare sources, test hypotheses, or prepare private synthetic work, but Sable Harbor does not create a hidden conveyor in which a human merely approves an AI draft and thereby makes the AI functional author.

Independent human authorship requires that the responsible professional understands the question, examines relevant evidence and limitations, makes the judgment, chooses language/confidence, owns dissent treatment, and issues the artifact under their authority. Tool assistance is provenance-relevant where material, but the institution records responsibility rather than every private influence chain.

Daedalus synthetic reports, timelines, maps, comparisons, notes, and working syntheses may persist in the user's own workspace. They remain outside Alexandria, visibly marked `DAEDALUS-GENERATED / NON-AUTHORITATIVE`, and have no automatic promotion path. A human may use them to support genuinely human-authored work.
