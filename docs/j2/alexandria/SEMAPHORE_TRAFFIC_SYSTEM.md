# SEMAPHORE TRAFFIC SYSTEM

**Document ID:** `SH-J2-SEMAPHORE-001` | **Version:** 1.0.0 | **State:** LOCKED DIRECTION

Semaphore is the cable-like longitudinal intelligence/decision traffic medium: durable, searchable, threaded, referential, supersession-aware, historically navigable, and human-authored. It is not Slack with formal typography and not a one-page cap on major decisions.

Traffic retains durable ID, human originator, issue/event time, subject stating why it matters, concise point-first opening, references/thread, distribution/access, business line/topics, related decisions/problems, corrections/supersession, and priority where useful. Routine/Priority/Immediate/Flash remain PROVISIONAL labels. Orientation Officers exercise professional judgment over what merits traffic; policy does not enumerate every allowed subject.

The record captures decisions, relied-upon judgments, alternatives, assumptions, unknowns, dissent, evidence references, predictions, triggers, later changes, and supersession—not every hallway conversation, private thought, or interpersonal influence chain. Retention follows future legal/privacy rules while preserving provenance wherever lawful.

AI may assist research; only a human Orientation Officer writes/issues institutional traffic. Daedalus cannot author, issue, or promote a cable.
