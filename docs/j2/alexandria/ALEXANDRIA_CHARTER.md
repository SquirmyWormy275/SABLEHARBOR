Alexandria **preserves** what Sable Harbor believed, when, why, and what changed; **connects** questions, evidence, judgments, decisions, outcomes, finance, business lines, JAG, and learning; and **orients** users to what is current, disputed, unresolved, historical, or superseded. Machine suggestions never silently become facts.

## Time

Records distinguish where applicable: event time; recorded time; institutional-access time; understood time; and institutional-change time. Queries must separately support “What did Sable Harbor actually know on date X?” and “What information existed or could reasonably have been known on date X?” Later knowledge may explain an earlier state, never silently backfill it. Current/active, superseded, disputed, historical, and unresolved states remain human-legible without status-code excess.

## Provenance

“Source that” traverses Canon, EIB, Judgment, decision, Semaphore, or Daedalus synthesis toward evidence where access permits. Lineage represents supporting, contrary, missing, contested, and considered/rejected evidence. A dead-end chain remains useful history. Source-supported statements and AI inference are visibly distinct.

## Access and retention boundary

Information access follows `../INFORMATION_ACCESS_DOCTRINE.md`. Raw Collection is most heavily gated. Restrictions may preserve visible questions/existence notices without disclosure. Exact entitlement roles, technology stack, and retention/deletion implementation remain OPEN; no “never delete” promise overrides later law or privacy duties.

## Desktop usability and planned AR/VR integration

Alexandria must remain fully usable through an ordinary desktop interface. Future augmented-reality (AR) and virtual-reality (VR) integration is planned, and the architecture must support those future interfaces. Headsets and spatial hardware are never prerequisites for ordinary use; AR/VR is not a v0.1 dependency.

These are interfaces to the same institutional environment, not new institutions or sources of authority. They must preserve the same access, disclosure, provenance, temporal-integrity, and human-authorship boundaries as the desktop experience. Visual prominence cannot create truth, consensus, or authority, and spatial presentation cannot expose restricted relationships.

Specific devices, vendors, interface implementations, and delivery dates remain OPEN. This planned direction makes no claim that AR or VR capability already exists. See [visualization and branching history](VISUALIZATION_AND_BRANCHING_HISTORY.md).
