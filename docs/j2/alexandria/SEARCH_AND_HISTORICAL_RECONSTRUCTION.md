## Search as institutional navigation

Alexandria search is built for people who may not know the repository name, owning function, final terminology, or perfect query. A future employee searching `transportation scheduling` should be able to enter years of relevant questions, evidence, traffic, judgments, decisions, outcomes, JAG observations, finance assumptions, business-line material, and learning—subject to access.

Search operates across full text and structured relationships: subject, topic, business line, entity/asset where applicable, related decision, Judgment Problem, collection requirement, author/origin, time dimensions, current state, supersession/correction, confidence, dissent, and reference thread. Results distinguish authoritative records, evidence, and Daedalus synthetic material.

Rabbit holes are a feature when provenance and orientation remain visible. A user can hold an item, follow references, move backward to sources, move forward to reliance/outcomes, compare periods or businesses, and return to the original question without losing the investigative trail.

## Historical reconstruction

“Take me to what we knew on the morning of X” reconstructs the accessible institutional state then: records available, understanding formed, current Canon, live judgments and dissent, unresolved gaps, and decisions already made. It excludes later knowledge unless clearly shown as a later explanatory overlay.

A separate knowability inquiry identifies information that existed or could reasonably have been accessed at the time even if Sable Harbor had not found, routed, or understood it. The system never collapses actual knowledge and possible knowledge into one hindsight narrative.

## Restricted results

Search does not become an entitlement bypass. Questions and existence notices may travel wider than evidence, but result counts, snippets, relationship graphs, comparisons, and Daedalus answers must not leak protected content or enable prohibited inference. The exact retrieval engine and authorization implementation remain OPEN; this doctrine defines acceptance behavior.

## Quality

Search quality is judged by relevant discovery, provenance preservation, temporal correctness, understandable status, and safe disclosure—not clicks, query volume, or the appearance of a single definitive answer.
