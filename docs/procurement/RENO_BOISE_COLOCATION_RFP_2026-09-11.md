- **Reno primary:** Switch TAHOE RENO — The Citadel Campus.
- **Boise independent recovery:** IDACORE Boise, 2653 S Victory View Way, Boise, Idaho 83709.

This package is no longer a competitive provider-selection RFP. It is the mandatory quote, diligence, negotiation, contracting and acceptance schedule for the selected providers. A selected provider's inability to meet a mandatory boundary is an owner exception requiring explicit resolution.

Obtain binding or quote-stage pricing for 75, 100, 150, 200, and 250 kW usable IT commitments. For each show cabinets/cage/private suite, A/B power, metering, cooling density, liquid-cooling options, installation, cross-connects, carriers, 10/100 Gbps transport, OOB, remote hands, receiving/storage, cameras, taxes, deposits, minimum term, renewal/escalator, expansion reservation, relocation conditions, and all one-time/recurring charges.

Obtain written confirmation of the exact Switch building/campus allocation, customer enclosure model, maximum supported rack density, liquid-cooling path, expansion mechanism, and Nevada colocated-business tax-abatement process applicable to Sable Harbor-owned equipment. Public tax or uptime marketing statements are not sufficient accounting/control evidence.

## IDACORE Boise recovery schedule

Obtain binding or quote-stage pricing for 10, 25, 50, and 100 kW recovery footprints with 100 TiB, 250 TiB, and 500 TiB durable-data scenarios. Current public planning baseline is $300/kW/month with power included and a 12-month standard term; the executed commercial schedule controls after contracting.

Show cabinets/cage options, dual-path power delivery to Sable Harbor equipment, 100 TiB+ storage accommodation, independent Internet/transit carriers, replication/private transport options from Northern Nevada, remote hands, secure receiving, emergency expansion, cross-connects, IP transit, OOB, installation, taxes, deposits and all one-time/recurring charges.

## Mandatory technical diligence — both providers

Provide single-line electrical diagrams sufficient for diligence; utility-feed description; UPS/generator redundancy; fuel runtime/replenishment; maintenance method; environmental ranges; maximum rack density; liquid cooling; fire detection/suppression; carrier list and entrance map; meet-me rooms; physical access layers; CCTV retention; loading/receiving; remote-hands staffing; material single points of failure; and recent material outage history.

For Boise specifically, prove recovery independence from Switch Reno: carrier route and last-mile diversity, independent provider administration, independent credentials, and no requirement for Switch systems to restore protected services.

## Mandatory assurance response

Provide current SOC 1 Type II and SOC 2 Type II or state why unavailable; report period and bridge letter; ISO 27001 scope if applicable; CUECs; subservice organizations; BCP/DR test summary; generator/UPS test summary; incident-response policy summary; insurance certificates; privacy/security incident notification terms; and evidence-delivery process.

IDACORE's public representation of SOC 2 Type II, PCI DSS, NIST 800-53, SSAE-16 and HITRUST readiness/certification is a diligence lead only. Sable Harbor must obtain the actual applicable report/certificate, scope, period, exceptions and bridge coverage before reliance.

## Contract red lines

Sable Harbor owns all equipment/data/keys. No provider lien may prevent emergency access to or orderly removal of equipment/data. Provider personnel access to the Sable Harbor enclosure must be restricted/logged. Security incidents require <=1 hour notification; P1 availability events <=30 minutes. Ordinary material maintenance requires advance notice. Provider must support forensic preservation, audit evidence, recovery tests, and exit. Material subcontractor changes require notice. Pricing must identify escalators and pass-throughs.

Neither provider may obtain institutional authority over Alexandria, Atlas Meridian, Foundry Field, recovery priorities, encryption keys, entitlement decisions or Sable Harbor records merely by operating the facility layer.
