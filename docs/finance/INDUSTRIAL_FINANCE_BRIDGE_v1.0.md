The [industrial case](../../industrial/README.md) is the current selected Pale Sun, Red Wash, ARU and BS&T financial implementation. It supersedes the old Red Wash bridge's open legal suffixes, Pale Sun nonentity treatment and unfinished ARU operating perimeter. The [old bridge](RED_WASH_FINANCE_PLATFORM_BRIDGE.md) remains the historical description of the finance-platform v0.1 snapshot.

The industrial model creates 2025 ARU bottom-up contracts/payroll/cost schedules, monthly double-entry books and statements; acquisition consideration and funding; the January 1–6 seller stub; a postclose 2026 ARU model; the integrated mine successor; and reciprocal intercompany eliminations. Group statements are a controlled industrial perimeter, not a revised whole-enterprise consolidated release.

The original Red Wash standalone source retains its numeric comparison. Successor schedules explicitly bridge opening ARO, intercompany service, external origin linehaul, interface depreciation, taxes, equity funding and closure cash. Comparing only the original standalone statement to an integrated invoice would omit this bridge.

Do not replace the finance-platform v0.1 source lock or silently load this case into an old generation run. Its released enterprise model has a different perimeter and different calibration assumptions. The new participant archive carries its own source revision, catalog, database and checksums. A future enterprise integration must create a new version and reconcile entity opening balances, shared services, funding and elimination entries across the larger group.
