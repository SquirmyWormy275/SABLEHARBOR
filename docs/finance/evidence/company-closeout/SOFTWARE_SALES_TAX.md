# Prospective software sales-tax population

Recorded September 15, 2026. Source `enterprise/closeout/source/software_sales_tax.json`
completes ordinary fictional deployment and contract precision for all **75 existing
subscription contracts: 60 Foundry Field and 15 Atlas licenses**. It does not
replace the current August 88-customer population or establish every enterprise
receipt's location. No existing accepted contradictory tax-exclusive term was found
in business contract source, legal instruments or billing canon. Seller absorption
is newly authored for these other contracts, not automatically inherited from FF-003.

## Facts and authority

Each selected contract explicitly has a fictional Sacramento city billing address
and exclusively Sacramento city user deployment. City situs is a declared fact,
not inferred from a fictional correspondence string or headquarters. FF-003 retains
its accepted exact address, customer, issuer and all 140 user-location facts. Other
addresses have no physical-property or geocoding claim. The contracts provide
prewritten provider software for repeated sale; they do not permit the customer
to run its own code on infrastructure and do not transfer reproduction rights.
The separate bespoke Atlas engagements and human-led Advisory services are outside
this subscription population and must not be taxed merely because software assists
performance. No insurer purchaser or other digital purchase from SHI is authored.

## Composition and settlement

`software_sales_tax.csv` links each actual modeled subscription invoice to its
customer, contract, principal, scope, tax, journal source and planned cash date.
`software_tax_customer_population.csv` tests complete annual customer aggregation.
The provider posts seller tax expense/payable by legal SHI book and the correct
Foundry/Atlas unit before computing income tax; income-tax, funding, statements,
CSV/SQLite and sovereignty reports consume these same entries.

**INV-base-FF-003-TERM-0 is excluded from every new posting/payment.** Its existing
$152,250 issuance-only supplement remains exactly once, with no cash, principal
change, default-derived relief or new deduction in a management view. Later
FF-003 terms and the other scenarios are separately generated transactions and
receive their own supported scope rows.

For the other new obligations the source authors a synthetic monthly CDTFA account
frequency and payment on the last business day of the following month. Frequency
is explicitly authored; it is not inferred from a real account. The legal due date
is the last day of the following month, subject to applicable business-day rules;
planning before a weekend deadline is timely without moving a cash leg into a
later month. December 2031 obligations retain their January 2032 due/payment plan
outside the selected statement horizon. No real remittance or government
acknowledgement is claimed. [CDTFA filing/payment guidance](https://www.cdtfa.ca.gov/industry/tax-practitioners/filing-and-payments.htm)
and [filing dates](https://www.cdtfa.ca.gov/taxes-and-fees/sales-use-tax-returns-filing-dates.htm),
reviewed September 15, 2026.

Commercial SLA credits and bad debts do not automatically reverse this tax.
Tax-relief review is explicitly not performed in the selected scenario; the gross
issuance amount remains paid/accrued, with potential relief unclaimed and no tax
receivable invented. This is an inspectable tax-control exception, not a finding
that commercial credit authority is sufficient tax authority. Likewise the current
source contains no actual deployment verification: these are conditional future
facts available only as September-authored scenarios.
