# Illinois loss-limit authority update

Prepared September 15, 2026 UTC. Scope: calendar-year corporate losses in the 2026–2031 company provision; no assertion of a filed carryover or common member loss pool.

The [General Assembly's current section 207](https://www.ilga.gov/Documents/legislation/ilcs/documents/003500050K207.htm), subsection (d), retains a $500,000 maximum for 2024–2026. For 2027–2031 the limit is the greater of $500,000 and respectively 15%, 30%, 50%, 65%, and 80% of income before the loss deduction. Actual use also cannot exceed that income or available losses. The [official SB3019 status](https://www.ilga.gov/legislation/BillStatus?DocNum=3019&DocTypeID=SB&GAID=18) records Public Act 104-0468 on June 16, 2026.

Primary indexed statutory text was read on September 15, 2026; several dynamic Act/fulltext download endpoints failed. No downloaded original PDF hash is asserted. `tax_limits.py` implements the declared calendar years and rejects unscoped dates, nonfinite values and negative carryovers. Separate state workpapers establish taxpayer membership, apportioned income and available losses before applying this limit. An obsolete assumption of unlimited use after 2026 must not enter the successor.
