## Scope and supersession

The company edition uses a synthetic management accrual basis, in USD, with monthly legal-entity books and consolidation eliminations. It does not assert US GAAP compliance, an audit opinion, filed tax returns or independently confirmed bank balances. This continues the express scope of `docs/finance/ACCOUNTING_POLICIES.md`, the Red Wash R2 canon and `industrial/transaction/05_ARU_CLOSING_AND_TAX_DELIVERY.md`.

It also governs the separately evidenced opening corrections for unsupported Core goodwill, omitted equipment depreciation and Red Wash inventory/cost recovery. It does not change frozen source bytes, prior release acceptance boundaries, legal ownership, acquisition consideration, or the historical evidence status of those releases. A release must identify the specific implemented provision and correction versions; this policy alone does not establish numerical completion.

Source initialization corrections are distinguished from historical operating events and current-period expenses.

## Recognition and reconciliation

Each correction must have balanced legal-book entries, a source and authority reference, effective period, scenario, and a bridge into consolidation and statements. Shared-service allocations are separated from legal obligations and tax deductions. Intercompany inventory adjustments use the group's actual production-support cost and preserve the legal service charge. Physical quantities are not duplicated when inventory has cash-cost and depreciation layers.

The $30 million Core goodwill removal is a noncash source-initialization correction, with no substitute asset or inferred tax benefit. ARU's accepted opening $14,762,500 book goodwill and conditional $13 million tax goodwill remain independently identifiable. Subsequent statutory tax measurement is a separately disclosed management-basis adjustment; it does not silently restate the accepted purchase-price allocation or create acquisition goodwill to balance deferred taxes.

Book and tax inventory methods, depreciation, depletion and research timing are separately reconciled. A tax capitalization requirement does not itself create a book asset. Red Wash's accepted abnormal repair/stabilization period expense is preserved, while applicable tax production-cost capitalization is tracked as a book-to-tax difference. Estimate changes and newly authored normal-production allocations retain their scope and sensitivity.

## Tax provision, settlement and valuation

Federal taxpayers, disregarded entities and state combined-report members are separate concepts. Current tax follows the selected statutory taxpayer/perimeter, jurisdiction, period-specific rates, apportionment and member-specific loss limitations. A combined report does not authorize pooling all historical member losses. Book tax expense, gross current liabilities, estimated payments and recoverable prepayments are separately presented; an overpayment is not a negative liability or an inferred refund receipt.

Deferred schedules retain gross deductible and taxable differences, applicable rates, reversal periods, loss limitations and valuation allowances. Recognition requires a stated source of realization in the same relevant taxpayer/jurisdiction and a compatible reversal or carryover period. Asset deferred liabilities reversing before a closure obligation do not automatically support realization of that later obligation. Unsupported ARO and contingent-reserve benefits receive a full valuation allowance. Conditional future profits alone do not establish recoverability.

Unpaid transaction taxes and employer levies do not automatically create current tax deductions. The selected payment/economic-performance method and any later deduction must be explicit. Preparation, modeled submission, acknowledgement, liability accrual, statutory due date, payment request, receipt and settlement remain distinct states. FF003 retains its separately accepted issuance-only boundary and $152,250 payable until supported disposition.

Historical paid-in capital, retained earnings, opening corrections and subsequent contribution receipts reconcile as separate equity components. The approved $183 million historical subscriptions are not new successor cash. Conditional member funding is financing from outside operations; neither unused capacity nor an unpaid tax bill is internally generated cash. Treasury and sovereignty schedules must show outstanding booked cash requirements and financing shortfalls alongside cash balances.
