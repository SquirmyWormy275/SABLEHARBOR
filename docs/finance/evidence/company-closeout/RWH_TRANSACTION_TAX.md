## Reporting and income-tax boundary

Book accrual is separate from Illinois gross-receipts reporting under 86 Ill. Code 130.401. Collection allocation, old-invoice sale rates, return rounding, accelerated-payment lookback and actual filing/payment state require their own workpapers; no remittance is generated here. An opening receivable collection cannot create a second sale or tax expense.

Treas. Reg. 1.461-4(g)(6) generally places economic performance for taxes at payment. No recurring-item exception is asserted. The parent provision separately adds back changes in unpaid FF003 and software tax liabilities, reverses that temporary difference when modeled payment occurs, and reserves the resulting deferred deduction. The RWH unpaid-tax addback belongs in its separate PS/RWH statutory income-tax successor; native industrial scenario rates alone do not substantiate that return.

This workpaper is a reviewable successor, not an accepted release or evidence of real tax filing. Primary authority and period facts remain in the controls source; the company closeout manifest identifies final acceptance.
