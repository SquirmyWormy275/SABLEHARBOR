# Quantitative baseline reconciliation

## Decision

**Selected working baseline: Alternative B — CoreCo calibration plus incremental industrial operations.**  
**State:** `MODEL_PROPOSED`, reversible. No number in this document is `LOCKED` canon.

Alternative B best preserves a decade of plausible software scale while recognizing that Red Wash and ARU entered through acquisitions and cannot fit inside the old software-only functional allocation without unexplained displacement.

## Revenue and operating logic

- Foundry Field remains the cash-generating core: subscriptions and usage, implementation, support, and specialist services.
- Atlas Meridian is controlled-commercial: modest license/design-partner revenue and high science/validation cost.
- Willow is a cost center; transfers are traced but do not create consolidated revenue.
- Pale Sun/Red Wash recognizes uranium-concentrate sales from produced and shipped inventory; no enrichment or fabrication activity exists.
- Cradle recognizes small equipment/service/recovery participation revenue and does not own its host mine by default.
- ARU/BS&T earns external freight, terminal, storage, and handling revenue. Services to Red Wash are intercompany and eliminated.
- Advisory has a few method-transfer engagements; it is neither a mature division nor a generic consulting pool.
- Emberline has no standalone 2026 revenue. Residual contracts, IP, assets, and people carry successor-unit lineage.

## Capital and ownership working case

The legacy $172.5M equity-raised figure remains calibration. The baseline assumes an additional 2025 acquisition financing package of approximately $70M equity and $86M term/asset debt across Red Wash and ARU, plus rollover/contingent consideration where configured. Exact rounds, investors, ownership percentages, purchase prices, financing, and liability allocation remain `OPEN`; generated records tag each value `SCENARIO_INPUT`.

## Accounting consequences

- Separate legal-entity books and a consolidation book.
- Acquisition-date fair-value layers for mineral interests, plant, rolling stock, customer relationships, goodwill, debt-like items, and ARO.
- Intercompany service, freight, technology, cash, and management-fee balances with eliminations.
- Mine production inventory by stage; depletion and depreciation linked to operational drivers.
- ARO accretion, revisions, and reclamation spending separated from current environmental remediation.
- Software subscription, services, mine sales, freight, recovery participation, and advisory revenue use distinct accounts and performance dimensions.

## Rejected interpretations

- The legacy Federal Systems LLC is not included in baseline because `PS-017` remains open.
- Emberline is not revived as a 2026 segment merely to preserve old headcount or revenue.
- Red Wash and ARU are not collapsed into CoreCo departments; that would erase operational accountability and acquisition consequences.
- Cradle host assets are not consolidated unless a future scenario establishes control.
