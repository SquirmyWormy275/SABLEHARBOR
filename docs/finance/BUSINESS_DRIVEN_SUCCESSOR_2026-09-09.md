## Advisory fee and staffing discipline

Measurable Value Matters model the proposed 60% committed / 40% variable structure. Capability Matters use an explicit committed component and testable transfer gate; Decision Matters remain fixed fee with no contingent recommendation incentive. The contractual payout curve has a threshold, interpolation and cap. Independent certification applies the declared attribution fraction and records the original baseline hash. Below-threshold outcomes can earn zero variable fee.

Hours consume paid capacity and delivery cost. Rejection and rework cannot create additional committed fees. Acceptance can defer recognition even where billing has created a receivable and deferred-revenue liability. Declined matters generate neither delivery nor billing. Matter Principal, Acceptance Principal, originator, Independent Reviewer and Value Officer are separately identified synthetic roles. Atlas license fees are not bundled into matter fees.

Conditional workforce records distinguish authorization from occupancy. Each occupied person is paid once; assignment fractions sum to one. Four Advisory governance positions do not supply delivery capacity. Two Willow staff contribute fractional assignments; neither J2 nor Atlas supplies an Advisory staffing reserve. J2's 237 authorized billets remain distinct from a conditional occupancy scenario. Eligibility tests cannot grant carry, and no carry expense or obligation is accrued.

## Working capital, equipment and cash

Annual subscriptions and committed matter billing create gross receivables and deferred revenue. Accepted performance releases deferred revenue. Explicit due dates and collection delays drive receivable aging; a separately labeled expected-loss estimate produces the allowance. Vendor costs accrue to payables before scheduled cash requests.

Stream 17 direct processing and assigned direct labor enter captured-lot inventory once; modeled net realizable value can reduce carrying cost. Demotte experimental work remains expense. Capture is not a sale. Accepted assayed product releases inventory cost, accrues host share and invoices the customer. Host settlement follows customer collection. A hard bypass yields no recovered output.

Reusable equipment is capitalized and depreciated from the following month. Qualified transfers move original gross cost and accumulated depreciation with no enterprise profit or cash. Existing legacy asset balances remain separate.

Treasury tests all cash requests against finite member support and the minimum cash floor. Unfunded operating requests, equipment requests and debt principal are separately classified liabilities; only identified requests are eligible for deferral. Subsidiary funding cannot be redirected by this mechanism. Later repayments retain their original cash-flow class. A funding gap marks an infeasible conditional plan. It does not assert an agreed payment extension, allow equipment to operate without qualification or erase creditor rights.

## Management and capital views

A nonposting support allocation spreads net corporate cost using declared occupied/FTE weights, while preserving direct segment books and statutory shared-service charges. It is a management comparison, not a second expense posting. Industrial accepted workforce counts are kept distinct from conditional Core occupancy.

The quality-of-balance-sheet view shows reported equity and equity excluding the retained $30 million unallocated legacy intangible. It supplies no invented acquisition or impairment evidence. The standalone mine-upgrade appraisal includes common mine capital omitted from the earlier differential logistics comparison. Closure cost/support sensitivities are independent planning assumptions, not replacements for the accepted booked ARO or engineering estimates.
