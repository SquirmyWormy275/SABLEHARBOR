# Willow / Evalon finance and corporate model

**Document ID:** `SH-FIN-WIL-EVAL-20260906-001`  
**Version:** 0.1.0  
**As of:** September 6, 2026  
**Status:** MODEL_PROPOSED financial/corporate implementation constrained by locked canon  
**Controlling canon:** `docs/canon/WILLOW_EVALON_CLOSEOUT_2026-09-06.md` and existing corporate lore

## Purpose and boundary

This document turns the newly closed Willow/Evalon canon into an accounting and corporate-documentation model without pretending that generated amounts are observed company history. Existing finance releases remain frozen to their source locks. The 2026 enterprise operating model currently carries a **24-person Pittsburgh-area Willow population** and treats Willow as a bounded laboratory/cost center rather than a product P&L; this document preserves that architecture.

Evalon is historical. It does not receive a 2026 P&L, legal entity, or active business-unit reporting segment merely because its history is now better documented.

## 1. Corporate status

### Willow

- Internal Sable Harbor program/capability, **not a separate legal entity**.
- Primary physical center: the Pittsburgh-area Fort.
- Gid Voss retains experimental and epistemic authority in Pittsburgh.
- Sacramento institutional accountability remains through Rachel Sloane's established seam.
- Willow may incur customer-attributable or intercompany-attributable project costs, but it is not managed as a conventional revenue-maximizing product P&L.
- Production deployment requires an operating owner and qualification gate; a Willow cost center cannot manufacture operating authority.

### Evalon

- Historical 2020–2022 eastern coal/industrial mandate and advanced-engineering outpost.
- No separate legal entity is created retrospectively.
- Historical revenue, expenses, customer work, and assets belong to the legal Sable Harbor books applicable at the time and may be tagged to an Evalon historical program/cost-center dimension where synthetic reconstruction requires it.
- After recharter, surviving equipment, records, people, and useful methods transfer to Willow or other successor functions. The Evalon reporting dimension closes rather than continuing as a shadow organization.

## 3. The Fort — property and fixed-asset architecture

The Fort should be carried as a parent-company research property/site with separate fixed-asset classes sufficient to preserve useful economics and physical lineage.

### Principal asset groups

**Land / site rights**
- land or leasehold interest;
- fencing, gates, roads, gravel circulation and hardstand;
- drainage, utilities and site security infrastructure.

**Big Shed**
- laboratory building/leasehold improvements;
- electrical distribution and three-phase service;
- ventilation, compressed air, extraction and utility systems;
- machine tools, benches, test stands and instrumentation;
- prototype fabrication and integration equipment.

**Small Shed**
- administrative office improvements;
- meeting/work rooms;
- short-stay lodging rooms and associated furniture/fixtures;
- ordinary office and network equipment.

The lodging component is an internal business facility. It is not modeled as a public hotel or separate hospitality business.

**White Shed**
- secure intake and controlled storage improvements;
- mechanical/utility plant;
- quarantine/staging fixtures;
- secure cages, racks and material-handling equipment.

**The Museum / yards**
- outdoor staging and non-sensitive storage;
- retained experimental artifacts and retired prototypes.

Museum items remain on the fixed-asset register when capitalization/retirement policy requires it. Cultural value does not justify carrying a fully depreciated or retired asset at an invented economic value. A retired prototype may remain physically present with zero net book value and a retained provenance record.

## 5. Expense architecture

Willow's recurring cost base should distinguish at least:

- employee compensation and benefits;
- contractors and specialist services;
- experimental consumables;
- instrumentation and calibration;
- prototype components;
- machine-shop and fabrication expense;
- software/model compute;
- field travel and mobilization;
- site occupancy, utilities and maintenance;
- safety, environmental and qualification expense;
- insurance;
- short-stay lodging operations;
- records/provenance and controlled archive expense;
- failed/abandoned experiment write-offs where capitalization had occurred.

The model should preserve the distinction between a failed experiment and a control failure. A technically unsuccessful experiment can be an ordinary planned expense. Unauthorized travel, untraceable cash, or unsupported deployment is not reclassified as successful R&D merely because useful learning resulted.

## 6. Capitalization policy

Default treatment should be conservative:

- ordinary experimental labor and failed prototypes expense as incurred unless applicable accounting policy clearly supports capitalization;
- reusable laboratory equipment capitalizes under the enterprise fixed-asset threshold and useful-life policy;
- building/site improvements capitalize when criteria are met;
- internal software follows the enterprise software-capitalization policy rather than a Willow-specific exception;
- research artifacts retained in the Museum do not acquire balance-sheet value because they are historically interesting;
- a prototype transferred into production must receive a documented operating owner, asset identity, qualification state, maintenance responsibility and accounting disposition.

## 7. Evalon-to-Willow transition accounting

The 2022 recharter should be represented as an internal program transition, not an acquisition or business combination.

A synthetic transition schedule should classify each Evalon item as:

1. transferred to Willow and remaining in service;
2. transferred to Foundry/another successor function;
3. retained as historical/experimental artifact;
4. abandoned or scrapped;
5. leasehold/facility item surrendered or replaced;
6. unresolved pending evidence.

No goodwill is created. No purchase accounting is performed. Historical Evalon customer contracts close, transfer, or terminate according to their actual synthetic records rather than being assumed to follow Willow.

## 8. Sar-e-Sang financial/control treatment

The 2022 Sar-e-Sang event requires a distinct control record in any detailed synthetic reconstruction.

At minimum, the accounting evidence should preserve:

- authorized travel scope before deviation;
- known employee advances and reimbursed amounts;
- cash withdrawals/advances;
- submitted receipts and unsupported expenditures;
- third-party-paid or subsidized costs that cannot be cleanly attributed;
- post-return expense review and exceptions;
- legal/security review costs if modeled;
- any write-off or employee receivable decision;
- management conclusion that useful research output does not cure the control failure.

The ledger must **not** invent a terrorist payment, bribe, intelligence payment, FBI case number, criminal fine, or government investigation. The canon establishes uncertainty and a serious internal discussion about potential counterintelligence exposure, not a proven unlawful transaction.

## 11. 2026 Willow management reporting

The existing enterprise scenario's 24-person Willow population remains the current working calibration. This closeout does not change it merely because the Fort is better specified.

Recommended monthly management views:

- Willow operating expense by cost center and project;
- committed versus spent experimental budget;
- fixed-asset additions and disposals;
- field deployment/qualification cost;
- project continuation/change/transfer/kill status;
- transferred prototype lifecycle and receiving owner;
- Fort occupancy and facilities cost;
- Small Shed temporary-lodging usage/cost;
- material control exceptions separately from experimental failures.

Willow should not be evaluated primarily on patent count, prototype count, utilization, or artificial internal revenue. Its financial reporting should make resource consumption and transfer economics legible without converting the laboratory into a consulting utilization machine.

## Closeout consequence

Willow is financially legible as a parent-company laboratory and institutional capability, while Evalon remains a closed historical program whose people, assets, methods, and scars can be traced into successor functions. The accounting model preserves the central canon distinction: experimental value does not erase control failure, and a laboratory artifact does not become an operating asset merely because it works.
