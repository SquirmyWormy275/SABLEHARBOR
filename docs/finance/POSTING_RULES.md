# Posting rules

| Source event | Debit | Credit |
|---|---|---|
| Opening capital | Cash | Equity |
| Annual platform invoice | AR | Deferred revenue |
| Monthly platform recognition | Deferred revenue | Foundry revenue |
| Customer receipt | Cash | AR |
| Payroll paid | Payroll/benefit expense | Cash |
| Matched capital vendor bill | Fixed asset | AP |
| Vendor payment | AP | Cash |
| Depreciation | Depreciation expense | Accumulated depreciation |
| Debt draw | Cash | Long-term debt |
| Interest accrual | Interest expense | Accrued interest |
| Mine production | Concentrate inventory | AP/production obligations |
| Uranium shipment | AR and COGS | Uranium revenue and inventory |
| ARU waybill | AR | Freight or intercompany revenue |
| ARU fuel/crew | Direct cost | AP |
| Cradle production/sale | Inventory, AR, COGS/host share | AP, revenue, inventory |
| Willow experiment | R&D expense | AP |
| Atlas evaluation and fee | R&D expense and cash | AP and Atlas revenue |

Every implemented rule has a debit/credit reconciliation test and journal-to-source identifier.
