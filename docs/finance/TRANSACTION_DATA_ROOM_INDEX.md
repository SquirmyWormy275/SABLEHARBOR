# Transaction data-room index

This is an architecture and readiness index, not a claim that every underlying legal or technical document exists.

1. Corporate formation, subsidiaries, governance, capitalization, awards, financing, debt, liens, and ownership history.
2. Historical financial statements, monthly management reporting, accounting policies, close evidence, reconciliations, budgets, forecasts, and tax summaries.
3. Customer, contract, renewal, backlog, concentration, implementation, pricing, retention, and unit-economic materials.
4. Product architecture, Foundry/Foundry Field boundary, Atlas evaluations, Willow experiments, R&D, security, privacy, resilience, and third-party technology.
5. Workforce roster, compensation, incentives, contractors, succession, safety, and employee obligations using privacy-safe identifiers.
6. Red Wash title/rights, technical studies, resources/reserves limitations, mine plans, production, inventory, offtake, permits, environmental matters, closure/ARO, capex, and economics.
7. Cradle host rights, process streams, pilots, genealogy, recovery evidence, equipment, participation terms, project economics, and gate decisions.
8. ARU/BS&T corporate structure, routes, terminals, equipment, waybills, maintenance, customers, rates, custody records, safety, capex, and deferred-maintenance assessment.
9. Acquisition agreements, consideration, working-capital adjustments, assumed liabilities, PPA, goodwill, integration costs, synergies, contingent consideration, and opening-balance support.
10. Forecasts, SOTP, component DCF/NAV and multiple cases, QOE adjustments, enterprise-to-equity bridge, buyer/seller cases, sensitivities, known limitations, lineage, and data-quality results.
