## Contact

| State | Decision |
|---|---|
| LOCKED | Contact is the collection apparatus, not a think tank and not a white-paper function. |
| LOCKED | Contact is not self-directing. Orientation sets broad priorities; Judgment Officers issue problem-specific collection requirements/RFIs; HQ arbitrates conflicts/scarcity. |
| LOCKED | Contact chooses lawful collection source, method, timing, provenance, and handoff. |
| LOCKED | Six disciplines: systems collection, human collection, JAG collection, external collection, exploitation, and directed observation/evidence creation. |
| LOCKED | Contact preserves raw signal and provenance and does not turn retrieval into institutional judgment. |
| LOCKED | Metrics are gaps closed, relevance, provenance quality, timeliness, and discriminating value; not report count or data volume. |
| PROVISIONAL | Flash/Current/Active/Standing tempo labels. |
| REJECTED | Universal surveillance, fishing expeditions, raw-event hoarding, or generic BI/news aggregation as a substitute for collection management. |

## Judgment

| State | Decision |
|---|---|
| LOCKED | Judgment Officers are problem owners, like case officers/internal investigators/roving intellectual commissions. |
| LOCKED | Judgment is not a queue where Contact sends evidence and analysts write reports. |
| LOCKED | Each consequential problem receives a commission/writ stating the question without implying the answer. |
| LOCKED | Working portfolio is approximately five live problems: four directed and one mandatory bounded self-directed curiosity problem. |
| LOCKED | JO rights include direct inquiry across Sable Harbor, site/operation attendance, sitting with collectors, riding with JAG, requesting records/collection through Contact, experts, Open Problem Hours, and source protection. |
| LOCKED | A JO has no line command. Their asymmetric power is to compel institutional attention to a question, not operational action. |
| LOCKED | Problem Book is a living record of question, current belief, reasons, evidence/provenance, contrary/missing evidence, assumptions, what does not make sense, next inquiry, and what would change the officer's mind. |
| LOCKED | JAG handoff and problem transfer require human conversation/read-back, not ticket reassignment. |
| LOCKED | Judgment Table is professional attack/challenge, not a vote or consensus committee. Serious dissent travels with the judgment. |
| LOCKED | JO self-knowledge records support calibration and growth, not ranking, scoring, punishment, or throughput measurement. |
| LOCKED | Judgment Watch belongs to Judgment but works at Contact intake for triage, correlation, routing, and problem formation. |

## Orientation

| State | Decision |
|---|---|
| LOCKED | Orientation owns the EIB, enterprise questions and assumptions, changes of mind, reorientation, dissent travel, intellectual history, Canon, and epistemic integrity in major decision environments. |
| LOCKED | Orientation does not own collection, Judgment investigations, line strategy, IT, generic communications, execution follow-up, command authority, or Internal Audit. |
| LOCKED | Orientation Officers function like senior intelligence briefer/adviser analogs; they do not need to know more than Daniel, but must know the state, provenance, confidence, dissent, and limits of what Sable Harbor thinks it knows. |
| LOCKED | They may intervene when uncertainty is presented as fact, material dissent is omitted, confidence overstated, or information deficiency matters. |
| LOCKED | They do not vote, own the agenda, direct outcomes, or automatically delay decisions. “Noted and proceeding” is valid. |
| LOCKED | Orientation Officer is a vocation, not executive rotation, Chief-of-Staff credential, or path to power. |
| LOCKED | Working corps target is approximately 20-25 commissioned officers, with trainees/fellows outside. |
| LOCKED | Ordinary commissioned officers permanently forfeit later C-suite line office, operating VP authority, voting board membership, and equivalent decision power. |
| LOCKED | Anti-court controls bar budgets, staff supervision, personnel actions, gatekeeping, political brokering, private intel hoards, and conversion of access into power. |
| LOCKED | Do not build a forensic influence/blame graph of which Orientation Officer influenced which executive. |
| LOCKED | Executive assignments rotate approximately 24-36 months and are assigned to offices, not personalities. |
| LOCKED | Office of CEO and Board have separate officers; Finance is a standing observation post with a senior CFO-office presence plus approximately 2-3 embeds as a refinable model. |
| LOCKED | Orientation orients itself through a named Head/Director and professional challenge, not a steering committee. |
| OPEN | Exact rank nomenclature and whether a former Head may later become a non-executive director. |
