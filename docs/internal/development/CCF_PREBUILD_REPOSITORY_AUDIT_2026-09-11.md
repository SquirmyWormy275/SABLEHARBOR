**Status:** Development assessment and proposed sequencing; not new corporate canon, a control assessment, or an assurance opinion.

Runtime release-record cleanup is separately tracked in [PR #122](https://github.com/SquirmyWormy275/SABLEHARBOR/pull/122). ## Recommendation

Start the native CCF implementation now. The repository has enough business architecture, governance, common controls, and synthetic operating detail to support it. The prerequisite is a consistent versioned register and scope model, not another enterprise-wide round of lore, vendor selection, or facility design.

A complete CCF needs three separately reportable deliverables:

1. **Definition and implementation:** native controls, risks, objectives, applicability, accountable roles, local procedures, inherited responsibilities, and controlled changes.
2. **Synthetic operating case:** expected occurrences, source populations, evidence, failures, exceptions, remediation, and independent assessment across the selected business boundaries and periods.
3. **Deployment and external assurance readiness:** implemented systems, authorized actors, contractual obligations, actual provider evidence, and any separately accepted external reporting scope.

The first two are appropriate development objectives for this synthetic enterprise repository. The third cannot be inferred from generated records, a passing CI build, a vendor decision, or publication of a release. Do not make a production deployment or professional opinion a hidden acceptance requirement for the synthetic CCF.

Other worktrees were left untouched. No unmerged work was treated as accepted canon.

External standards and legal requirements were not independently refreshed in this sweep. Versions listed in existing planning documents are source assertions to validate during the mapping workstream, not a claim that every listed edition remains current.

## Concrete inconsistencies and tooling issues found

1. The final paragraph of the policy/artifact inventory still describes the internal-assurance charter/maturity decision as future or OPEN. Later controlling ESS/IA doctrine and the foundation index resolve the independence/maturity direction. Reconcile that sentence while preserving any genuinely unfinished charter materialization or procedures; do not reopen the institutional decision.
2. Foundation build-index item 7 names the enterprise objective/risk file as though it were colocated under `docs/controls`; the source lives under `docs/governance`. Make this a proper relative link during the foundation refresh.
3. Older services/finance documents preserve pending states and limitations from their own snapshots. Use dated successor authority and explicit scope. In particular, do not edit the pinned runtime input's historical `OWNER_AUTHORIZED_PENDING_MERGE` field merely to match a later release; the release closeout records the acceptance overlay without rewriting original bytes.
4. The combined pytest command using runtime and operations directories failed collection because both contain `test_integration.py`. The same scoped invocation with `--import-mode=importlib` collects successfully. Standardize a documented all-suite command or package/test naming strategy for the CCF work; root pytest alone cannot be represented as all repository tests.
5. Branch protection is a confirmed current gap, not merely an old issue title. At review time, `gh api .../branches/main` reported unprotected and `.../rulesets` returned `[]`. This sweep does not change repository settings or claim independent peer review occurred.

## Dependency order and build gates

**Wave 1 — Registry and authority foundation (CCF-01 through CCF-04).** Implement the schemas, typed references, state model and migrations; normalize common controls/objectives/risks; reconcile the two local registries and existing source catalogs. Add a read-only coverage report and correct current CCF navigation/status drift. Complete when all imported references validate, historical versions remain queryable, unresolved fields remain visible, and no synthetic record can become an operating assertion through export or status defaults.

**Wave 2 — Three complete control examples (CCF-05 through CCF-11).** Use (a) finance close/revenue and independent review, (b) worker-to-identity JML/access certification, and (c) runtime change/backup/restore. For each, carry one complete chain from risk and applicability through owner/procedure, scheduled population, evidence, failed case, exception/expiry, remediation and independent retest. Finance exercises the existing mature engine; IAM tests cross-system authority; runtime tests design-only and inherited evidence. Use fixtures and explicit synthetic actors where deployment does not exist.

**Wave 3 — Enterprise and business coverage (CCF-06, CCF-09 through CCF-12, CCF-15).** Expand policies and controls by risk and applicable boundary. Add the specialized business adapters and responsibility matrices. Complete when every approved applicability decision has a local implementation or an explicit owned gap, and evidence completeness can be reported by period and boundary. Do not require each unit to instantiate all 166 controls independently.

**Wave 4 — Assessment, longitudinal scenarios and mappings (CCF-13/14).** Expand beyond ideal present-state examples into acquisitions, role changes, incomplete historical evidence, incidents, waivers and recovery. Add scoped monitoring and independent assessment outputs, then version-pinned external mappings. Complete when conclusions are traceable to the correct population and time, limitations cannot be hidden by aggregation, and mappings never stand in for implementation or evidence.

**Separate deployment gate.** Runtime procurement, physical installation, load/thermal qualification, recovery measurements, provider reports, actual identities, executed legal instruments and external practitioner work belong to their own accepted execution tracks. They can run alongside CCF development. Their absence must remain visible wherever an operating or external-assurance conclusion depends on them.
