# CCF substantive source comparison delivery

The earlier control library supplied draft procedures and blank coverage workpapers. This change compares specific source conditions with those procedures and records 50 findings, corrective procedures and tests. It also adds 193 ISO objective checks, authored comparisons across all 17 C5 domains and source navigation linking 623 C5 child criteria to 168 parents, their guidance and separate customer responsibilities.

The HIPAA addressable register resolves all 22 specifications one-to-one to retained regulatory paragraphs; environmental decisions remain open. Findings address specific retention and notification clocks, required roles, subcontractor and privacy processes. SOC2 findings add accountability, two-way communication and description fact reconciliation. ISO findings distinguish ISMS/AIMS approval and review-input requirements rather than assuming identical clause lists. C5 findings cover exact identity/vulnerability timing, product functions, cryptographic/physical requirements, permitted alternatives and customer duties.

A concrete architecture decision emerges: the approved two-site Reno/Boise reference cannot establish the sharpened C5 requirement for more than two locations and continued service after two simultaneous location failures. No third site or changed assessment selection is assumed. Other inputs needed include actual services, data/AI uses, contracts, product capabilities, appointments and qualified source/applicability review. The current ISO27000 reference edition and the historical HIPAA legal-status overlay also require explicit reconciliation.

Review depth remains visible across all 970 inventoried requirements: 186 have targeted duty findings, 128 additional requirements have ISO objective-condition comparisons, 537 additional requirements have C5 domain-condition comparisons, 116 retain prior baseline section analysis, and three source-context/amendment gates lack a new substantive comparison. These categories do not claim exhaustive child/paragraph interpretation, complete coverage, independent acceptance or operating effectiveness.

The `enterprise.ccf.assurance.substantive` build/verify adapter verifies retained source bytes, generates the review package and re-performs its contents. Persisted source/catalogue/design/analysis bindings reject stale findings. No review approvals are manufactured, and Atlas remains a read-only reference guide. See the assurance README for commands and `enterprise/ccf/assurance/review_data/REVIEW_NOTES.md` for exact limits and linked publisher/HHS status checks.
