## Decisions and limits retained

New histories are synthetic conditional 2027–2031 scenarios; retained 2026 records
are calibration. Unit cash attribution is not a bank balance or weekly liquidity
forecast. Industrial capacity can remain infeasible. Detailed Advisory histories
cover selected matters rather than every engagement. Forecast-vintage cash is Core
requested cash before Treasury and tax.

Legal/tax elections, named appointments, production Alexandria/Daedalus runtime,
entitlements and retention, exact geography and the approved headquarters image
remain separately gated. J2, ESS and Internal Audit remain internal functions.
No simulated approval supplies legal, custody, operating or production authority.
