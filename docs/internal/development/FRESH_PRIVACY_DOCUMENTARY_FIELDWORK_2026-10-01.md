# Selected privacy fieldwork

`enterprise.audit_suite.source_privacy_execution` creates fresh, isolated A/B
audit workrooms from an independently accepted operational source library. It
requires exact accepted source pins and separate adapter/privacy-method review
pins. It does not read company authoring recipes, transformations, the Key or
historical audit observations.

Each workroom starts with zero work/evidence and the complete 2027 programme at
the December 31 09:00 UTC initial clock. Separate company operator, audit
performer and reserved reviewer identities remain distinct. Ordinary activation,
kickoff, PBC issuance, source grants, clock advance to January 15 2028, discovery
and exact-version collection produce the retained originals. Every examination
reparses those actual AVAILABLE artifact bytes and their collection receipts.
The original company stores remain unchanged.

The selected task vector is DAT002 TOD, IMPLEMENTATION, TOE,
ACTION-H-PRIVACY-PURPOSE, SOC2 CC6.7 and SOC2 C1.1. Each task has its own observed
population, entire selection, workpaper and recorded item comparisons. Large
case selections are partitioned into citation-bounded sample execution records;
the union still covers every selected item exactly once.

The method reconciles arbitrary request IDs/inlet sequences and documentary
customer/counsel permission, recipient, release scope, source availability and
receipt state. It separately inspects retained authority/design,
configuration/change/monitoring and dataset/lifecycle histories. It does not
interpret legal decisions as correct merely because their fields are present.

All six full authored tasks remain IN_PROGRESS/LIMITATION. Recorded documentary
discrepancies are bounded findings; no whole HIPAA failure, actual routing,
network transfer, independent recipient confirmation, real BA status or legal
applicability is asserted. The September close was recorded before the claimed

uncollected dependencies and enterprise/year population gaps remain explicit.
The performer does not use the reserved reviewer.

Run the module with `--repository`, `--destination`, `--source-pins`,
`--program-pack`, `--adapter-review`, `--adapter-review-sha256`, `--method-review`
and `--method-review-sha256`. The source-pin JSON has exactly the AcceptedLibrary

must be new and their parents private (0700). Outputs use 0600 ordinary bytes.
Failed attempts remain preserved and grants are revoked in cleanup.
