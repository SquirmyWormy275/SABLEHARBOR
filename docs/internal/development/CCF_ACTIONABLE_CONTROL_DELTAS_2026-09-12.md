# Actionable CCF procedure deltas

The preceding review left corrective findings separate from the shared procedures and most C5 analysis at domain depth. The new `enterprise.ccf.assurance.actions` adapter combines original procedures with source-linked proposed steps and produces a prioritized implementation backlog. SOC2/HIPAA remains the baseline; ISO27001, ISO42001 and C5 can be selected independently or together.

The author layer adds 116 targeted baseline condition/test checks and three context/amendment analyses. It supplies an individually authored comparison/test direction and proposed control route for every one of the 623 C5 child criteria. Reuse validation, enhanced procedures and assessment-only work are distinct; eight conditional capability candidates identify product/architecture work without asserting actual absence or approving deployment. Fifty prior corrective findings retain their original source/design bindings.

The climate amendment procedure distinguishes the 4.1 relevance determination from the explanatory 4.2 note. Full ISO27000:2026 and ISO22989:2022 reference reconciliation remain source dependencies. Full AI Annex B and C5 parent-guidance/exception interpretation and actual operating facts remain review work. These first-pass comparisons do not establish normative completeness or independent acceptance.

Each C5 action retains basic, sharpened or additional-complement provenance. Additional-complement criteria remain distinct from corresponding customer responsibilities. The more-than-two-location criterion remains conditional; Reno primary and Boise recovery remain the approved reference. Nothing here claims deployments, PHI processing, signed agreements, appointed owners or an evidence period.

The builder verifies original source bytes, checks persisted author/source/design bindings and writes an atomic private bundle. Verification re-performs the entire delivery, rejecting even checksum-resealed edits. Tests exercise baseline/extension selection, conditional architecture treatment, source and classification populations, stale inputs and forged implementation status. Atlas remains read-only; all authoring and deliverables belong to SABLEHARBOR.

Commands and authoring scope are in `enterprise/ccf/assurance/README.md` and `action_data/README.md`. The original substantive adapter remains a reproducible historical review stage; the actions adapter adds the later analysis without retroactively changing that stage's review-depth counts.
