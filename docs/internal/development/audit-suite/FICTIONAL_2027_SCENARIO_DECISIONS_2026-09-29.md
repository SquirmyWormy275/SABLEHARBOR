**Status:** owner-selected training-world direction, September 29, 2026. This
record extends the [approved first reference scope](../CCF_ASSURANCE_SCOPE_PROPOSAL_2026-09-11.md)
for the fictional audit rehearsal. It does not change the historical 2026
canon or represent real deployment, contract execution, PHI processing, legal
status, or external assurance.

The owner selected both of these choices during the implementation session:

1. Model a plausible **fictional 2027 transition** in which the already
   selected Reno primary and Boise recovery sites become operating during the
   simulated period. Build dated company-native contract, commissioning, and
   recovery records. Preserve the 2026 baseline: providers selected,
   procurement pending, contracts unexecuted, capacity unreserved, and sites
   nonoperating at the last canonical source date.
2. Include a **simulated customer ePHI flow and BA/subcontractor obligation
   chain** in that fictional period, with synthetic contracts and legal
   decisions. Keep the simulated data nonpersonal and payload-free. Do not
   imply any real Sable Harbor entity processes PHI, has a real BAA, or has an
   actual BA/subcontractor role.

Implement these as company-owned source histories with explicit event,
availability, and actual import times; attributed in-universe actors;
customer/provider/Sable Harbor responsibility boundaries; complete source
identities; and Clean/Messy causal differences. A simulated 2027 fact must be
labeled as such in every producer, receipt, audit view, export, and Key. Use
ordinary scoped company-source access and collection in a **fresh** audit
engagement after a complete registry is frozen. Do not transplant artifacts
into the current 13-component A349/B324 pair or mark its 409 tasks per side
complete from these design decisions.

The exact contracting entity, customer service terms, dates and named
in-universe approvers need source-pinned scenario specifications and
independent review before acceptance as fictional company history. Real-world
management assertions, legal advice, qualified SOC 2 review and actual Type 2
operating evidence remain separate gates. A later synthetic audit may assess
the fictional period without presenting it as a real examination.
