# Internal audit-rehearsal delivery timeline

**Planning baseline:** September 29, 2026. Target is a source-faithful,
fictional Clean/Messy CCF/audit rehearsal for the approved SOC 2
Security/Availability/Confidentiality and HIPAA scenario. This is an
engineering schedule, not a promised SOC 2 Type 2 report or claim of real
operating history.

Assume two to three focused builders, one dedicated independent reviewer,
prompt resolution of specific owner/qualified decisions, and no core data
model redesign. The reviewed task inventory is 409 procedures per branch;
283 per branch route through further company-native discovery or period
activity across 43 controls. The current 13-component pair is a technical
baseline with all tasks uncredited and cannot absorb new source systems.
The workload estimate is 20–40 focused builder-weeks before review rework;
the calendar below requires that capacity to be sustained. Intermittent
Codex sessions or a single builder do not imply the same elapsed dates.

| Target | Deliverable and exit evidence |
|---|---|
| October 13, 2026 (week 2) | Seal source-pinned fictional 2027 service, Reno/Boise transition, simulated ePHI/BA obligation, owner and source specifications. Keep actual-world facts distinct and obtain independent design review. |
| December 8, 2026 (week 10) | Build and independently review the remaining company-native source portfolio and Clean/Messy histories. Reconcile every control and task to a source, authorized nonoccurrence or explicit gap; propose a complete registry. |
| January 5, 2027 (week 14) | Freeze the independently checked source registry; start a fresh zero-evidence A/B pair; collect through ordinary PBC, scoped grants and company-source reads; verify exact native-to-retained joins and preservation. |
| February 2, 2027 (week 18) | Perform and review the 818 branch-task instances with population, sample, workpaper, finding and remediation dispositions; calibrate the private instructor Key only after source/procedure checks. |
| February 16, 2027 (week 20) | Finish owner walkthroughs, UX corrections, restore/replay and access checks, then seal the internal fictional rehearsal packet with residual limitations. |

These are **exit checks**, not preassigned passing results. Missing source facts,
qualified decisions, or failed independent review move the relevant batch and
require a dated reforecast. A task disposition can remain a supported gap or
`NOT_RUN`; the calendar does not turn it into a pass.

**Range:** December 22, 2026 (week 12) is an aggressive earliest engineering
finish if source development and review parallelize unusually well. February
2–16, 2027 (weeks 18–20) is the working target. March 16, 2027 (week 24) is
contingency for source, population, review or usability rework. Reforecast at
the week-2 specification and week-10 source-registry gates rather than
calling a partial source collection complete.

Real-world management assertions, actual PHI/BA role, signed contracts,
deployed sites, actual Type 2 operating period and a qualified external
assessor are outside this engineering calendar. They require their own
attributable evidence and timing.
