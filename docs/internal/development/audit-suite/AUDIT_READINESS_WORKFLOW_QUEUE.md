# Company-source audit readiness workflow queue

Recorded September 13, 2026 from the owner's instruction. Status: **QUEUED; implementation not started**. This queue extends the implementation direction and supersedes engagement-specific prepared evidence as the target operating architecture. Historical delivery and test results remain historical results.

## Required outcome

SableHarbor is a persistent fictional company whose operations create records independently of an audit. Auditors and agents obtain evidence through company personnel and authorized source-system inspection or exports. Starting an audit must not manufacture supporting company facts or deliver a preassembled answer set. The audit may retain immutable copies of records it actually collected, with source identifiers, versions, retrieval times and hashes.

Existing synthetic history may initialize company operations, with explicit migration provenance. Preserve original business-event dates separately from actual import dates; never invent historical integrations or collection events. Audits share a consistent company history; explicitly isolated training branches may introduce different scenarios. Private scenario mechanisms and grading rubrics are not company records and must remain inaccessible to auditors and company personas.

The first acceptance target remains corporate shared controls supporting fictional Reno primary and Boise recovery, SOC 2 Security/Availability/Confidentiality Type 2 readiness and the HIPAA business-associate/subcontractor scenario. Optional frameworks follow after the base workflow. Atlas remains read-only. This queue does not authorize deployment, purchases, external messages or actual-company data collection.

## Critical acceptance checks

### Shared realism and Clean/Messy operating conditions

Owner clarification: enterprise realism is required in both modes. Clean/Messy governs the company's operating condition, not the depth of its world or the auditor's access to answers. Apply these requirements to AQ-01 through AQ-08; they are queued acceptance work, not delivered capabilities.

- **Shared foundation (AQ-01–AQ-04):** persistent source systems, coherent organization and service boundaries, complete period-based operating history, causal links across systems, versioned records and realistic role responsibilities. Transfers, terminations, changes and incidents produce related operational consequences. Expand from established company canon without rewriting frozen facts.
- **Clean mode (AQ-04):** controls operate as designed; scoped populations reconcile; approvals and reviews have support; exceptions, when present, are handled according to the control. Staff provide accurate, role-appropriate answers. Clean is not a preassembled answer set or automatic passing opinion.
- **Messy mode (AQ-04):** selected, explicitly modeled failures cause explainable cross-system discrepancies, missing or late approvals, inconsistent operation or inadequate exception handling. Staff may misunderstand, disagree or provide incomplete answers. Problems derive from company events and behavior, not a separate planted evidence package or arbitrary contradictions.
- **Bounded knowledge (AQ-05):** company truth, each employee's knowledge and auditor knowledge are distinct. Both modes require authorized source collection. Staff may consult colleagues and correct statements with history; neither company nor auditor agents receive hidden scenario truth or grading rubrics.
- **Professional audit effort (AQ-06–AQ-07):** both modes require population reconciliation, sample selection, testing and supported conclusions. Accept defensible alternative procedures. An auditor's choices affect discoveries; a Clean label never substitutes for testing, and a Messy label never establishes a finding.
- **Acceptance (AQ-07–AQ-08):** exercise matched isolated Clean/Messy branches of the same company baseline with equal source-system depth and collection mechanics. Show how selected operating differences propagate to records and conclusions while preserving each branch's continuity across engagements. Qualified reviewers assess realism and evidence sufficiency in both modes; do not claim indistinguishability from a real company based solely on software checks.

- Company records exist before an engagement and remain available after it; a second engagement sees the same applicable history.
- Every collected item resolves to its source version and collection context. Later company corrections do not change retained audit snapshots.
- An auditor cannot read future records, hidden scenario truth, grading rubrics or records outside granted access. Company agents cannot invent missing support.
- Full-period populations reconcile to source activity, including exceptions and exclusions. A generated PDF alone does not prove the underlying activity occurred even within the simulation.
- Retroactive imports retain original bytes and truthful import provenance. Conflicting versions remain explainable, and migration is recoverable.
- The rehearsal begins without an evidence package and reaches supported conclusions, including qualified or incomplete conclusions where warranted.

Readiness means an auditor can investigate the fictional company and perform the scoped audit with traceable evidence. It does not require hiding control failures or guaranteeing a passing outcome. Real external assurance would require actual company operations and records; synthetic history cannot establish those facts.
