Its boundary is the pinned V18 native corpus, the independently accepted October

audit observations, retained audit evidence, prepared PBC responses, rubric,
instructor Key, or grants. Further company populations require an explicitly
reviewed successor rather than widening this initialization silently.

V1's independently reviewed 69-version SEC proof, module, registry and private
files stay unchanged. V2 uses a separate module and registry. The operator recipe
declares physical source custody, complete immutable input identities, raw content

The deterministic transformation hash pins every projected body and reference.
No regex or substring replacement modifies source content. Detectors only reject
undeclared author annotations; no-hit sources do not receive automatic clearance.
