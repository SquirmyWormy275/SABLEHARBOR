## Supported simulated conclusions

The existing `task.update` command permits attributed progress and conclusions.
Prior helpers deliberately preserved tasks while recording bounded observations;
that restriction applied to those executions, not to all subsequent work.

`COMPLETE` describes performance of the exact procedure. A fully performed procedure
may reach a supported `PASS`, `FAIL` or `LIMITATION`. Partly performed work should
show `IN_PROGRESS` and its actual limits. Wholly unperformed work remains `NOT_RUN`.
Absence of support does not establish `NOT_APPLICABLE`. A local successful operation
does not establish corporate population completeness, full-period effectiveness or
completion of a broader procedure. Record the exact remaining clauses rather than
keeping every task at “not started” or inventing a successful audit.

The conditional duties need individual applicability reasoning. Existing accepted
company facts control. Unknown operating facts may require a qualified limitation;
reserved canonical decisions, actual deployments, actual PHI processing and real
contractual commitments cannot be invented to remove one.

## Acceptance and publication

Routine authorized fictional operations, technical reviewer workflows and supported
simulated audit conclusions can proceed. Qualified realism/sufficiency/calibration
review and owner usability feedback remain distinct requirements where the queue
calls for them. Automated tests cannot supply those decisions. This distinction is
not a reason to leave other engineering or substantive work unfinished.

Keep private scenarios, inspection records, assessments, credentials and populated
exports in excluded private storage. Preparing a local acceptance packet does not
authorize publication, deployment, a real assurance opinion or writes to Atlas.
