## Judgment

Sable Harbor has a substantial company model and a strong evidence infrastructure. It already has seven business-line operating models, legal-entity books and consolidation, detailed industrial records, commercial exceptions, workforce and capacity mechanics, control occurrences, retained history, scoped exports, controlled publications, and source-bound facilities. The next work should complete and reconcile those records into one dated, inspectable company release.

I would not yet call the repository company-complete. The most material gaps are the current-period operating population, financial adjustments that have not reached the enterprise view, the workforce/payroll source population, unresolved capital and legal terms, company-specific regulatory obligations, and implemented information policies. The audit portal can organize and evaluate this evidence; its existence does not establish the missing underlying company facts.

The intended deliverable is a professionally inspectable **synthetic operating company**. Completion does not require purchasing real colocation, filing real tax elections, or obtaining real permits for fictional operations. It requires deliberately authored and accepted in-universe facts, credible source records, consistent dates and accounting, meaningful exceptions, and accurate synthetic/proposed/operated status. Actual external execution remains a separate boundary if SH is ever used for live business.

The final acceptance exercise should include:

1. A clean independent import and documented regeneration/reperformance for the declared release.
2. Forward and reverse traces for every material business line and the corporate functions above.
3. Normal and adverse cases: missing record, late approval, wrong scope, changed source, duplicate adjustment, unauthorized access, failed control and later retest.
4. Cross-functional checks: the same people, services, assets, dates, contracts and balances agree across operating evidence, accounts, controls and maps.
5. An exception register with owner, affected claim, impact, due/disposition and supporting evidence. A modeled control failure can be valid company evidence; an unexplained absent source cannot be silently called complete.
6. Public/private separation: public company records, auditor/candidate work, protected scenario truth and evaluator rules/results have separate authority and access. Hidden truth and NAILEX proprietary implementation stay outside public SH.
7. An accepted release receipt identifying exactly what passed, which source and artifacts were accepted, and which bounded extensions remain open.

This gives NAILEX a stable company to evaluate while its independent authority resolver, methodology, grading and broader engagement capabilities develop. It also gives a real professional a credible, inspectable corpus whose uncertainty and failures are part of the record.
