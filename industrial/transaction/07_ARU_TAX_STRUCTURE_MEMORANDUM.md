## Intended structure and real eligibility rules

The legal transaction is SHIH's purchase of 100% of ARU stock on January 7, 2026. A §338(h)(10) election generally treats an eligible target as making a deemed asset sale and liquidation for federal tax purposes. A qualified stock purchase requires at least 80% of voting power and value acquired by a corporation within the prescribed twelve-month period. The election is joint; every S target shareholder must participate. Form 8023 ordinarily is due on the fifteenth day of the ninth month after acquisition, yielding October 15, 2026 here. [IRS Form 8023 instructions, October 2023 revision](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i8023).

S eligibility requires a domestic eligible corporation, allowable shareholders, no more than 100 shareholders and one stock class. Corporate shareholders and nonresident alien shareholders are ineligible. [IRS S corporation requirements](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/s-corporations).

## BS&T subsidiary treatment

ARU's wholly owned domestic railroad is modeled as a valid QSub from January 1, 2008 until the acquisition-related termination. IRS explains that a valid QSub election makes the subsidiary's assets, liabilities and tax items those of the S parent for federal tax purposes; it does not require an actual corporate dissolution. QSub eligibility requires complete ownership by an eligible S parent. [IRS Form 8869 instructions, December 2020 revision](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i8869).

The case therefore includes the railroad's assets and liabilities in the old-ARU deemed-sale model while preserving BS&T's legal company, regulatory duties, workforce and separate operating books. This is an application of the cited QSub treatment to the explicitly assumed case facts, not a separate BS&T §338 election. The post-close corporate ownership/tax model must reflect termination of QSub status and continuity of the legal railroad. No deduction or asset-basis amount is established merely by calling a subsidiary a QSub.
