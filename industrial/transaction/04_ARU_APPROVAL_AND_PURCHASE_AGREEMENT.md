**Consideration.** Enterprise value is $62M. The final bridge uses the source schedules; fees, retained leases and post-close capital are not counted again as seller proceeds.

**Working capital.** The derived peg is $4.5M, measured as eligible receivables + inventory + operating prepayments less trade payables and ordinary operating accruals. Buyer submits the closing statement within 60 days; sellers have 30 days to object with line-level reasons; unresolved accounting disputes go to an independent accountant limited to disputed items. Adjustments are dollar-for-dollar.

**Representations and disclosure.** Sellers represent title to shares, capitalization, tax status, authority, financial records, identified contracts, labor arrangements, environmental matters, claims and disclosed assets. The information standard does not convert management forecasts into guaranteed results. Known defects stay in disclosure schedules and the operating model.

**Risk allocation.** General representations survive eighteen months, to July 7, 2027. The derived environmental survival period is five years, to January 7, 2031. The larger identified claim has a $0.6M closing accrual and a specific seller indemnity for covered adverse development above that amount. Recovery cannot exceed actual covered loss and cannot be duplicated across insurance, escrow and seller recovery.
