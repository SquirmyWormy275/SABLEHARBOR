# Industrial model decisions and reconciliations

**Document ID:** SH-IND-DEC-001 | **Version:** 1.0.0

**Effective date:** 2026-09-05 | **Available at:** 2026-09-05T23:59:59-07:00

This record explains selected model assumptions. Fictional corporate choices control company canon; arithmetic, external law and real geography control their own domains. A selected assumption is not independently verified historical evidence. Every derived schedule must reconcile to its operating activity, accounting treatment, ownership and funding source.

| Topic | Selected treatment | Consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Current mine geography | 42.22° N, 108.18° W in Sweetwater County; approximately 6,885 ft; NAD83 / UTM zone 12N | The old Carbon County, 6,420-ft and zone-13 maps remain superseded historical artifacts. New collars and maps use the new frame. |
| Legal ownership | SH LLC → SH Industrial Holdings Inc. → Pale Sun Inc. → Red Wash Mining LLC; ARU Inc. is an industrial-holdings sibling, owning BS&T Railway Company | The management layer and legal subsidiaries no longer conflict. Formation dates precede their transactions. |
| Acquisition timing | Red Wash closes July 18, 2025; ARU closes January 7, 2026; ordinary inbound service starts July 7, 2026 | No ARU mine revenue in 2025 and no full-year normalized interface revenue in 2026. |
| Rail network | Forty total route miles across the mainline and two branches | Yard/track assets and loaded-plus-empty traffic are separate measures; a mine spur is not included. |
| Mine interface demand | 205 recurring + 10 project cars; 10 additional conditional buffer slots | Normalized base billing uses 215 cars; unused buffer slots generate no invoice. |
| Service economics | Price and variable cost build from commodity handling, truck trips, equipment and labor | Earlier revenue/EBITDA targets are comparison points. The derived base is retained when lower. |
| Service reliability | Tuesday/Friday windows plus separately costed exception dispatches | A strict two-window-only plan cannot promise a 72-hour elapsed SLA for every arrival. |
| Capital | $3.25M mine interface + $5.25M ARU interface = $8.5M; ARU catch-up $11M separately | No double counting of the $15M screen, residual $6.5M, sustaining capital or acquisition price. |
| Cash and costs | Conservative integrated scenario adds interface invoices and separately paid origin linehaul; zero displaced external-cost savings claimed | Funding covers the resulting cash deficit. The model does not assume unproved savings. |
| Mine ARO | Preserve the original $16M/$1.04M standalone comparison; integrated opening 2026 liability accrues from July 18, 2025 | Accretion is noncash expense; actual modeled reclamation settlements reduce both cash and the liability. A liability-calibrated closure schedule is explicitly not an engineering estimate. |
| ARU seller bridge | $62M EV less $12.5M net debt = $49.5M seller economics; $1.5M preclose distribution and $48M purchase consideration | The $3M escrow is inside the $48M, and $2M cash remains in the business. |
| ARU funding | $13.5M refinanced debt, $2.5M retained finance leases, $22.5M new debt and $39M equity before fees | Acquisition fees, subsequent catch-up/interface capital and operating liquidity receive separate treatment. |
| Purchase accounting | Identifiable fair values, known reserve-related deferred tax and residual goodwill | Reserves and the tax election cannot be counted twice or mistaken for spendable acquisition financing. |
| Consolidation | Reciprocal ARU invoice/Red Wash expense and receivable/payable eliminate | External costs, debt, capital consumption and equity funding remain visible. |
| Publication | Exact artifact availability and explicit forecast/commitment classes | September month-end results cannot enter a September 5 case as completed facts. |

Direct uranium custody, a future mine spur, mine-expansion commissioning dates, actual regulatory evidence and independently surveyed access rights remain outside the selected operating authorization. These gates do not prevent the completed ordinary-inbound model or the reconstruction of the approved fictional acquisition case.
