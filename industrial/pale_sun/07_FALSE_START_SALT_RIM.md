Salt Rim Recovery Venture was a fictional proposal to restart a western uranium recovery operation through a seller/operating-partner arrangement. Dillon Severn, a fictional veteran uranium specialist, supplied its principal expert thesis. Names and details are synthetic. Neither the venture nor Severn is a disguised statement about a real company or individual.

## Why Vilander deferred — July 8, 2024

After two failures, Vilander recruited Severn to test whether he was missing ordinary industry practice. Severn had a persuasive résumé, knew counterparties and could explain almost every objection. Vilander initially treated that fluency as independent evidence. When another reviewer asked why a seller's output claim lacked a reconciled shipment ledger, Severn described the gap as normal legacy recordkeeping. Vilander accepted the explanation too readily.

The error was not hiring an expert. It was allowing an expert's confidence to determine which contradictory evidence deserved attention. Vilander began presenting the opportunity as supported by a network when several supposedly independent opinions originated with the same person.

The evidence does not establish deliberate dishonesty by Severn. His experience was real within the fictional case, and some technical observations remained useful. The disqualifying defect was the lack of independently supported operating conditions behind the proposed economics.

## Kill criteria and decision

The review required traceable product/production reconciliation, independent operator confirmation of the restart assumptions and an explanation of contrary evidence that could be tested. A strong résumé could not waive any of these requirements. Mari pressed Vilander to say which recommendation he could own if Severn left the room. His answer became narrower until it no longer supported a purchase.

On October 4 he killed the opportunity and recorded his own responsibility for delaying the challenge. The specialist's final note still argued that experienced operators could solve the issues. It was retained as dissent, not converted into proof or suppressed as embarrassment. No settlement, accusation of misconduct or undisclosed existential failure is invented.

## Rule carried into Red Wash

**Respect expertise. Never outsource judgment.** Henry Norwood's knowledge would be valuable without becoming controlling truth. Walt Sutter's recollection would generate a testable claim, not replace a resource model. Cole's stop authority would justify a pause without making every explanation he offered correct. Mari's competence would not remove Martin Shaw's need for an independent transaction price and liability bridge. Vilander's next task was to integrate these authorities without taking them over.
