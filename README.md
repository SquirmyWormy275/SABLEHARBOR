# SABLE HARBOR

<img src="assets/brand/logos/sable-harbor__reverse-horizontal.png" alt="Sable Harbor" width="360">

Sable Harbor is a fictional company whose records let you explore how a business works. Follow its people and decisions, examine contracts and accounts, or work through an operating problem. The archive includes company history, organization charts, financial models, controls and facility plans. Documents and workbooks can be read without running code.

## Read and download

Read Markdown on GitHub, open PDFs for formatted documents and download Excel workbooks for calculations. Complete packages are listed in the [download guide](docs/reader/USE_CASES.md#downloads-and-tools). Keep a package’s release, scenario and period together when working through its records.

To find any repository file, open **[All files](https://github.com/SquirmyWormy275/SABLEHARBOR/wiki/Files)**, choose a collection and select the file. Every original file is within three clicks of this README or the Wiki home page. The directory covers documents, data, maps, artwork, code and configuration.

Interactive maps and offline browsers run from a downloaded package or checkout. The audit workspace and CCF tools have their own setup guides. Document review needs no installation.

## Dates and status

The company and its scenarios are synthetic. Records may describe reconstructed history, a forecast, a proposed facility or an accepted company decision. Their dates and status explain which applies:

- **LOCKED:** accepted within the fictional company.
- **PROVISIONAL / OPEN:** working direction or a matter awaiting resolution.
- **SUPERSEDED:** an earlier record preserved as history.

The [source and format guide](docs/reader/SOURCES_AND_FORMATS.md) explains how to follow a record when documents disagree. [Records and decisions](docs/wiki/Records-and-Decisions.md) connects current facts with their history, and [decisions and remaining work](docs/wiki/Open-Questions.md) records accepted resolutions and the work beyond their scope. Released exercises retain their original sources and assumptions.
