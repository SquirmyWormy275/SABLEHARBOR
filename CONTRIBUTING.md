# Contributing

Changes must identify affected business lines, controlling sources, evidence states, generated artifacts, schema/release impact, public-safety impact, and unresolved facts.

Do not infer open canon. Update the relevant business-line dossier, central registry, audit matrix, and wiki source together. Use a branch and pull request; do not silently merge release candidates or generated artifacts.

Required validation depends on scope, but may include the enterprise portal, organization maps, brand system, domain tests, generated-artifact inspection, schema/migration checks, and manifest/checksum verification.
