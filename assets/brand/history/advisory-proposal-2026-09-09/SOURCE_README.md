# Sable Harbor Advisory logo and letterhead

## Files

- **Sable-Harbor-Advisory-Logo.png** — full-name horizontal logo with a transparent background, 2172 × 724 pixels. The exact generated image is embedded in both stationery files.
- **Sable-Harbor-Advisory-Letterhead.docx** — editable US Letter template. Replace the bracketed date, recipient, subject, letter and signature fields. The logo and practice footer repeat automatically as the letter grows.
- **Sable-Harbor-Advisory-Letterhead-Print.pdf** — blank US Letter stationery, ready for printing or compositing with correspondence. Print at actual size.

## Design

The full-name wordmark extends the existing Advisory bracket emblem. Graphite gives the identity its weight; steel blue identifies Advisory. The central square is the decision point linking two bodies of information. The wider spacing in ADVISORY gives the secondary line clarity at correspondence scale.

The intended palette follows the repository: Ink #101214, Advisory Steel Blue #456C98 and Muted Gray #747A80. Generated raster pixels are not a certified spot-color or vector master. Both stationery files use a white paper field and contain no invented legal suffix, office, address, phone number, email or website.

The footer uses the canonical practice names: Intelligence Advisory, Intelligence Capability and Operational Excellence.
