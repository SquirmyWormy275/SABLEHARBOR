# Advisory visual and letterhead proposal, September 9, 2026

**Status: proposed historical visual, never locked as production identity.**
The [current Advisory logo](../../logos/advisory__primary-horizontal.svg)
and [brand rules](../../README.md) remain controlling. This folder preserves
the original proposed logo, editable letterhead and two byte-distinct print
PDFs found in Downloads during the October 23 recovery.

The [original package README](SOURCE_README.md) says the work was developed
from repository commit `7988f47` on September 9. It explicitly calls the
visual a proposal and gives no approval to replace existing brand assets.
The package was observed on the workstation in October; observation time is
not a new design or approval date.

identical 1200-pixel Poppler rasterizations (zero changed pixels in a direct

and [recovery manifest](RECOVERY_MANIFEST.json) preserve the hashes and
provenance. The exact 950,630-byte original ZIP is indexed at the
[historical release](https://github.com/SquirmyWormy275/SABLEHARBOR/releases/tag/advisory-visual-proposal-historical-2026-09-09),
SHA-256 `bb1f144e842d7790736eb6f49dedd537681c48f521207268b8459584d407322f`.

historical design candidate, not evidence of approval, office occupation or
