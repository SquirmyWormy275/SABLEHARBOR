# J2 HEADQUARTERS DOCTRINE

**Document ID:** `SH-J2-HQ-001` | **Version:** 1.1.0 | **Effective:** September 3, 2026 | **State:** LOCKED
**Owner:** J2 Headquarters | **Related:** Contact, Judgment, Orientation, JAG, Education | **Cross-reference:** `J2_CHARTER.md`; `J2_OPERATING_MODEL.md`; `J2_ESTABLISHMENT.md`

J2 Headquarters exists so Contact, Judgment, Orientation, JAG, and Education remain one institution rather than disconnected expert teams. It is deliberately smaller than the operating arms, but it is not skeletal. Its legitimacy comes from stewardship, professional rigor, and unusually high-quality support rather than a large command tower.

HQ is custodian of J2 doctrine, professional standards, training standards, Daedalus doctrine, cross-loop priorities, collection-priority arbitration when capacity conflicts, profession/talent stewardship, and institutional continuity. It ensures handoffs preserve context, recurring learning crosses loops, and local optimization does not fracture the whole.

Orientation sets broad enterprise intelligence priorities; Judgment Officers own problem-specific requirements; Contact owns collection method. HQ intervenes only where requirements conflict, scarce collection capacity requires choice, doctrine is disputed, or enterprise continuity is at risk. It does not replace those accountabilities with centralized tasking.

The Head of J2 has administrative reporting to the CEO and protected access to the Board. The exact corporate title may evolve, but the protected access and institutional independence are part of the office, not a personal privilege.

## Headquarters establishment

The J2 Headquarters establishment is approximately **28 billets**. The headquarters staff includes:

- Head of J2
- Deputy Head of J2
- Chief of Staff
- a small executive office
- operations and cross-arm coordination
- people and professional services
- information and knowledge services
- security and access administration
- budget, procurement, and travel support
- workplace, hospitality, and administrative support

The purpose of this support is to remove friction from high-value professional work. J2 is expected to be one of the best-supported places to work inside Sable Harbor without allowing the support tail to become the institution's center of gravity.

## Employment proposition

J2 is intentionally a highly desirable professional home. Its advantages are substantive rather than ornamental: excellent tools and workspaces, high-quality administrative support, strong research support, travel and field access, serious professional education budgets, good food and hospitality during sustained work, sabbaticals, fellowships, study opportunities, and the time and support required to develop genuine expertise.

These advantages exist because Immaculate Intelligence requires people who can do unusually demanding intellectual and field work without wasting their energy on avoidable administrative friction. Perks do not purchase loyalty to management and may never be used to compromise professional independence.

## Establishment roll-up

The working J2 establishment is **237 billets**:

- Contact — 78
- Judgment — 42
- Orientation — 24
- Junction Advisory Group — 30
- Education — 35
- J2 Headquarters — 28

This establishment is a canonical design point rather than a requirement that every billet be continuously filled. Headcount may move modestly with enterprise scale, but material changes to the proportions or missions require an explicit canon decision rather than administrative drift.

## Independence and limits

HQ owns professional standards without turning J2 into a credential bureaucracy. It protects Judgment curiosity, Orientation independence, Contact provenance, JAG field truth, and Education quality. It also protects operating units from J2 mission creep: J2 does not command, punish, conduct personnel actions, run line functions, or silently become Internal Audit, Legal, Compliance, or Risk.

J2's privileged access exists to improve institutional contact with reality and decision quality. It does not create executive authority.
