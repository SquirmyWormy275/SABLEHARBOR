# Demotte Reclamation Services — name and visual identity

**Decision ID:** DEM-RS-001
**Date:** September 7, 2026
**State:** LOCKED owner-selected name and artwork update, effective on acceptance into main
**Scope:** Editorial replacement of Morrow Run Reclamation Services with Demotte Reclamation Services

## Owner direction and scope

After Quality Forest Communications' tree/radio artwork was selected and locked, the next proposed company was Morrow Run Reclamation Services. The owner directed a rename to **Demotte**, regeneration of the image and a repository update. When that direction was mistakenly applied to QFC in an image proposal, the owner expressly rejected the QFC change and reaffirmed that QFC was locked.

This decision applies **Demotte Reclamation Services**, shortened to **Demotte**, to the existing West Virginia reclamation company. It is an editorial replacement throughout the story, not an added in-universe renaming event. Quality Forest Communications retains its existing name and exact owner-selected yellow-Q artwork under [QFC-VIS-001](QUALITY_FOREST_COMMUNICATIONS_VISUAL_IDENTITY_2026-09-07.md). The rejected Demotte Communications/tree-D proposal is excluded from this repository's assets and inventory.

## Existing operating role

Demotte remains the fictional host for Cradle's first U.S. acid-mine-drainage recovery deployment in north-central West Virginia. It operates ordinary treatment for water from a legacy underground coal complex. Host treatment, discharge compliance, immediate stop/bypass authority and remediation liabilities remain with the host. Cradle retains its bounded recovery module and recovery right. The name update changes no ownership, liability, commercial term, deployment geography or financial input.

The [Cradle closeout](CRADLE_CLOSEOUT_2026-09-06.md), [structured record](../structured/cradle_closeout_2026-09-06.json) and [organization map](../organization/PROJECT_CRADLE.md) carry the current name. Earlier validation text remains dated historical evidence, with a linked naming addendum. This decision supersedes the prior company name only.

## Regenerated artwork

The owner-directed logo uses a traditional industrial roundel with an Appalachian ridge, strata and winding stream, alongside **DEMOTTE / RECLAMATION SERVICES** in substantial industrial lettering. The owner subsequently requested blue and white with a more retro 1980s mining-company character. The deposited revision uses blue and white, a simplified geometric emblem, heavy slanted industrial lettering and horizontal rules.

The [horizontal PNG](../../assets/brand/logos/demotte-reclamation-services__primary-horizontal.png) is **2079 × 756 pixels**, RGB, **881,116 bytes**. SHA-256: `cb5bdbc6cb950dac198312222eb47659547ae6a30994c90dff514b0707cfd15f`. The [visual manifest](../../assets/brand/demotte_reclamation_visual_manifest.json) records its exact source and the owner's instruction to regenerate and update the repository. After viewing this blue-and-white revision, the owner explicitly instructed: **“nice. lock it and send it to the repo.”** This exact revision is the selected controlling artwork.

Preserve the full source PNG and its white background. This is the sole deposited production form; no vector master or alternate layout is supplied. Its specific ridge/stream motif and PNG source override the general brand-system motif and SVG-first rules for this identity. Demotte is an external host operator, not an additional Sable Harbor business line.
