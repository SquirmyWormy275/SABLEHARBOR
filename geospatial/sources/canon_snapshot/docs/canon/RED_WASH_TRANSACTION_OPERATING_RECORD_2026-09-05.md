## 1. Authority and evidence doctrine

This record selects the controlling Red Wash transaction and 2026 operating case in
the fictional Sable Harbor universe. It does not claim that Red Wash, its counterparties,
permits, employees, shipments, or financial results exist in the real world.

Four independent labels must not be collapsed:

- canon state (`LOCKED`, `PROVISIONAL`, `OPEN`, `SUPERSEDED`);
- record origin (`PUBLIC_SYNTHETIC_DIEGETIC` or `EXTERNAL_REALITY_ANCHOR`);
- evidence/model state (`DERIVED`, `SUPPORTED_ESTIMATE`, `PROVISIONAL_ASSUMPTION`,
  `SCENARIO`, `OPEN`, `CONFLICT`, or `SUPERSEDED`); and
- period role (`SYNTHETIC_CALIBRATION` through August 2026 or
  `MANAGEMENT_FORECAST` for September–December 2026).

There is no `ACTUAL` layer in this package. Locking a fictional case decision does not
turn a generated row into a historical actual. Real-world sources constrain the
synthetic model; they do not authenticate synthetic company facts.

Production cost and DD&A are incurred measures, not automatic period expense. The
released package applies a transparent weighted-average inventory roll-forward to
opening inventory, 2026 production, cost of sales, and ending inventory. The currently
selected supporting assumptions are $45.00/lb opening cash cost, $5.50/lb opening
DD&A, an 18% modeled income-tax rate, and $0.550M other working-capital use. These are
`SUPPORTED_ESTIMATE` scenario inputs, not additional locked historical facts.

The generated statement package and cash-flow bridge are subordinate `DERIVED`
outputs. If they do not reconcile to the operating drivers, the generator must fail
rather than force the selected narrative. This release does not claim a separate
trial-balance dataset.

The selected DD&A bridge is a `MODEL_PROPOSED` method supported as a

units-of-production factor; and $50.0M multiplied by 7.0% produces $3.500M of 2026
DD&A incurred. This simplified composite calculation is not an asset-class
purchase-price allocation, an audited GAAP useful-life determination, or tax basis.
