# Cradle geographic supersession — implemented September 7

The accepted [Cradle closeout](../../docs/canon/CRADLE_CLOSEOUT_2026-09-06.md) supersedes Belle/Kanawha with Bedford near Fairmont. The active search envelope, object register and atlas now use Bedford and its 15–20 acre target. The former envelope survives in `historical_sites` as `SUPERSEDED_EDITORIAL_SITING`. It does not imply historical Cradle occupancy or an in-world move.

Kelly Gang Mining in Tasmania and Demotte in north-central West Virginia are external field hosts, geographically and institutionally separate from Bedford. Exact parcels and access remain implementation work. Cradle business canon is closed.
