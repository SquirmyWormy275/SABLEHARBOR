# Site-selection evidence and current limits

Current regional business constraints are recorded in the accepted canon and the Geo object register. Bedford is near Fairmont, with a 15–20 acre target; the active envelope is a much larger analyst search window. The Fort uses the locked 10–20 acre site concept and named component functions. Neither window is an exact parcel.

Fairmont reference rail, highways, connecting/secondary roads and hydrography were retrieved from USGS federal GIS services on September 7; exact requests, feature counts, hashes and rights are in `sources/reference_manifest.json`. These references do not grant access, identify vacant industrial land, or determine floodplain/contamination status.

Sacramento and Hazelwood federal references remain preserved. Belle/Kanawha reference snapshots and original site-reference images remain historical research; they are not Bedford evidence. No Belle barge requirement transfers to Bedford by implication.

Before precise campus siting: verify occupied land, current planning, flood/drainage context, brownfield constraints, transport/utility access and fictional property boundaries. Existing industrial facility/road geometry is accepted synthetic implementation and retains its explicit real-world limitations.
