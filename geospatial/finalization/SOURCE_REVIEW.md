# Source interpretation and geographic crosswalk

This is a fixed review of the 6,896 discovery carriers left after the five previous batches. Exact extraction was checked against 471 archived source files at `d91a22c35c91b213204411421de88815f27d8e16`, including original locators and SHA-256 hashes. Whitespace-normalized grouping produced 4,956 wordings. Their dispositions are stored in `GROUP_DECISIONS.json.gz`; the original wording, source locator and surrounding units remain in `EXTRACTED_RESIDUAL.json.gz`. The verifier rejects missing carriers, changed wording, duplicate assignments, unknown object IDs and changed source bytes. It does not automatically approve later text.

The review covered narrative claims, their institutional/financial context, older proposals, source pointers, identity material and generated publication text. Of 458 grouped publication wordings, 291 have exact alphanumeric-normalized counterparts in retained Markdown/JSON/text/CSV source units; those are explicitly marked text counterparts rather than independent corroboration. The other 167 were inspected separately: 135 workbook-preview headings, publication metadata, organization labels, operating-boundary text and source links. SVG identity coordinates were assessed as logo artwork through title/description and source role; they are not geographic coordinate observations. This is not a claim to have manually read every PDF page or audited financial, legal or operating assertions.

## Interpretation rules

Related object IDs identify a subject, not a new geographic fact. A source can mention several subjects without establishing their co-location, ownership, tenure or agreement. Full source claims remain visible, including superseded assertions and numerical models.

| Rule | Current interpretation |
|---|---|
| EDITORIAL_KLEIN_SUCCESSOR | KLEIN-001 replaces Evalon editorially throughout the story. The old wording is retained as source history, not a second outpost or an in-world rename. The 2022 recharter is distinct from the accepted 2024 move. |
| BEDFORD_SUPERSEDES_BELLE | September 6 Cradle canon places Bedford in the Fairmont area. Belle/Kanawha alternatives remain historical reference, not current Bedford geography. |
| DEMOTTE_HOST_NAME | The current U.S. host is Demotte Reclamation Services, replacing Morrow Run editorially. DeMotte, Indiana is a separate biography reference. Kelly Gang Mining replaces the former Gunns placeholder for the Tasmanian host; neither host becomes owned by Cradle. |
| CURRENT_WYOMING_GEOGRAPHY | The accepted industrial successor controls the Sweetwater County Red Wash anchor, Taylor hub and Wamsutter connection. Carbon County/old coordinates and Bloodstone-as-town remain superseded. Bloodstone survives in predecessor company/mine history. Utah/Cane Creek/Ballard & Thompson are discarded railway origins. The 1907/1916 detailed casualty claims remain disputed; the current source-secure history does not promote them. |
| SOURCE_PERIOD_NOT_TENURE | Incorporation, acquisition, program activity, visits, incidents and observations are distinct from property occupancy. Current geometry is not projected into the unlocated 1898/1954 network. A current observation does not invent an opening day. |
| EXTERNAL_EXECUTION_NOT_ESTABLISHED | Regulatory frameworks, synthetic permits, provider selections, insurance assumptions, tax models and documents do not establish actual outside execution or release the uranium-custody gate. |
| CONDITIONAL_MODEL_NOT_ACTUAL | Forecast capital, capacity, staffing, 2027–2031 schedules and financial allocations retain their source assumptions. A conditional outside rail provider uses the existing network; Rawlins remains truck-served and gains no inferred BS&T branch. The old 126-person mine and other legacy enterprise calibrations do not replace the selected industrial census. |
| NONPROPERTY_REFERENCE | Retain biographies, rejected prospects, historical customer incidents and real reference institutions without creating company tenure. Exact addresses are unknown or not applicable. |
| AUTHORITY_DOES_NOT_CONVEY_PROPERTY | J2/ESS independence, operating authority, share ownership, host treatment responsibilities and financial allocation remain separate from site title. Source legal-name variants do not create additional physical facilities. |

A carrier with no locating content is retained with its full context. Words such as *state*, *field*, *branch*, *capital*, *project* and *office* frequently describe workflow, Git, accounting, authority or a profession. A heading or link is a navigation aid. Neither becomes a missing parcel simply because the discovery expression matched it.
