# Klein shop and Fort continuity — proposed decision

**State: PROPOSED — awaiting owner acceptance.** This is a concrete fictional-history proposal, not recovered evidence. It is excluded from the accepted chronology, map layers, occupancy intervals and issue-closure claims. Its acceptance would need a dated controlling canon decision and a separately validated register update.

## Evidence already established

The [2021 shop section](../../docs/canon/SABLE_HARBOR_CORPORATE_LORE_CANON_v0.3.1.md#74-the-klein-shop--2021) expressly describes Klein leasing a light-industrial building outside Pittsburgh. The same narrative places Willow's institutional recharter in 2022; that decision does not say the team moved buildings. The [Fort closeout](../../docs/canon/WILLOW_KLEIN_CLOSEOUT_2026-09-06.md) establishes a later 10–20 acre working compound, three principal sheds and the Museum yard. It explicitly preserves old Klein artifacts but does not establish whether those artifacts stayed on the same parcel or were moved.

## Recommended fictional history

Adopt **separate premises with a staged move in 2024**:

1. The original Klein shop is the leased light-industrial building documented in 2021. The surviving team continues using that shop after the 2022 Willow recharter; the institutional change does not itself cause a relocation or a property transaction.
2. During 2024, Willow moves its principal Pittsburgh research operation to a separate industrial compound that becomes known as the Fort. The move occurs in stages so equipment can be inventoried, transported and recommissioned without treating every rig as continuously available. The old shop is vacated by the end of that calendar year.
3. The retained Klein sign, marked equipment and selected retired prototypes travel with the team. Those objects explain physical continuity and the Museum's accumulated history without implying that the original shop and the Fort share a parcel. The Fort's Big Shed, Small Shed, White Shed and Museum retain their accepted functions. Exact building commissioning dates are not created by the move year.

**The 2024 move and continued 2022–2024 use are new proposed facts. They are not found in the accepted sources.** Year precision is deliberate: this decision would establish a bounded relocation year, not fictitious exact lease commencement/expiration days.
