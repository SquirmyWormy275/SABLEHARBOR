# Atlas visual QA — v0.1.0-rc4

All eleven atlas pages were rendered and inspected at the final implementation checkpoint. The review covers the enterprise framework and census, Red Wash regional/site views, Sacramento, the Fort, Bedford, Wamsutter, open questions and the two BS&T views. Corporate label separation and Wamsutter sidebar/legend spacing were corrected and those pages re-rendered. Text bounding boxes remain on-page.

The maps show candidate A, Bedford/Fairmont, the Fort/Klein lineage, distinct Cradle hosts and the accepted industrial case. Early railway geometry and exact campuses remain explicitly qualified. Visual inspection is separate from native QGIS project validation.

Current map source and byte hashes are in MAP_MANIFEST.json. The PDF/PNG/SVG previews are intentionally retained as reproducible review evidence and controlled map products.
