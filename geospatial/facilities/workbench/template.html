Attendance and capacity

Workers are scenario populations, not payroll. Assigned desks remain reserved regardless of attendance. Shared desks must meet both sharing-ratio and simultaneous-attendance requirements. Baseline seat capacities remain fixed. Unknown capacity stays unknown.
