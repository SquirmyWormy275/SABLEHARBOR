# R01 first successor master — passed

The fifth candidate for SH-MAP-SAC-002 passes the first-successor visual gate. The exact PNG, SVG and PDF are retained as `R01_FIRST_CANDIDATE.*`; hashes, build inputs, corrected findings and intentional differences are in [R01_FIRST_GATE_REVIEW.json](R01_FIRST_GATE_REVIEW.json). The original failed review remains available at commit `51d92e1`.

All four approved originals were reviewed at their original 3240 × 2304 resolution, then compared with the final candidate in four saved side-by-side images. The master was also reviewed in a 50% overlay. The candidate PNG and independently rasterized PDF were inspected at 3240 × 2304. The SVG is the editable vector source for the reviewed drawing.

Corrections include bundled matching DejaVu fonts, restored subtitle baseline and exact palette, building accent strips, split south parking and clear arrival, restored road and walking connections, darker program text, and restored Education service geometry. Education’s public entrance is 44 ft east of its west wall (campus x512 ft); the x504–520 ft public path meets the bridge ending at x522 ft. Service access aligns separately with its apron.

Intentional derivative differences are R02 markers and continuity prose, a subordinate stable-ID caption, small type-tracking/label-position/wrapping differences, simplified tree circles and regularized planting, and south parking segmentation preserving 252 total bays. Original reference bytes are unchanged. The source package remains conceptual, with no actual parcel or construction status implied.

The four buildings, ten floors, 175,392 gross sq ft, 362 workplaces and 60 residential rooms preserve the approved baseline. Workplaces and rooms remain separate from people, learning and meeting capacity.

This gate permits subsequent sheet generation only. Every subsequent floor and atlas surface still requires its own numerical and visual QA; the earlier 63-sheet review does not apply to R02.
