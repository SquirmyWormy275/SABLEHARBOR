V02 content, a different building treatment

V02 is the preferred baseline. V04 retains its wording and cool neutral palette. The main change is from double-bordered rectangles to restrained dimensional building masses, using the saved floor counts and floor heights. Entrances and paths remain on the same ground coordinates.

The roof offset is an oblique drawing convention; it does not move a building or assert facade details. Ground-plane scale applies to the site layout.

Real maps reviewed

  • Salk Institute entrance and parking map — simple dimensional masses and direct reception guidance.
  • Getty Center Map and Highlights — architecture visually distinct from directory labels.
  • Stanford self-guided tour map — route emphasis and plan symbols.
  • Kati Lacey’s Wolfson College map — reviewed as an expressive comparator; its cartoon illustration style was not adopted.

These are design references, not Sable Harbor canon. Third-party artwork is linked at its original publisher, not copied into this repository.
